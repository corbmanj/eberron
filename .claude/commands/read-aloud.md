# Read Aloud Text Generator

When this command is invoked, write one or more Read Aloud text blocks for a D&D 5e session set in a homebrew Eberron campaign. Apply the following rules to every block, without exception.

## Rules

**Be visually rich and specific.**
Give players enough concrete detail to picture exactly where they are. Describe the size of the space, what's on the walls, what's on the floor, what the light looks like, what objects are present. A player who closes their eyes should be able to draw a rough map of the room from the description. Richness comes from specific, accurate detail — not from elaborate prose.

**Stay grounded and conversational.**
Write the way a person talks, not the way a novelist writes. No flourishes. No literary devices for their own sake. The DM should be able to read this aloud at the table and have it land naturally.

**Use concrete sensory details only.**
What do they see? What do they hear? What does the air smell or feel like? Describe actual physical things. Do not describe feelings, moods, or impressions — describe the things that produce those feelings and let the players draw their own conclusions.

**No metaphors that don't make physical sense.**
Every comparison must be something a character could actually perceive. "The walls are the color of old bone" is fine. "The silence presses in on you like a living thing" is not — silence doesn't press.

**Short, direct sentences. Present tense.**
One idea per sentence. No subordinate clauses stacked on each other. No passive voice if you can avoid it.

**Never tell the players how to feel.**
Do not write "unsettling," "eerie," "beautiful," "foreboding," or any other emotional label. Describe what produces the feeling. Let the players feel it themselves.

## What NOT to do

❌ "Sound behaves wrong — your footsteps are quieter than they should be, but somehow more present."
❌ "The oppressive darkness seems to breathe with malevolent intent."
❌ "A chill runs down your spine as you behold the ancient horror before you."

## What TO do

✅ "The room is thirty feet across. The ceiling is domed, maybe twenty feet up. The walls are stone, carved floor to ceiling with glyphs that are still lit — faintly, unsteady. In the center: a stone basin on a raised plinth. The light coming from it is dim and the wrong color."

## How to use this command

Invoke with a brief description of the scene:

`/read-aloud the party enters the sanctum for the first time — a circular chamber with a glowing basin, dark veins in the stone, and a floating deathless woman near the far wall`

Write the Read Aloud block, then stop. Do not add DM notes, mechanics, or commentary unless asked.
