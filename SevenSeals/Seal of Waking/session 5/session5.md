# Session 5: The Light That Holds

## Seal of Waking — Act V: Irian

*A session for 4 level-9 characters. Estimated runtime: 3–3.5 hours.*

---

## Session Overview

The party enters the sanctum, resolves Celaen, and walks out of the vault. They report to Varaen, collect what they're owed, and ride out at dawn with an elven escort to Shae Mordai. They pass through the City of the Dead, cross into the Irian manifest zone, and find the plane of Eternal Dawn looking like something is wrong with it. The Seal of Waking is here. So is whatever the disruption has been breaking.

> **DM Note — Session 4 Recap:** The party cleared Room 8 last session. The three Vowbound Cultists are dead. The party has the journal, the sending stone (heard the loop message), and the insulating cloth. Aerin scouted the sanctum as a spider and reported back. Session 5 picks up at the sanctum door.

## Opening

The party is standing in Room 8. The cultists are dead. The sending stone has gone quiet. The sanctum door is in front of them — thick stone, no handle, the incomplete Severed Word ward carved around the frame (they were trying to seal it from the outside; they didn't finish).

Aerin has reported what she saw through the crack: a 40-foot circular room, a basin pulsing with dim wrong-colored light, dark veins spreading across the floor, and Celaen floating near the far wall, drifting and talking to herself.

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> The door to the sanctum is right there. No handle. No mechanism. The carving the cultists were working on runs around the entire frame — dense, tight glyphs pressed into the stone in a continuous band. They didn't finish it. Whatever they were trying to do, the ward is incomplete.
>
> Through the crack in the door, you can still hear her.
>
> *"It won't stop. I can hear it. It keeps speaking."*

The door pushes inward when pressed. No trap. No lock. The cultists sealed themselves inside from this side and the ward never completed.

---

## Room 9: The Sanctum

### Celaen ir'Talorath — Resolution

The door to Room 9 opens inward. When they enter:

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> The basin is in the center of the room on a raised stone plinth — low, broad, filled with faintly glowing water. It should be warm light. It isn't. The light swells and dims unevenly — long and slow, then too fast, then halting — like something struggling to breathe. Dark veins run out from the base across the floor.
>
> There's a black crystal wedged into a crack at the basin's rim. Eight inches long, sharp-edged. Every time the light swells, the crystal swells with it. Every time it dims, so does the crystal. They're locked together.
>
> She's near the far wall, floating. She hasn't moved much since Aerin saw her. She sees you come in.
>
> "I told them to stop. They wouldn't stop." She's looking at the basin, not at you. "It keeps speaking. Every hour. Every minute. I can hear it and I can't — I can't think around it."
>
> Her eyes come back to you. "Is it still there? That... thing. Can you see it? It hurts me to get near it."

She knows the Shard is there. She can't get near the basin — the passive damage from the Shard has been burning her each time she tries. She can't remove it herself. That's why she needed someone.

---

### The Shard of Severed Dawn

*AC 12 | HP 20 | Vulnerable: Radiant*

Wedged into a crack at the rim of the basin. Touching the basin or the Shard without the insulating cloth: 7 (2d6) necrotic damage.

**To destroy:**
- Attack it (wrap the insulating cloth around your hand first, or take the damage)
- Cast *Dispel Magic* at 3rd level (DC 14 Arcana) or 5th level (automatic)
- Use the *Shatter* scroll — it deals double damage to the Shard

**When destroyed:**

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> The crystal cracks. The crack splits. Then it comes apart, and the burst hits you.
>
> Then it's over.
>
> The basin flares — not the sick pulse from before. A real flare, full and warm, spreading across the floor and up the walls. The dark veins in the stone are still there, but they're not moving anymore. Going cold.
>
> The air changes. Warmer. That feeling from the forest — the one that made you feel more awake — it's back, stronger, in here.

Every creature in the sanctum: **DC 14 CON save or 21 (6d6) necrotic damage, half on success** (the shard's death burst).

After that, the vault's positive energy regeneration is restored: **1 HP at the start of each turn** while in the sanctum.

---

### Celaen — After the Shard Is Destroyed

She goes still. The erratic floating stops. She stands in the air with her hands over her face.

After a moment:

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> She lowers her hands. Her light has steadied — warm, not cold.
>
> "Three weeks," she says. "I knew something was wrong. I could feel it pulling at the basin, at me, and I couldn't think around it. It was like trying to speak with someone shouting in your ear."
>
> She looks at the shards of the crystal on the floor.
>
> "Who sent them?"

She listens without interrupting. She doesn't ask clarifying questions — she takes it in.

**If they mention Irian:**

> "Yes. The dawn stuttered. That's the correct word. I've felt that pulse six times in eleven centuries, and each time it meant something had gone wrong in the world above. But what they put here — this wasn't the cause. This was someone taking advantage of a wound."
>
> Pause.
>
> "Whatever is wrong in Irian, the cult didn't make it. They found it and made it worse."

**If they mention Varaen:**

> "Tell him I'm stable. Tell him I'm sorry for the patrol."
>
> She doesn't elaborate on the patrol. Move on.

**If they mention the sending stone:**

> She looks at it for a long moment.
>
> "Keep it. Varaen will want to know what that voice said."

---

### Option: Freeing Celaen Entirely

If the party offers to destroy the basin — releasing Celaen from her vigil entirely — she will consider it.

> "Eleven centuries." She looks at the basin. Not the Shard. The basin.
>
> "I knew I would have to decide this eventually."
>
> A moment.
>
> "Yes. Do it."

**Destroying the basin:** AC 14, HP 60 combined (basin and remaining Shard fragments). When it goes, the burst is the same. Celaen's light spreads outward as she releases — warm, sustained for a moment, then she is simply gone.

No dramatic death. No final speech. She just steps out.

> **DM Note:** If they free Celaen this way, Varaen's response is more complicated (see Beat 4). The deal still holds — Celaen's release was her choice — but it costs him something, and he won't pretend otherwise.

---

## Beat 3: Vault Exit

They leave the way they came in. The vault's light has stabilized behind them — the glyphs on the walls are steady now, all of them, even the ones near the entrance that had been flickering.

Raeven is at the archway.

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> She's been there the whole time, sitting on a rock off to one side, elbows on her knees. She stands when she sees you.
>
> She looks at the archway. Waits.
>
> "And her?"

*If Celaen is stable:* Raeven's jaw unclenches. "Good." She turns and starts walking back toward the waystation. "It's almost dark. Come."

*If Celaen is at rest (basin destroyed):* She closes her eyes for a moment. When she opens them she says, "Was it her choice?" If yes: "Then it's done." She turns and walks. If no — if the party destroyed the basin without Celaen's consent — Raeven says nothing, but the escort for the rest of the session is colder.

---

**At the waystation:** The party sleeps here. They're in a manifest zone — the positive energy regeneration applies to rest. They take a long rest.

> **DM Note:** The vault has been tough. Let them rest. Session 5 still has Shae Mordai and Irian ahead. Full recovery is appropriate.

---

## Beat 4: Return to Varaen

Morning. Varaen is already at the carved stone table when they enter. He reads their faces before anyone speaks.

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> "Tell me."

He listens to everything. When they finish, he is quiet. Then:

> "The Cult of the Severed Word. We've seen their markings on the coast for two years. We didn't know what they were working toward."
>
> He sets both hands flat on the table.
>
> "You did what I asked. More than I asked."

He places the ir'Talorath token on the table.

> "Shae Mordai. Eastern gate. Present this. You'll be escorted to the manifest zone." He pauses. "Inside the zone there is a tower — the Spire of Lasting Light. It was built before the Undying Court, before the city. It housed something important once. Something that is still there."
>
> He doesn't say what. He meets them directly. "You will not be left alone in the city. You will be, in the zone. You leave when your business is finished. Do you agree to these terms?"

If they agree:

> "Raeven leaves with you at midday. Tonight you will be inside the city."

---

**If they hand over the journal:** He reads it slowly, start to finish. Sets it down. When he speaks again the formality is still there, but something behind it has shifted. "I will want to speak with you when you return from the zone. There are names in this I recognize."

**If they hand over the sending stone and describe the voice:** He listens carefully. "You spoke to them directly." Not a question. A long pause. "What did the voice sound like?" Whatever the party describes, he files it away. "This changes our posture on the coast. I will need to convene the court."

**If Celaen was freed:** He is quiet for longer than usual. "She chose this?" If yes: "Then it was time." He doesn't say anything else, but before they leave, he pauses. "She asked for you to come. She trusted strangers with her house. That matters." He means it.

**If they took something from the vault:** He knows. Same resolution as established — return it and the deal holds.

---

### Supplies for the Manifest Zone

Before they leave, Varaen has someone bring a small case to the table. Inside: glass vials packed in cloth, and a sealed scroll.

> "The zone is not hostile on its own. But something in it has been wrong for months. These are precautions."

**What he offers depends on how the vault went:**

| Outcome | Potions Given | Scroll Given |
|---------|--------------|--------------|
| Vault cleared, Shard destroyed, no vault items taken | 2 Potions of Radiant Resistance | Scroll of *Protection from Energy* |
| Vault cleared, minor complications (item taken and returned, basin destroyed without Celaen's consent) | 1 Potion of Radiant Resistance | Scroll of *Protection from Energy* |
| Vault cleared but party handled things roughly or took items and kept them | 1 Potion of Radiant Resistance | — |

**Potion of Radiant Resistance** — resistance to radiant damage for 1 hour. No concentration.

**Scroll of *Protection from Energy*** (3rd level) — resistance to one damage type for 1 hour. Concentration. The caster must choose the damage type when the scroll is read — radiant is the obvious choice given where they're going.

> **DM Note:** Varaen doesn't explain what specifically to expect inside the zone — he doesn't know about the Broken Dawnwardens. He just knows the zone has been behaving strangely and that the ir'Talorath house uses these when working near it. The party may ask what he's preparing them for; he'll say honestly that he doesn't know, only that the light in the zone has been unstable.

## Beat 5: Shae Mordai

*Midday departure. The escort is Raeven and two others. The road to Shae Mordai takes most of the afternoon.*

The city is in a valley, visible from the tree line. As they approach:

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> You see it before you reach it — white stone through the trees, catching the afternoon light. The city is old. Every surface you can see from here is covered in carving: glyphs, reliefs, names. The streets are quiet, but not empty.
>
> The eastern gate is a single thick archway with no door. Two guards in formal armor stand at the sides. Raeven presents the token. They step aside without speaking.
>
> Inside: the city is the same as outside — pale stone, ancestor glyphs everywhere — but the scale is different. The buildings are larger than they need to be, and older than anything you've seen on this island. Some of the carvings on the walls are worn nearly smooth by centuries of weather.
>
> And there are the Deathless.

Describe a few of them as the party moves through the city. They're not performing. They're not dramatic. They're just here, going about whatever the dead go about.

**The Deathless in Shae Mordai:**

> - An old woman seated on a stone bench, eyes half-closed, a child kneeling beside her showing her something in a book. She looks at it. Nods.
> - A man in armor, clearly ancient, standing at the base of a building with his arms crossed, watching a work crew re-point the stone between the carvings. He gives them a direction. One of the workers adjusts.
> - One who notices the party. Just looks at them, calm, for a moment while they pass. Makes no move toward them. Looks away.

> **DM Note:** The city is not sinister. The Deathless are not threatening. This is the point — this is a culture built around death being a transition, not an end. Let it be strange and beautiful, not dangerous. The party is guests.

**If Finella gets looks:** She will. She might get one or two cold glances from living elves on the street — not hostile, just noticeable. Raeven doesn't acknowledge it. The Deathless don't care.

The escort takes them through the city to a walled garden near the center.

---

## Beat 6: The Manifest Zone Threshold

The garden is enclosed by a low stone wall, chest height. The trees inside are enormous — older than the buildings around them. The path through the gate leads to a clearing at the center, and at the center of the clearing: the air is different.

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> You can see where the zone begins. The light in the clearing is slightly different — warmer, and it doesn't come from the sun. The shadows are softer. The grass is more vivid than the grass outside the wall.
>
> Raeven stops at the garden gate.
>
> "We go no further. The zone is thirty feet ahead, more or less where the trees thin out." She hands you the ir'Talorath token. "In case anyone asks."
>
> She looks at the clearing. "There's no road in. You walk in and you'll feel the shift. There's no danger at the crossing — not usually." A beat. "Come back out when your business is finished. I'll be here."

She steps back. The gate is open. Thirty feet to the threshold.

> **DM Note:** "Not usually" is doing a lot of work. The party may ask what she means. She doesn't elaborate — she doesn't know that the Broken Dawnwardens are in there, and even she wouldn't expect that. She just knows the zone well enough to hedge.

## Beat 7: Irian — The Reveal

They step through.

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> The crossing isn't dramatic. You take a step and the air is different.
>
> It's warmer. The kind of warm that's comfortable without being hot. The smell changes — something clean, like the air right after rain.
>
> The light is still coming from the sun, technically — you can see it, low on the horizon, at the angle it always is in a place like this — but it's also coming from everywhere. The shadows here are soft. The grass is very green. There are flowers you can't name that are growing in colors you haven't seen look that vivid.
>
> Your whole body feels slightly more awake. Not energized, not electric — just *on*. Like you've been slightly tired for so long you forgot what not-tired feels like.
>
> In the distance, maybe half a mile, there's a tower. Tall — eight stories at least, maybe more. From here you can see it was white once, the same pale stone as Shae Mordai. Some of it still is. But it's wrong now. The upper floors are dark, the stone stained in a way that doesn't match anything around it. The silhouette is slightly off, like sections of it have shifted from where they were originally built.
>
> The sky above the tower is where the light keeps failing. Not like a cloud — more like a candle in a draft. In a place where the light comes from everywhere, a patch of sky that keeps almost going out, directly above that tower.
>
> The path leads toward it.

HP regeneration applies immediately: **1 HP at the start of each turn**, without limit, for the entire time they are in Irian (until they leave the manifest zone or until the Broken Dawnwardens appear — their instability locally suppresses it within 60 ft of them, in the same way the Shard suppressed it in the vault).

## Beat 8: The Broken Dawnwardens

As the party approaches the structure on the hill, the flickering in the sky gets worse. When they're about 200 ft from the structure:

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> Three shapes come out of the flickering light.
>
> They're made of it — the light — or they were. They float, each one roughly humanoid in shape, and they glow. But the glow isn't steady. It pulses like the Shard did: wrong rhythm, wrong color, a warm light doing something cold.
>
> They're supposed to be beautiful, you think. Whatever they were is still visible in the shape of them. Whatever happened to them is visible too.
>
> They don't seem to be looking at anything. One of them turns toward you, slowly.
>
> Then all three turn toward you.

**3 Broken Dawnwardens.** Roll initiative.

---

### Broken Dawnwarden

*Medium Construct, unaligned (fractured)*

*Dawnwardens were luminous constructs of positive energy — guardians native to Irian that maintained its light and turned away things that didn't belong. The disruption to the plane's anchor broke them. They no longer recognize friend from threat. They emit and blast indiscriminately.*

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| **AC** | 14 (natural armor) |
| **HP** | 68 |
| **Speed** | 0 ft, Fly 40 ft (hover) |
| **STR** | 10 (+0) |
| **DEX** | 14 (+2) |
| **CON** | 15 (+2) |
| **INT** | 3 (−4) |
| **WIS** | 8 (−1) |
| **CHA** | 6 (−2) |

**Damage Immunities:** Radiant, Poison, Psychic

**Condition Immunities:** Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Paralyzed, Poisoned

**Senses:** Darkvision 120 ft, passive Perception 9

---

**Traits:**

**Unstable Radiance (Passive).** A creature that starts its turn within 10 ft of the Dawnwarden takes 3 (1d6) radiant damage. This used to be healing. It isn't anymore.

**Suppressed Zone.** While a Broken Dawnwarden is alive and within 60 ft of a creature, that creature does not benefit from Irian's passive regeneration.

**Fractured Burst.** When the Dawnwarden is reduced to 0 HP, it releases a pulse of unstable light. Each creature within 15 ft: DC 13 DEX save or 10 (3d6) radiant damage, half on success.

---

**Actions:**

**Searing Pulse.** *Ranged Spell Attack:* +5 to hit, range 60 ft. *Hit:* 13 (3d8) radiant damage.

**Cascading Flash (Recharge 5–6).** Each creature within 30 ft of the Dawnwarden: DC 13 DEX save or 14 (4d6) radiant damage, half on success.

---

**Tactics:**
- They don't coordinate. They have no tactics. They attack whatever is moving.
- They use Cascading Flash as soon as it's available — they're not intelligent enough to hold it.
- They don't distinguish between each other. If two Dawnwardens are adjacent, they will hit each other with Unstable Radiance and potentially Cascading Flash. Don't remind the players of this, but track it.
- They don't retreat and don't pursue past the hillside — they're fixed to the disruption site.

> **DM Note:** Radiant is a damage type most parties at this level resist poorly — the encounter is designed to feel uncomfortable without being punishing. If the party is very depleted, reduce to 2 Dawnwardens. If they're fresh, let all three run. The Fractured Burst on death creates incentive to keep distance, which fits the fight nicely.

---

### After the Fight

When the last Dawnwarden drops, the flickering in the sky above the tower steadies slightly. Not gone — still there — but the worst of it pulls back, like the pressure has dropped.

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> They come apart slowly. Not like the Shard came apart — not a burst. Just a dissolve, the light in them losing coherence and spreading out, and the air around you getting slightly warmer where they were.
>
> The flickering above the tower is still there. But the path to it is clear.

The positive energy regeneration resumes: 1 HP per turn again.

## Beat 9: The Tower

The tower is larger up close than it looked from a distance. Eight stories. The lower floors are the original pale stone — ancestor glyphs carved into every surface, the same dense record-keeping as the vault in Shae Mordai, but the style is older. Whatever this was built to celebrate, it predates the Deathless Court.

The upper floors are dark. The stone there has been stained or altered — not damaged, not broken, just wrong. Like something spent a long time sitting in those rooms and the walls absorbed it.

The ground floor entrance is an open archway. No door. The interior is lit — faintly, from the floor, the original glyph-light still working down here even if nothing above it is.

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> The entrance is an open arch. The stone around it is covered in glyphs — dense, continuous, running from the ground all the way up and around the frame. They're still lit. Faint, warm. Whatever corrupted the upper floors hasn't reached this far down.
>
> Inside: a circular room, maybe thirty feet across. A staircase winds up along the outer wall. The first few steps are the same pale stone as the outside. Further up, the stone darkens.
>
> The flickering light you saw from across the plane — up there, somewhere above you, it's still going.
>
> The Seal is in this tower. So is whatever has been wrong with Irian for the past several months.

**Session ends here.** The party is standing at the base of the tower, staircase in front of them. This is the cliffhanger.

> **DM Note:** Don't let them start climbing tonight. End the session on the image — the open arch, the darkening stairs, the flicker coming from somewhere above. Session 6 is the tower: eight floors, eight encounters, each one a different thing the disruption has twisted. The Seal is at the top.
