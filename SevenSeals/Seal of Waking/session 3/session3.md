# Session 3: The Undying Wood

## Seal of Waking — Act III: The Shore to Shae Mordai

*A session for 4 level-9 characters. Estimated runtime: 3–3.5 hours.*

---

## Session Overview

The party makes landfall on Aerenal's unguarded shore, says goodbye to Nor, and heads into the ancient forest on foot. The forest is beautiful, primordial, and completely indifferent to them — until it isn't. An ambush by Aerenal Stalkers goes well right up until the party realizes they are massively outnumbered. The elves arrive without fanfare and save their lives. First contact with the natives is cold, controlled, and immediately complicated: the elves have a problem they can't solve themselves, and the party is the obvious tool.

### Session Flow

| Beat | Type | Est. Time | Description |
|------|------|-----------|-------------|
| 1 | Transition | 15 min | Final hours of the voyage — Nor's farewell |
| 2 | Exploration | 10 min | Making landfall — the empty shore |

## Beat 1: The Last Hours

The sea voyage is almost over. The party has had a night since Gretchen. The mood aboard *The Fortunate Lapse* is quieter than before.

### Nor

Nor isn't performing anymore. He's navigating. He says more with silence than he usually does with words.

If the party approaches him:

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> He's at the wheel, watching the horizon. When he hears you approach he doesn't turn around.
>
> "She wasn't an animal," he says, after a moment. "I kept thinking she was. Six years. Called her a whale, called her a monster." He pauses. "She was a spell. She didn't have a reason for any of it. She just — was."
>
> He's quiet again.
>
> "I don't know if that makes it better or worse."

He's not looking for a response. If the party offers comfort, he nods. If they don't say anything, that's fine too. He doesn't bring it up again.

> **DM Note:** If the Dream Shard was earned in Session 2, it glows now — faint at first, then steady — as they approach Aerenal's coast. It's drawn toward Irian's manifest zone. If the party notices, Nor glances at it. "Been doing that for about an hour." He doesn't know what it means.

### The Coast

As Aerenal comes into view:

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> Dark cliffs, maybe a hundred feet. The forest starts at the top and runs all the way back — no clearings, no buildings, no smoke. No sign anyone lives here. The trees are enormous even from this distance, and the canopy is so dense it's difficult to see where one tree ends and the next begins.
>
> There are no lights. No boats. No movement on the cliffs.

### Nor's Farewell

Nor drops anchor offshore. He can't bring the ship in — no dock, uncertain depth. He lowers the rowboat.

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> He helps load your gear into the rowboat without being asked. When it's done he stands there with Reginald under one arm.
>
> "I'm not a land guy," he says. "You know that."
>
> He holds out the hand that isn't holding the chicken.
>
> "Come back. I'll be here." He pauses. "Well. A week. Maybe two. Hull needs work and I need to — yeah. Two weeks."

He doesn't watch them row away. When they look back, he's already at the wheel.

## Beat 2: The Shore

The rowboat beaches on dark sand. No dock. No elves. No signs that anyone has been here in a long time. Just the ocean behind them and the forest ahead.

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> The sand is darker than you're used to. More like charcoal than sand. The forest starts maybe forty feet from the waterline and there's no gradual transition — no scrubby beach grass, no dunes. Just the trees, beginning.
>
> The path in is obvious. Not maintained, not cleared, but worn. Something uses it. You don't know what.

> **DM Note:** Let them look around. There's nothing to find here — no threats, no NPCs, no secrets. The emptiness is the point. Give them a moment of "it's just us and this forest" before sending them in.

**Finella's Valenar heritage:** Valenar and Aerenal elves have a fraught history — the Valenar broke from Aerenal centuries ago, and the Aereni consider them deserters at best. Finella may feel this instinctively when she looks at the forest. Leave it as an RP note for now; the elves will notice later.

## Beat 3: The Forest Road

The path runs roughly northeast toward Shae Mordai — a day's travel through old-growth forest.

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> The trees here are enormous — trunks ten, fifteen feet across, roots that break the surface and run for thirty feet before going back underground. The canopy is high enough that you can't make out individual branches from the ground. It's dim down here, but not dark.
>
> It's quiet. Not silent — birds somewhere overhead, wind in the upper canopy — but sound doesn't carry the way it normally does. Your voices stay close.
>
> The path is worn but not maintained. No cut stumps, no cleared brush. Whatever uses it just walks the same line often enough that nothing grows there.

### Skill Checks

**Passive Perception 13:** The party has a feeling of being watched. Not animals. Not movement. Just a feeling. They are observed.

**Active Perception DC 14:** About two hours in — fresh prints on the path. Large. Feline. More than one set. Moving the same direction they are.

**Survival DC 13:** The prints are relatively fresh. Whatever made them came through here before the party did and is ahead of them, not behind.

> **DM Note:** If the party spots the prints and tries to prepare, let them. It doesn't help. The Stalkers are already circling back from ahead.

### The Air Changes

A few miles before the ambush site, the air shifts. The manifest zone is close enough to feel.

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> The light shifts — not brighter, but warmer. More golden. The shadows are still there but they're softer at the edges. The air smells cleaner than it did an hour ago, the way it smells after rain even though there's been no rain.
>
> If you've been tired from the walk, you're less tired now.

**Arcana or Religion DC 13:** This is the edge of Irian's influence — a manifest zone where the boundary between Eberron and Irian is thin. Positive energy bleeding through. Not dangerous. Just present.

## Beat 4: The Aerenal Stalkers

### Setup

The ambush happens on a section of path that cuts through a natural clearing — a gap in the canopy where old trees fell and opened up light. The Stalkers have been hunting here for days.

> **DM Note:** Start with 3 Stalkers. They're beatable at level 9. That's intentional — let the party feel competent. The surrounded reveal is what matters.

### The Initial Attack

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> Something moves in the trees to your left. Then nothing. Then three things happen at once.

Three **Aerenal Stalkers** break from the treeline. Roll initiative.

---

**Traits:**

**Pack Tactics.** The stalker has advantage on attack rolls against a creature if at least one of its allies is adjacent to the target and isn't incapacitated.

**Pounce.** If the stalker moves at least 20 ft straight toward a creature and hits it with a Claw attack on the same turn, the target must succeed on a DC 13 STR saving throw or be knocked prone. If the target is prone, the stalker can make one Bite attack against it as a bonus action.

**Silent Hunt.** The stalker has advantage on Stealth checks. It cannot be detected by passive Perception alone before it attacks — a creature must take the Search action to have any chance of noticing it first.

**Multiattack.** The stalker makes two attacks: one Bite and one Claw.

**Bite.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 10 (1d10+5) piercing damage.

**Claw.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 12 (2d6+5) slashing damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 13 STR saving throw or be knocked prone.

---

### The Turning Point

When the party has clearly gained the upper hand — two Stalkers down, the third wounded:

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> The third one makes a sound you haven't heard before. Low and sustained. Not a warning. More like a signal.

**Perception DC 12:** Pairs of eyes. In the treeline. All around them. Reflecting the light.

**Perception DC 16** (or automatic if they look up from the fight): Fourteen, maybe fifteen shapes. Just outside the trees. All crouched. All watching. None of them moving yet.

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> The one you've been fighting backs up two steps. It doesn't run. It just stops.
>
> There are shapes in the trees. All around you. You count at least a dozen before you stop counting. None of them are moving.
>
> They're waiting.

> **DM Note:** If the party missed the Perception check, give them the reveal anyway when a Stalker retreats and the others tighten the ring. Hold it for a beat before anything else happens. Let it land.

### The Elves

Before the Stalkers move:

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> Arrows. You hear them before you see them — three, four, happening almost simultaneously, coming from directly above.
>
> Three of the shapes in the treeline drop and don't get up. The others scatter. Not in panic — in immediate coordinated retreat, like a signal was given you didn't hear. In about ten seconds, they're gone.
>
> The forest goes quiet.
>
> Five elves drop from the canopy. They land without sound. They were up there the whole time.

## Beat 5: First Contact

The elves are composed. No urgency, no congratulations. They look at the party the way you look at a problem you've decided to deal with.

Their leader steps forward. Tall, pale in the way ancient things are pale — not unhealthy, just long past needing the sun. Dark armor. A shortbow across her back. She looks the party over without hurrying.

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> "The cats have been hunting this path for three days. You were not the first to walk into them."
>
> She doesn't elaborate on what happened to the others.
>
> "You are far from any port. You have no guide, no writ, and no invitation." She pauses. "Where are you going?"

If the party answers honestly (Shae Mordai, the manifest zone):

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> Her expression doesn't change, exactly, but something shifts.
>
> "That is a sacred site. It is not open to outsiders."
>
> She looks at them for another moment. "Come. You will speak with someone who can make decisions."

If they're evasive or lie:

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> "You are not merchants. You are not lost. What do you want in our forest?"

She's patient. She'll wait. She will get an answer.

> **DM Note:** This is **Raeven ir'Talorath**, patrol captain — junior to the elder they're about to meet. She doesn't offer her name unprompted. She doesn't explain where they're going. She simply expects them to follow. Her four soldiers flank the group — not aggressively, just present.
>
> If asked her name: "Raeven." Nothing more.
>
> **Finella's Valenar heritage:** Raeven notices immediately. She will look at Finella longer than the others. She says nothing — but her jaw sets slightly. File it for the elder's audience.

---

## Beat 6: Varaen ir'Talorath

Raeven leads the party to a **waystation** — a structure built into the base of a massive tree, so integrated into the roots that it looks grown rather than built. Inside: clean, spare, functional. Ancestor glyphs carved at the threshold, nothing else.

Waiting inside is **Varaen ir'Talorath**.

### Varaen ir'Talorath

Ancient in the way that makes mortal aging look like a rounding error. She's seated at a stone table with a map of the region carved into its surface. She doesn't stand when they enter.

She looks at each of them in turn. Long enough to be deliberate.

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> "Raeven tells me you were walking into sixteen Stalkers without knowing it."
>
> She says it without judgment. Just a data point.
>
> "Tell me why you want the manifest zone."

If the party explains their purpose:

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> "You are aware that Irian's light is not a resource to be harvested."
>
> She lets that sit.
>
> "If what you describe is true, the disruption you speak of would explain something we have been trying to understand. One of our ancestors has become erratic. A lesser Deathless — Celaen ir'Talorath, of our house — who has kept her vigil in the Vault for eleven centuries without incident. Three weeks ago, she began moving. Attacking. Speaking in fragments."
>
> "Our scholars believe she is reacting to a disturbance in Irian. Something in the manifest zone's light has changed, and she can feel it."

She meets them directly:

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> "We cannot approach her. In her confusion, she does not distinguish between us and threats. You, she has never seen. You may be able to reach her."
>
> "If you restore her to stillness — by whatever means necessary — I will give you access to the manifest zone. Escorted. With conditions. But access."

### The Terms

- They enter the Vault alone. No escort — the elves won't risk another attempt.
- They do not take anything from the Vault. She says this flatly, without accusation. She just says it.
- They return and report what they found before she arranges their escort.

### Questions the Party May Ask

**What does "by whatever means" mean?**

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> "If you can calm her, that is preferable. If you cannot, she must be put to rest. She is suffering. That is not what we built the Vault for."
>
> Pause.
>
> "Either outcome is acceptable to us."

**What is a Deathless?**

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> "Not what your people call undead. The Deathless chose to remain — preserved by Irian's positive energy, not animated by the negative. They are not corpses given motion. They are lives that chose not to end."

**Who was Celaen?**

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> "A commander. She fought in a war this continent won before your nation existed. She chose the vigil voluntarily. She has protected this region of the wood for eleven centuries."
>
> A pause.
>
> "She deserves better than what is happening to her. That is why I am asking strangers."

**Finella's Valenar heritage:** Varaen addresses it once, directly, before they leave.

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> She looks at Finella. "Your bloodline is Valenar."
>
> Not a question.
>
> "I will not hold you responsible for decisions made by ancestors I did not respect." She turns back to the map. "But know that I am watching."

> **DM Note:** Varaen is not warming to the party. She's evaluating them. If they are direct, honest, and competent, she'll deal with them fairly. If they try to negotiate her terms or push back, she's entirely willing to walk away from the arrangement. She has patience they don't. She will not explain herself twice.

## Beat 7: The Vault of Celaen ir'Talorath

The Vault is two hours deeper into the forest. Raeven walks them to the entrance and stops there.

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> The entrance is a stone archway set into a hillside, almost entirely swallowed by roots. The stone is covered in ancestor glyphs. Most of them are still. A few are moving — slowly rotating, rearranging — like something inside is restless.
>
> Raeven stops at the archway.
>
> "She destroyed the last patrol that entered. Three soldiers." She looks at the arch. "Whatever you do in there — be respectful. This is her house."

She steps back. They're on their own.

### Overview

The Vault is four chambers. The air inside is charged with positive energy — all creatures regenerate 1 HP at the start of each of their turns (this applies to everyone inside, including Celaen during combat). The light is sourceless, slightly golden, softer than torchlight.

The Vault is not a trap dungeon. It's a tomb. The danger is Celaen, and the moral complexity is the treasure.

---

### Room 1: The Entry Hall

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> The hall extends about sixty feet. Stone walls carved floor to ceiling with ancestor glyphs — not decorative, these are records. Histories. Battles, names, dates in an old Elvish calendar.
>
> Offerings line the base of the walls. Weapons. Folded cloth. Small carved figures. They've been here a long time but they're not dusty — the air preserves them.
>
> At the far end, two stone doors, slightly ajar. Light seeps through the gap. Moving light — not fire. Something is moving in there.

**Investigation DC 14:** The glyphs on the wall are shifting — slowly, but moving. They're repeating a phrase. Elvish: *"Who enters unbidden walks in debt."* It was probably always here.

**Perception DC 11:** The light through the doors is irregular. Pulsing. Like a heartbeat that isn't quite on rhythm.

---

### Room 2: The Gallery of Memory

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> The stone panels in this room are different — carved in relief, four scenes in sequence along the left wall.
>
> First: a young elf — no armor, no weapon — standing alone in front of something burning. You can't tell what it was. She's not running. She's watching it go.
>
> Second: the same figure, older now, in armor, at the front of a column of soldiers, weapons drawn, mid-advance.
>
> Third: a ceremony of some kind. She's kneeling, and an older elf is placing a mantle or sash across her shoulders. Other figures are watching. It looks formal. Significant.
>
> Fourth: she's alone, standing in front of a stone archway — this archway. Hands at her sides. The doors are open. She's walking in.
>
> It's her. Celaen. These are the four things that mattered.
>
> Along the far wall, stone shelves. Burial goods. A sword in its scabbard, a name engraved on the crossguard. A jade pendant around the neck of her stone effigy. Rolled scrolls sealed in a stone niche.

#### The Treasure

> **DM Note:** Let the party look. Let them want things. Then let them decide. The effigy's face is right there. It's detailed. Someone who knew her carved it.

**The Sword**

A longsword in its scabbard, laid on a stone rest. The name **Celaen ir'Talorath** is engraved on the blade in Elvish. It's clearly a burial good — it's literally named for the person in this tomb.

*Arcana DC 11:* It's magic. A +1 longsword.

*Perception DC 13:* There's a glyph of warding carved into the stone rest beneath the scabbard. *Arcana DC 12:* It will trigger if the sword is lifted without the passphrase.

- *Triggered:* DC 14 DEX save or 22 (4d10) necrotic damage, half on success. Resets after 1 minute.
- *Passphrase:* Inscribed in Elvish on the panel above the shelf — it was written for the burial ritual, not for thieves. Speaking it deactivates the ward. The sword still isn't theirs to take.

---

**The Jade Pendant**

Around the neck of Celaen's stone effigy. Her carved hands are folded over her chest.

*Arcana DC 13:* An Amulet of Natural Armor (+1 AC), very old.

No ward. Taking it means removing it from the effigy.

---

**The Scrolls**

Three scrolls sealed in a stone niche. *Arcana DC 12:* Spell scrolls — two 2nd level, one 3rd level (DM's choice based on party needs).

*Perception DC 15:* A faint ward glyph around the niche. *Arcana DC 13:* Breaking it triggers DC 13 WIS save or frightened for 1 minute. Not a trap mechanism — the weight of ancestral displeasure radiating from the room itself.

---

**Consequences of Taking Anything:**

Celaen, in her current state, won't notice. Varaen will.

When the party returns and reports, she'll pause. Look at whoever took something. "What you carry is not yours."

- *If they return it:* "Good." Nothing more. The deal proceeds, but she noted it.
- *If they refuse:* The deal is void. The elves won't attack them, but they're leaving without access.

---

### Room 3: The Antechamber

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> A stone door, sealed. The frame is covered in ancestor glyphs arranged in a specific pattern — not decorative. Functional.
>
> In the center of the door, a circular recess about the size of a hand. Around it, four small carved symbols: a flame, crossed swords, a kneeling elf, and an open archway.

This is an ancestor ward — not designed to keep people out, but to verify that those entering understand what they're entering.

**The Solution:** Press the symbols in the order of Celaen's life as shown in Room 2: **loss (flame) → battle (crossed swords) → ceremony (kneeling elf) → the vigil (archway).**

*Investigation DC 12 in Room 2:* Small numerals are carved into the bottom corner of each panel. The symbols on the door correspond to each scene in order.

*If they missed the numerals:* The panels read left to right, chronologically. The symbols on the door are arranged clockwise from the top in the same sequence — a patient party who looks at both rooms together can work it out without a check.

**Wrong combinations:** DC 12 CON save or take 5 (1d10) force damage. Resets after 1 minute. The ward is discouraging, not deadly.

When the correct sequence is pressed, the door opens silently.

---

### Room 4: The Sanctum

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> The sanctum is circular, about forty feet across. The ceiling is high enough that you can't see it clearly. In the center of the room there's a stone basin filled with liquid that gives off a steady white-gold glow — enough to light the whole space.
>
> The warmth you've been feeling since you entered the forest is stronger here. Not heat. Closer to the feeling right before you fall asleep somewhere comfortable.
>
> She's near the far wall. Floating maybe three feet off the ground. She looks like an elf — tall, pale, dark hair — except she's giving off light. Not a glow around her. The light is coming from inside her. She's moving, slowly, like she's pacing without walking anywhere.
>
> She sees you and stops.
>
> "You are not elves," she says. Her voice bounces off the stone walls wrong — too many echoes for the room size.
>
> "Who sent you to this place?"

---

### Celaen ir'Talorath — The Lesser Deathless

|------|-------|
| **AC** | 15 (radiant shell) |
| **HP** | 130 |
| **Speed** | 30 ft, Hover 20 ft |
| **STR** | 14 (+2) |
| **DEX** | 14 (+2) |
| **CON** | 18 (+4) |
| **INT** | 16 (+3) |
| **WIS** | 14 (+2) |
| **CHA** | 18 (+4) |

**Saving Throws:** CON +8, WIS +6, CHA +8

**Damage Resistances:** Bludgeoning, Piercing, Slashing from non-magical weapons

**Damage Immunities:** Necrotic, Poison, Radiant

**Condition Immunities:** Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Paralyzed, Poisoned

**Senses:** Truesight 30 ft, passive Perception 12

**Languages:** Elvish (ancient dialect), Common

---

**Traits:**

**Deathless Shell.** Celaen cannot be reduced below 1 HP by weapon attacks or spells alone. To destroy her, the stone basin (her anchor) must be broken.

**Vault's Restoration.** While in the Sanctum, Celaen regains 10 HP at the start of each of her turns. She cannot be worn down by attrition.

---

**Actions (if hostile):**

**Radiant Strike.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +7 to hit, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 13 (2d8+4) radiant damage. On a hit, DC 14 CON save or blinded until end of next turn.

**Dawn's Grasp.** *Ranged Spell Attack:* +7 to hit, range 60 ft. *Hit:* 16 (3d10) radiant damage and the target is restrained in bands of light until the end of Celaen's next turn.

**Aura of Dawn (Recharge 5–6).** Every creature within 30 ft: DC 15 CON save. *Fail:* 21 (6d6) radiant damage and blinded for 1 minute (save ends at end of each turn). *Pass:* Half damage, not blinded.

---

> **DM Note:** Do not open with combat. Celaen is confused, not aggressive. She'll attack if threatened, but she'll also respond to genuine engagement. Her opening line is uncertain, not hostile. Give the party a chance to talk before anything escalates.
>
> Her speech is fragmented. She slips between the present and memories of her vigil. She'll say things that don't quite parse — *"The light changed. The light was always—"* and then stop. *"You smell of the sea. The sea doesn't come here."* She's not incoherent. She's disoriented.

---

### Option 1: Pacify — Skill Challenge

**Goal:** 5 successes before 3 failures. Each approach can only succeed once.

| Skill | DC | Approach |
|-------|-----|---------|
| Persuasion | 14 | Speak to her directly — acknowledge who she was, not just what she is now |
| Insight | 13 | Read her fragmentary speech to understand what's driving the agitation |
| Arcana | 14 | Identify the Irian disruption as the source and name it to her — give it a shape she can understand |
| Religion | 13 | Invoke the correct ancestor-reverence language; treat her vigil as sacred |
| Performance | 13 | Hold the Dream Shard toward her (if the party has it). Its light mirrors hers. She recognizes it. |

**On failure:** She attacks. Combat begins. Players can still attempt Pacify during combat by using their action for a skill check instead of attacking (DC +2). If they accumulate 3 total failures across the whole encounter, she won't respond to Pacify anymore — Option 2 is the only path.

**On success:**

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> She goes still. The erratic light steadies.
>
> "Something in the light changed." Her voice is quieter now. Present. "I felt it shift. I thought — I thought something was coming for the Vault."
>
> She looks at her hands.
>
> "I have been here eleven hundred years. I know this light. And it is wrong."
>
> She looks up at you. "Fix it. Whatever is wrong in Irian — fix it."
>
> The light dims to something calm. She settles near the basin.
>
> "I will wait."

**Questions the party can ask:**

- *What changed?* "The dawn stuttered. It doesn't stutter."
- *What's in Irian?* "Light. Peace. The end of things that want to end. It should be safe." A pause. "It was safe."
- *The seal?* If the party mentions the Seal of Waking: her eyes focus. "Yes. That is the word. Something is waking that should be sleeping. Or something sleeping that should be waking." She shakes her head. "I cannot tell from here."

---

### Option 2: Destroy the Anchor

The stone basin in the center of the room.

*Arcana DC 13 or Investigation DC 14:* Identify it as what's binding Celaen here. Breaking it will release her — not destroy her, exactly, but dissolve the vigil and let her energy return to Irian.

**The Basin**

*AC 12 | HP 40 | No resistances*

Destroying it: the basin cracks. The glowing liquid drains into the floor. Celaen's light flares once — brilliant, warm — and her face clears entirely for just a moment.

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> She looks at you. For just a second, she's entirely present.
>
> "Thank you," she says.
>
> Then the light expands outward and is gone. The sanctum goes quiet. The air still hums, but softer now. Settled.

> **DM Note:** This is the merciful option, not the violent one. The elves understand this. When the party reports back, Varaen will ask: "She is at rest?" and then step away for a moment before continuing. She won't mourn in front of them.

---

### After the Vault

Raeven is waiting at the entrance.

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> She looks at them, then past them into the entrance. Something in her posture shifts.
>
> "Well?"

*If they pacified Celaen:* Raeven's expression is hard to read. "She is calm?" She listens. "Good. That is better."

*If they destroyed the anchor:* She's quiet for a moment. "She is at rest?" She closes her eyes briefly. "Yes. Good."

Back at the waystation, Varaen listens to the full report. Then:

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> "Then we have an arrangement."
>
> She sets a small carved token on the table — the ir'Talorath house mark.
>
> "Present this at Shae Mordai's eastern gate. You will be escorted to the manifest zone. You will not be left alone there. You will not enter the city proper without permission. And you will leave when we tell you to leave."
>
> She pauses.
>
> "Do you agree to these terms?"

If they agree:

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> "Then you will leave at dawn. Tonight you may rest here."

> **DM Note:** If the party stole something from the Vault and hasn't returned it, Varaen notices now. "What you carry is not yours." Give them the chance to make it right before this session ends. If they refuse, the deal is void and they leave Aerenal without access — the sneak-in option is still on the table, but now they know exactly what they'd be risking.
