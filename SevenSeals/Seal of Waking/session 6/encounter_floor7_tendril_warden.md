# Encounter: The Tendril Warden — Floor 7 Landing

---

### Encounter Summary

```
Difficulty:  Hard
Party:       4 players, level 9

Monster Roster:
  1× Tendril Warden — CR 9 (estimated)

The Warden's key combination is Tendril Anchor + Corruption Aura: a Restrained creature within
10 ft of the Warden takes 5 necrotic damage at the start of every turn and cannot move away
without spending an action to tear free. The DM should prioritize getting a target Anchored and
then ignoring them to attack someone else — the trapped character becomes a slow drain while the
Warden creates new problems. Tendril Slam should be held until two or more characters are
clustered.
```

### Tendril Warden

*Large Aberration, chaotic evil*

*This was a great construct-guardian of the Dawnwarden order — built to patrol the approaches to the tower. The corruption has consumed it entirely, remaking its form in void-matter. The shape of its original purpose remains. The purpose does not.*

**Condition Immunities:** Charmed, Frightened, Prone
**Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft, passive Perception 11

---

**Traits:**

**Corruption Aura.** Any creature that starts its turn within 10 ft of the Warden takes 5 (1d10) necrotic damage.

**Tendril Anchor.** When the Warden restrains a creature with Tendril Strike, that creature cannot willingly move more than 5 ft from the Warden's space until the restraint ends (start of the Warden's next turn, or by spending an action to tear free — no roll required).

**Void Form.** When reduced to 0 HP, the Warden collapses and dissolves over 1 minute. It leaves nothing.

---

**Actions:**

**Multiattack.** The Warden makes two Tendril Strike attacks.

**Tendril Strike.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +10 to hit, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 22 (3d10 + 6) necrotic damage. The target is Restrained until the start of the Warden's next turn.

**Tendril Slam (Recharge 5–6).** The Warden drives both arms into the floor and sends a shockwave of void-matter through the ground in a 30 ft cone. Each creature in the area: DC 16 STR save or take 28 (8d6) necrotic damage and be knocked prone; half damage and not knocked prone on a success.

---

**Tactics:**
- It moves immediately toward the party and uses Tendril Strike on the closest target.
- It prioritizes keeping targets Restrained and Anchored — a Restrained creature within 5 ft of it takes Corruption Aura damage every turn and can't escape without using an action.
- After Anchoring a target, it turns its attention to a different character, forcing the party to split focus.
- It uses Tendril Slam as soon as it recharges, targeting clusters of two or more characters.
- It does not retreat. It does not react to damage. It fights until dissolved.

---

**Loot:** None. It dissolves.

### Battle Map Description

> This map must be rendered in a strict top-down, directly overhead bird's-eye view. The camera is directly above looking straight down, as if the map were a floor plan. Not isometric. Not perspective. Not a three-quarter view. Directly overhead — the viewer is looking straight down at the ground.

**Map size:** 65×65 ft. Floor 7 is the largest interior space in the tower — the staircase opens into a full room rather than a landing, with a ceiling roughly 20 ft high.

**Terrain:**

- **Ground surface:** Stone floor, almost entirely covered in tendril growth — flat, dense, black organic matter everywhere except two paths: a strip around the staircase openings and a rough central clearing about 20 ft across. The covered areas are difficult terrain. The central clearing is where the Warden waits.

- **Cult carvings on all walls:** The walls are carved floor to ceiling with cult symbols overlaid on the original Aerenal glyphs — but these don't create terrain. They create atmosphere. The walls are visually dense and dark, with the original pale stone barely visible beneath.

- **Stone pillars (4):** The original tower architecture included four decorative pillars at even intervals around the room, each roughly 3 ft in diameter. They are now fully encased in tendril growth, making them irregular and slightly larger. Each provides full cover for a Small creature or half cover for a Medium creature adjacent to them.

- **Dense tendril masses (2 corners):** In the northwest and southeast corners, tendril accumulation has built up into solid impassable walls of organic matter, each roughly 10×10 ft. These reduce the effective combat area and create natural chokepoints on either side of the room.

- **Staircase openings:** The staircase descends to floor 6 at the south end of the room and continues up to floor 8 at the north end. Each opening is roughly 8 ft wide with no railing. A creature knocked prone within 5 ft of either opening must succeed on a DC 12 DEX save or slide toward the edge.

- **Color and light:** The room is dim. The windows on this floor are partially obscured by external tendril growth — enough to let in diffuse grey-dawn light, but not the clean warm light of the lower floors. The walls are almost entirely black. The stone pillars show pale beneath the vine casing. The central clearing is the lightest point in the room.

> Style: painterly and detailed, similar to a professional tabletop RPG battle map. Rendered strictly from directly above — top-down, no perspective, no isometric angle.
