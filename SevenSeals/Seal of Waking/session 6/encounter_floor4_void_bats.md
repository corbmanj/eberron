# Encounter: Void Bats — Floor 4 Landing

---

### Encounter Summary

```
Difficulty:  Medium
Party:       4 players, level 9

Monster Roster:
  6× Void Bat — CR 1

The bats are individually fragile but dangerous in clusters. The tactical threat is Pack Tactics —
two bats adjacent to a target are attacking with advantage, and the party can't easily thin them
with AoE on a floor this enclosed. The DM should spread bats across multiple targets on round one
to prevent the party from focusing fire efficiently.
```

---

### Stat Blocks

---

### Void Bat

*Small Aberration, chaotic evil*

*Void Bats are not animals. They are extensions of the same void-matter that has been creeping up the tower — corruption shaped into speed and hunger, drawn toward the light they cannot consume.*

| | |
|---|---|
| **AC** | 13 |
| **HP** | 22 (5d6 + 5) |
| **Speed** | 10 ft, Fly 40 ft |
| **STR** | 6 (−2) |
| **DEX** | 16 (+3) |
| **CON** | 12 (+1) |
| **INT** | 2 (−4) |
| **WIS** | 10 (+0) |
| **CHA** | 4 (−3) |

**Damage Resistances:** Necrotic
**Damage Immunities:** Psychic
**Condition Immunities:** Charmed, Frightened, Prone
**Senses:** Blindsight 60 ft, passive Perception 10

---

**Traits:**

**Pack Tactics.** The bat has advantage on attack rolls against a creature if at least one of the bat's allies is within 5 ft of the creature and the ally isn't incapacitated.

**Void Form.** When reduced to 0 HP, the bat collapses into tendril matter and dissolves over 1 minute. It leaves nothing.

**Echolocation.** The bat cannot use its blindsight while deafened.

---

**Actions:**

**Void Bite.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 8 (2d4 + 3) necrotic damage.

**Light Drain (Recharge 6).** One creature within 5 ft: DC 12 CON save or take 10 (3d6) necrotic damage and have any light source they carry reduced by 10 ft radius until the start of the bat's next turn.

---

**Tactics:**
- They don't coordinate with intention — they converge on the nearest target and pile on.
- With two bats adjacent to a target, they're attacking with advantage via Pack Tactics. With three or more, the target is in serious trouble.
- They are not intelligent. They don't retreat, don't protect each other, don't hold abilities. They attack.
- They are individually fragile — the challenge is volume and positioning, not raw HP.

---

**Loot:** None. They dissolve.

### Battle Map Description

> This map must be rendered in a strict top-down, directly overhead bird's-eye view. The camera is directly above looking straight down, as if the map were a floor plan. Not isometric. Not perspective. Not a three-quarter view. Directly overhead — the viewer is looking straight down at the ground.

**Map size:** 50×50 ft. The landing is circular, occupying the full interior width of the tower at this level.

**Terrain:**

- **Ground surface:** Stone floor, pale Aerenal limestone, but almost entirely obscured by tendril growth — a dense, flat layer of black organic matter covers roughly 70% of the floor surface. The only clear areas are a narrow path around the staircase opening and a rough central circle about 15 ft across. The covered areas are difficult terrain.

- **Sealed window alcoves:** Four window alcoves are set into the outer wall at even intervals. Each alcove is roughly 3 ft deep and 6 ft wide, completely packed with dense tendril matter — the vines have grown up the outside of the building and sealed them from within. The alcoves create recessed pockets in the wall that could serve as cramped cover.

- **Tendril pillars:** In three locations against the walls, the vine accumulation has built up into thick organic columns, 3–4 ft across, floor to ceiling. These are impassable but provide half cover for creatures adjacent to them.

- **Staircase opening:** A roughly 8 ft wide gap in the floor near the south wall where the spiral staircase descends. The opening has no railing. A creature knocked prone within 5 ft of it would need to succeed on a DC 12 DEX save or slide toward the edge.

- **Collapsed stone shelving:** Along the east wall, the remnants of built-in stone storage shelves have partially collapsed — slabs of stone at irregular heights, mostly covered in tendrils. This creates a low-cover obstacle (3 ft high) running roughly 12 ft along the wall.

- **Color and light:** Near-total darkness. No light enters through the sealed windows. No glyph-light from the floors below reaches here. The floor and walls are black with tendril growth. The only color is the stone visible in the cleared central area — pale grey under a thin layer of organic matter.

> Style: painterly and detailed, similar to a professional tabletop RPG battle map. Rendered strictly from directly above — top-down, no perspective, no isometric angle.
