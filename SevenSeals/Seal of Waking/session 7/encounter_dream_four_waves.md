# Encounter: Tam's Dream — Four Waves

---

### Encounter Summary

```
Difficulty:  Hard (escalating — Easy → Medium → Hard → Deadly)
Party:       4 players, level 10

Monster Roster:
  Wave 1 — 6× Cultist of the Severed Word — CR 2
  Wave 2 — 8× Nightmare Void Bat — CR 1
  Wave 3 — 2× Dream Quori — CR 7
  Wave 4 — 1× The Dream Sorcerer — CR 13 (estimated)

This encounter runs in four sequential waves with no rest between them. The party
cannot leave the dream or be woken by any means while the Dream Sorcerer lives —
make this clear if they try. Resource management is the core tension: every spell
slot spent in Waves 1–3 is gone when the Sorcerer arrives. The Quori are the pivot
point — they're the first genuinely dangerous wave and will drain the party's best
tools if the players aren't careful. The Sorcerer then opens with Dominate Person
on Tam — taking control of the dreamer in his own dream. Prioritize this.
```

> **Dream Mechanics:**
> - **HP damage is real.** Damage taken in the dream carries over to waking.
> - **Spell slots used are real.** They are spent when the party wakes.
> - **A PC reduced to 0 HP is ejected from the dream** — they wake up immediately, cannot re-enter, and regain 1 HP. They are effectively out of the fight and can only watch.
> - **The dream ends when the Sorcerer is destroyed.** Not before.

---

## Wave 1: Cultists of the Severed Word

*Six figures rise from below deck, moving silently. Their mouths are wrong. Their eyes are fixed.*

---

### Cultist of the Severed Word

*Medium Humanoid, neutral evil*

*The cult's devotees in dream-form — their vow of silence made absolute, the angular glyphs they carved in life now glowing faint blue-black on their skin.*

| | |
|---|---|
| **AC** | 13 (leather armor) |
| **HP** | 32 (5d8+10) |
| **Speed** | 30 ft |
| **STR** | 12 (+1) |
| **DEX** | 14 (+2) |
| **CON** | 14 (+2) |
| **INT** | 10 (+0) |
| **WIS** | 10 (+0) |
| **CHA** | 8 (−1) |

**Condition Immunities:** Frightened
**Senses:** Passive Perception 10

---

**Traits:**

**Severed Vow.** The cultist cannot speak and is immune to any effect requiring it to hear or produce a verbal component, such as *Command*. It has advantage on Charisma (Intimidation) checks that rely entirely on silence or physical threat.

**Glyph-Marked.** Once per turn when the cultist hits a creature with an attack, that creature must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or lose the ability to speak until the end of their next turn. A creature that cannot speak cannot cast spells with verbal components.

---

**Actions:**

**Multiattack.** The cultist makes two attacks: one with its shortsword and one with its curved dagger.

**Shortsword.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d6+2) piercing damage.

**Curved Dagger.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d4+2) piercing damage.

---

**Tactics:**
- Rush the party immediately — two cultists per PC if possible.
- Prioritize spellcasters: Tam and Ozzy. Silence prevents verbal spell components.
- They do not coordinate tactically beyond swarming. They are not smart. They are relentless.
- They do not retreat. They fight until destroyed.

---

**Loot:** Personal effects — small iron carving tools, vials of dark ink, a folded letter in cipher. Roll DMG Individual Treasure (CR 1–4) per cultist.

---

## Wave 2: Nightmare Void Bats

*The bats do not appear from the rigging so much as they detach from it — eight shapes that were hanging there, still, the whole time.*

---

### Nightmare Void Bat

*Small Aberration, chaotic evil*

*A void bat as it appears in a dream — the same shape as the ones in the tower, but the holes where its eyes should be are deeper and more deliberate, as if something made a decision about them.*

| | |
|---|---|
| **AC** | 14 |
| **HP** | 28 (8d6) |
| **Speed** | 10 ft, Fly 40 ft |
| **STR** | 6 (−2) |
| **DEX** | 17 (+3) |
| **CON** | 11 (+0) |
| **INT** | 2 (−4) |
| **WIS** | 12 (+1) |
| **CHA** | 4 (−3) |

**Senses:** Blindsight 60 ft (blind beyond this radius), passive Perception 11

---

**Traits:**

**Echolocation.** The bat can't use its blindsight while deafened.

**Pack Tactics.** The bat has advantage on attack rolls against a creature if at least one of the bat's allies is within 5 ft of the creature and not incapacitated.

**Void Form.** When reduced to 0 hit points, the bat dissolves over 6 seconds. It leaves nothing.

---

**Actions:**

**Void Bite.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 8 (2d4+3) necrotic damage.

**Light Drain (Recharge 6).** The bat emits a pulse of void-matter in a 15-ft radius. Each creature in the area must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or take 10 (3d6) necrotic damage and have any light they are producing — including magical light — extinguished until the start of their next turn.

---

**Tactics:**
- Arrive as Wave 1 ends or is clearly losing — they don't wait for every cultist to fall.
- Never attack alone. Pack Tactics is their entire combat identity.
- Target whoever is most isolated from the group.
- Light Drain as soon as it recharges, aimed at the densest cluster.
- They cannot be frightened and do not disengage. They dissolve when killed.

---

**Loot:** None. They dissolve.

---

## Wave 3: The Dream Sorcerer

*Something rises from the sea on the port side. It does not climb — it simply ascends, water running off robes that are the color of deep ocean. Where its face should be, there is nothing. It stands at the rail and watches.*

---

## Wave 3: Dream Quori

*The rigging goes still. Then two shapes descend from above — not dropping, but lowering, as if the dream is producing them. They are riding something. The shapes beneath them are wrong: dark, four-legged, moving like carousel horses left in the dark too long. You have seen this before. Not here. Not on a ship. Somewhere worse.*

---

### Dream Quori

*Medium Aberration, lawful evil*

*Native to Dal Quor, the plane of dreams, the Quori follow open wounds between planes the way water follows cracks. Tam's scar is an open wound. These two have been waiting at its edge since the dream began.*

| | |
|---|---|
| **AC** | 15 |
| **HP** | 91 (14d8+28) |
| **Speed** | 30 ft, Fly 40 ft (hover) |
| **STR** | 14 (+2) |
| **DEX** | 16 (+3) |
| **CON** | 15 (+2) |
| **INT** | 17 (+3) |
| **WIS** | 14 (+2) |
| **CHA** | 18 (+4) |

**Saving Throws:** INT +6, WIS +5, CHA +7
**Damage Immunities:** Psychic
**Condition Immunities:** Charmed, Frightened
**Senses:** Truesight 60 ft, passive Perception 12
**Languages:** Deep Speech, telepathy 120 ft

---

**Traits:**

**Dream-Born.** While in a dream, the Quori has advantage on all saving throws. It cannot be banished or transported out of the dream by any means short of ending the dream itself.

**Fear Aura.** Any creature that starts its turn within 10 ft of the Quori must succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw or be Frightened of it until the start of their next turn.

**Shadow Mount.** The Quori arrives mounted on a shadow-aberration. The mount has AC 10, HP 20, and is destroyed if it takes any damage. Its square counts as difficult terrain. Destroying the mount does not harm the Quori.

---

**Actions:**

**Multiattack.** The Quori makes two Psychic Lash attacks and one Claw attack.

**Psychic Lash.** *Ranged Spell Attack:* +7 to hit, range 60 ft. *Hit:* 15 (2d10+4) psychic damage. *(Spell-like — not counterspellable.)*

**Claw.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 9 (2d6+2) slashing damage.

**Dream Pierce (Recharge 5–6).** One creature within 60 ft must succeed on a DC 15 Intelligence saving throw or take 22 (4d10) psychic damage and be Stunned until the end of their next turn. Half damage and not Stunned on a success.

---

**Spells:** *(Spell save DC 15, Spell attack +7)*

| Spell | Level | Uses | Counterspellable |
|-------|-------|------|-----------------|
| *Detect Thoughts* | 2nd | At will | ✓ |
| *Fear* | 3rd | 2/day | ✓ |
| *Dominate Person* | 5th | 1/day | ✓ |

*Fear: The first Quori casts this immediately on its first turn, targeting as many party members as possible. It uses its second casting if the first is counterspelled or fails against the whole group.*

---

**Tactics:**
- One Quori casts Fear immediately. The other opens with Psychic Lash on the most isolated target.
- They use Detect Thoughts (at will) to read surface thoughts — they know what the party is planning before the party acts on it. Use this to make them feel unnervingly reactive: if the party is about to concentrate, the Quori targets that caster; if someone is saving a spell, the Quori presses them.
- Dream Pierce is saved for a concentrating caster — Stunned drops concentration.
- Dominate Person is a last resort if one Quori is below half HP: it dominates rather than attacks, staying relevant while bleeding resources.
- They do not flee. They dissolve when destroyed.

---

**Loot:** None. The shadow mounts collapse. The Quori dissolve, leaving only the impression that something was reading the room very carefully the entire time.

---

## Wave 4: The Dream Sorcerer

*Something rises from the sea on the port side. It does not climb — it simply ascends, water running off robes that are the color of deep ocean. Where its face should be, there is nothing. It stands at the rail and watches.*

---

### The Dream Sorcerer

*Medium Aberration (Dream Projection), chaotic evil*

*A projection of something that exists elsewhere — tall, robed, featureless where a face should be. It has been watching this dream for some time. It appears, at last, to have decided to participate.*

| | |
|---|---|
| **AC** | 17 (dream armor) |
| **HP** | 136 (16d8+64) |
| **Speed** | 30 ft |
| **STR** | 14 (+2) |
| **DEX** | 16 (+3) |
| **CON** | 18 (+4) |
| **INT** | 20 (+5) |
| **WIS** | 14 (+2) |
| **CHA** | 22 (+6) |

**Saving Throws:** CON +8, INT +9, CHA +10
**Damage Resistances:** Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from nonmagical attacks
**Condition Immunities:** Charmed, Frightened
**Senses:** Truesight 60 ft, passive Perception 12

---

**Traits:**

**Dream Authority.** The dream does not end while the Sorcerer is alive. No creature in the dream can be forced awake by any means — including magic — until the Sorcerer is destroyed or a creature is reduced to 0 HP.

**Spell Resistance.** The Sorcerer has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

**Projection.** When reduced to 0 hit points, the Sorcerer does not die. The robes collapse. The featureless mask cracks — and for just a moment, each creature within 30 ft catches a glimpse of something beneath it: a face, cold and interested, that seems to be looking back at them rather than away. Then it is gone. The dream ends. Everyone wakes up.

---

**Actions:**

**Multiattack.** The Sorcerer makes two Sorcerous Lash attacks.

**Sorcerous Lash.** *Ranged Spell Attack:* +10 to hit, range 60 ft. *Hit:* 14 (2d8+5) force damage. *(Spell-like — not counterspellable.)*

**Nightmare Pulse (Recharge 5–6).** The Sorcerer releases a wave of concentrated dreamstuff in a 30-ft radius. Each creature in the area must succeed on a DC 18 Wisdom saving throw or be Stunned until the start of their next turn. *(Spell-like — not counterspellable.)*

---

**Spells:** *(Spell save DC 18, Spell attack +10)*

| Spell | Level | Uses | Counterspellable |
|-------|-------|------|-----------------|
| *Counterspell* | 3rd | 3/day | ✓ |
| *Dominate Person* | 5th | 2/day | ✓ |
| *Chain Lightning* | 6th | 2/day | ✓ |
| *Disintegrate* | 6th | 1/day | ✓ |

*Dominate Person: The Sorcerer casts this as a reaction immediately after finishing its first Sorcerous Lash, if a target is available.*

---

**Tactics:**
- On its first turn, it casts Dominate Person on Tam — taking control of the dreamer inside his own dream. If Tam is already incapacitated or ejected, it targets the next most dangerous spellcaster.
- It uses Counterspell aggressively. It has 3 uses and will burn all of them on high-level party spells (5th level or higher).
- Once 3+ party members are within 60 ft of each other, it casts Chain Lightning.
- It does not move unless forced to. It stands where it rose and receives the fight.
- It uses Nightmare Pulse as soon as it recharges, targeting the largest cluster.
- It saves Disintegrate for a PC who has dealt it more than 50 damage, or who it cannot otherwise neutralize.
- If Dominate Person succeeds, it commands Tam to attack his allies on the Dominated turns. Simple, brutal, effective.

---

**Loot:** None. It is a projection. When the mask dissolves, the sea below the port rail is calm and dark and empty.

### Battle Map Description

> This map must be rendered in a strict top-down, directly overhead bird's-eye view. The camera is directly above looking straight down, as if the map were a floor plan. Not isometric. Not perspective. Not a three-quarter view. Directly overhead — the viewer is looking straight down at the ground.

**Map size:** 80×40 ft. The deck of a mid-sized sailing vessel — larger than it should be for a ship this small, the way spaces in dreams expand past their real dimensions.

**Terrain:**

- **Deck surface:** Dark, salt-worn planks. The grain of the wood is visible but wrong — the lines don't quite converge the way wood grain should. Slightly luminescent at the seams, as if light is bleeding up from below.

- **Two masts:** One forward, one amidships, each roughly 3 ft in diameter at the base. Rigging descends from both — thick rope lines crossing the deck at various heights (difficult terrain for creatures moving below them, half cover if crouching near a mast). This is where the bats were hanging.

- **Cargo crates (4, lashed amidships):** Two groups of two crates each, secured with heavy rope on either side of the central mast. Each group is roughly 5×5 ft and 3 ft high — full cover from one side, half cover from the other. Sturdy enough to stand on.

- **Ship's wheel and helm (stern):** A raised platform 5 ft above deck level, accessible by four steps on either side. The wheel turns slowly on its own — no one is steering. The raised position gives height advantage (+1 to attack rolls for ranged, clear sightlines aft).

- **Open hatches (2):** One forward, one just behind the amidships mast. Each is a 5×5 ft opening leading below deck — 10 ft drop, DC 12 DEX save to grab the edge if knocked back near one. The hatches are dark. Nothing comes up from them during the encounter.

- **The sea:** Visible on both long sides of the ship — a 5-ft-wide section of open rail on port and starboard. The water is the wrong color: not black, not blue, but both at once, perfectly still, faintly luminescent. A creature knocked into the dream sea falls 10 ft and must spend their movement to climb back up (DC 14 Athletics). The water is not dangerous — but it is wrong, and any creature that touches it knows immediately that something below the surface is aware of them.

- **Sky and lighting:** No stars. No moon. A flat dark expanse overhead. The ship produces its own faint light — the luminescent seams in the deck, a lantern at the bow that burns without flame. The overall light level is dim, except within 10 ft of the bow lantern (bright light).

> Style: painterly and detailed, similar to a professional tabletop RPG battle map. Rendered strictly from directly above — top-down, no perspective, no isometric angle.
