# Session 4: The Vault of Celaen ir'Talorath

## Seal of Waking — Act IV: The Crypt

*A session for 4 level-9 characters. Estimated runtime: 3–3.5 hours.*

> **DM Note — Correction from Session 3:** Varaen ir'Talorath is male. Session 3 materials incorrectly used female pronouns. Use he/him going forward.

## Session Overview

The party enters the Vault of Celaen ir'Talorath — an ancient tomb now poisoned from within. The Cult of the Severed Word has planted an artifact deep in the sanctum, and its corruption has been spreading outward for weeks: warping the vault's stone guardians, tearing lesser ancestor spirits from their rest, and driving Celaen herself into an erratic frenzy. The party must fight through three layers of that corruption, solve a puzzle, and reach Celaen at the heart of it. When they return to Varaen, the deal holds — and the path to Shae Mordai opens.

## Opening

Picks up immediately after the end of Session 3. Raeven has walked them to the vault entrance and stopped.

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> The entrance is a stone archway set into a hillside, almost entirely swallowed by roots. The stone is covered in ancestor glyphs. Most of them are still. A few are moving — slowly rotating, rearranging — like something inside is restless.
>
> Raeven stops at the archway. She looks at it for a moment.
>
> "Whatever you do in there — be respectful. This is her house."

She steps back. They're on their own.

> **DM Note:** The vault has one rule that the party should discover rather than be told: **positive energy regeneration does not function correctly here anymore.** The corruption has suppressed it. They do NOT regenerate 1 HP per turn. The restored version of this mechanic only kicks in after the artifact is destroyed in Room 9.

## Room 1: The Entry Hall

The hall is 15 ft wide and 60 ft long. The ceiling is 12 ft. Stone walls carved floor to ceiling with ancestor glyphs — continuous records, histories, names, dates. Offerings line the base of both walls: bundled cloth, small carved figures, a few bladed weapons laid flat.

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> The air should be warm in here. It isn't.
>
> It's close — like the air in a room where something has been burning and the smoke is almost gone. The glyphs on the walls are lit from within, faintly, but the light is unsteady. Flickering.

**Perception DC 11:** The glyph lines on the walls are discolored — slightly darker as you go deeper, like the stone absorbed something it shouldn't have. The corruption starts faint here and gets worse further in.

**Investigation DC 13:** One of the offerings near the base of the right wall has been disturbed — a small carved figurine slid aside and set back down slightly wrong, not flush against the others. Someone moved it and replaced it in a hurry. Underneath, scratched faintly into the stone floor: a vertical line with a horizontal slash. Small. Easy to miss. *Arcana or Religion DC 14:* A Severed Word navigation glyph — the kind a team would scratch to mark a route in the dark. Someone came through here, and this was their first marker.

## Room 2: The Gallery of Memory

25 ft wide, 35 ft long. Four large relief panels along the left (east) wall, running floor to ceiling. Stone shelving along the right (west) wall.

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> Four carved panels on the left wall, each one a scene from the same woman's life.
>
> First: a young elf, no armor, no weapon, standing in front of something burning. She's not running.
>
> Second: older now, in armor, at the front of a column of soldiers, weapons drawn.
>
> Third: kneeling. An older elf placing a mantle or sash across her shoulders. Formal. Important.
>
> Fourth: alone, standing in front of a stone archway with the doors open. Walking in.
>
> On the right wall: a stone effigy of the same woman, hands folded at her chest, a jade pendant around its neck. A sword in a scabbard on a carved stone rest below it. A shelf with rolled scrolls sealed in a stone niche.
>
> At the bottom corner of each panel, small numerals in Elvish.

> **DM Note:** The panels are numbered 1–4, left to right. This is the key to the puzzle door in Room 6. If the party notices the numerals here, they'll have the solution before they reach the door.

### The Treasure

Let the party look. The effigy's face is right there. Someone who knew her carved it.

**The Sword**

A longsword in a scabbard on a stone rest, the name *Celaen ir'Talorath* engraved on the blade in Elvish.

*Arcana DC 11:* It's magic. A +1 longsword.

*Perception DC 13:* A glyph of warding is carved into the stone rest beneath the scabbard. *Arcana DC 12:* It triggers if the sword is lifted without the passphrase.

- *Triggered:* DC 14 DEX save or 22 (4d10) necrotic damage, half on success.
- *Passphrase:* Inscribed in Elvish on the panel directly above the shelf — written for the burial ritual. Speaking it deactivates the ward. The sword is still not theirs to take.

**The Jade Pendant**

Around the neck of the effigy. Her carved hands are folded over her chest.

*Arcana DC 13:* An Amulet of Natural Armor (+1 AC), very old. No ward. Taking it means removing it from the effigy's neck.

**The Scrolls**

Three scrolls in a sealed niche. *Arcana DC 12:* Spell scrolls — *Gentle Repose* (2nd level), *Shatter* (2nd level), and *Speak with Dead* (3rd level).

*Perception DC 15:* A faint ward glyph around the niche. *Arcana DC 13:* Breaking it triggers a DC 13 WIS save or frightened for 1 minute.

**Consequences of Taking Anything**

Celaen won't notice. Varaen will. When they return: *"What you carry is not yours."* Return it and the deal holds. Refuse and the deal is void.

## Room 3: The Breach

15 ft wide, 20 ft long. A side corridor branches east off the main path just past the Gallery — the cult's entry point.

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> The left wall of the corridor has a section about eight feet wide where the stone has been broken through from the outside. The rubble has been pushed inward and swept to the sides. Someone made this opening deliberately and took their time doing it.
>
> The edges of the breach are scored with chisel marks. Along the broken stone, scratched into the wall at eye height: a vertical line with a horizontal slash, repeated every few feet heading deeper.
>
> About ten feet inside the breach, face down on the floor: a body. Human. Dark traveling clothes. He's been dead for weeks.
>
> The corridor runs east about twenty feet before turning south into shadow.

**The Cult's Route:** The breach leads into a service corridor the cultists used to bypass the main entry wards. The scratched glyphs are navigation markers for a team working in the dark. There are faint boot prints in the dust — multiple people, some going deeper, fewer coming back. This man was one of them — he made it back to the breach but not through it.

**Investigation DC 12:** The man's tongue has been surgically removed — old, healed over, not a wound from the fight that killed him. On his belt: a small iron chisel and an empty vial. His clothes have no insignia, but the stitching on the inside of his collar repeats the same symbol scratched on the walls — a vertical line with a horizontal slash. *Arcana or Religion DC 14:* A Severed Word glyph. Cult of the Severed Word. He was here deliberately.

**What killed him:** A warden from Room 4 caught him on the way back out. He made it this far.

### Trap: The Cult Tripwire

The cult set a simple trap before the hall of wardens, to warn them of anything following.

**Perception DC 13:** A thin wire stretched across the corridor at shin height, connected to a small suspended vial of alchemist's fire wedged in a crack above the door.

**Thieves' Tools or DEX DC 12:** Disarmed safely.

**Triggered:** The vial shatters. 7 (2d6) fire damage to whoever triggered it (DC 12 DEX save for half). The sound echoes through the vault — the Wardens in Room 4 cannot be surprised.

## Room 5: The Hall of Offering

25 ft wide, 25 ft long. A formal antechamber for ancestor offerings — carved wall niches at regular intervals, each containing a small clay figurine or ceremonial object. In the center of the far wall, a ceremonial shield mounted on a display bracket.

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> The walls here have niches, each one holding something small — carved figures, folded cloth, clay vessels. All of it undisturbed. Unlike the other rooms, this one feels cleaner. The corruption hasn't reached this far. The air is just cold.
>
> On the far wall, mounted on a bracket between two niches: a round shield. Dark wood, silver inlay, ancestor glyphs running around the rim in a continuous ring.

### The Shield

*Perception DC 11:* The glyphs are still active — they shift slightly when you look directly at them.

*Arcana DC 12:* A Shield +1. The wood is old — if it takes more than 15 damage in a single hit, it shatters.

### Trap: The Ancestor Ward

The bracket has an active ancestor ward — not a punishment trap, just a gate. It's designed to prevent removal by anyone who hasn't spoken the offering prayer.

*Perception DC 11:* A carved glyph on the wall directly beside the shield, outlined in faint blue light.

*Arcana DC 13:* It triggers if the shield is removed without speaking the prayer aloud.

*The prayer:* Inscribed in Elvish on the niche directly to the left of the shield. Translation (DC 12 Elvish or Comprehend Languages): *"I carry this with care and return it with thanks."* Speaking it aloud deactivates the ward for 1 minute.

**Triggered (removing the shield without the prayer):** DC 14 DEX save or 22 (4d10) force damage, half on success. The ward resets after 1 minute.

The shield isn't theirs to take — same rules as Room 2. Varaen will notice.

## Room 6: The Antechamber

20 ft wide, 20 ft deep. Mostly empty. The south wall is a single large sealed stone door with no handles.

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> The door at the far end is sealed — no handle, no mechanism. The frame is covered in carved glyphs arranged in deliberate geometric patterns, not records. Instructions.
>
> In the center of the door: a circular recess, palm-sized. Around it, four carved symbols: a flame, crossed swords, a kneeling figure, an open archway.
>
> The glyphs on the door frame are moving. Just slightly. The symbols around the recess are still.

This is an ancestor ward — designed to verify that those entering understand whose house they're walking through.

**The Solution:** Press the symbols in the order of Celaen's life as shown in the panels of Room 2.

**flame (loss) → crossed swords (battle) → kneeling figure (ceremony) → open archway (entering the vigil)**

*If they have the numerals from Room 2:* Automatic. The panels were numbered and the symbols correspond.

*If they don't:* The panels were left to right, chronological. The symbols on the door are clockwise from the top. A patient party can reconstruct the order.

*Religion or History DC 12:* Aerenal ancestor rites follow a life-cycle structure. Loss comes first — it is what shapes everything that follows.

**Wrong combinations:** DC 12 CON save or 5 (1d10) force damage. The ward resets after 1 minute.

When the correct sequence is pressed, the door opens without sound.

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> The door pulls inward, slow and steady, without grinding. Beyond it: a passage, maybe thirty feet long, and something wrong with the walls.

### Encounter 2

> **Read Aloud:**

**3 Corrupted Vigil Shades.** These were lesser ancestor spirits — not the Deathless, but residual echoes bound to the vault by ancient rite — that the artifact has torn loose and twisted. They float. They don't speak. They remember nothing.

The dark fluid in the center 10 ft of the corridor is concentrated necrotic runoff from the artifact. It isn't deep — just a thin coating on the stone.

- *Perception DC 12:* Noticeable before stepping in — the staining is visible.

### The Dead Handler

The cult handler slumped in the corner has been dead for several days. On his body:

**A sending stone** — one of a matched pair. The other stone is with someone in the Cult of the Severed Word. If a creature speaks into this stone, the other activates. Whoever is holding the other end will answer — and realize immediately that it isn't their operative.

*This is a significant lore opportunity if the party chooses to use it. They may learn something about the cult's broader plans. They may also tip off whoever is on the other end that the mission has failed. DM discretion on what the voice says — it should be unsettling and short.*

**A leather journal** — entries in a small tight hand, the last few written in increasingly shaky script as the corruption affected him. Key entries:

- *Entry 1 (arrival):* The breach is made. Wards bypassed at the secondary access point as planned. Wardens are dormant. Proceeding to the sanctum.

**An insulating cloth** — a small square of dark material, heavily treated. *Arcana DC 12:* Designed to handle cursed or necrotic objects without contact damage. If used to handle the Shard of Severed Dawn, it protects the bearer from the artifact's passive necrotic drain.

## Room 9: The Sanctum

40 ft circular. The ceiling is domed, 20–25 ft high. The central stone basin on its raised plinth — but the light from it is wrong. It should be white-gold and warm. It's dim, guttering, and where the basin's edge meets the stone of the plinth, there are dark veins spreading outward like cracks.

And Celaen is here.

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> The basin is still glowing, but barely. The light it throws is uneven — it pulses with a slow, sick rhythm, and the shadows it makes are wrong. Dark veins run out from the basin's base across the floor like roots.
>
> She's near the far wall. Floating, three feet off the ground, but not steady. She drifts a foot left, stops, drifts back. Her light should be warm. It's cold. White, almost blue.
>
> She sees you immediately.
>
> Her eyes are wide. Not angry. Terrified.
>
> "It won't stop," she says. Her voice bounces off the walls with too many echoes. "I can hear it. It keeps speaking. It will not — I told it — it will not —"
>
> She focuses on you. Just for a moment.
>
> "Help me or leave. I don't care which."

---

### The Artifact: The Shard of Severed Dawn

Wedged into a crack in the stone basin — a jagged black crystal about eight inches long, pulsing slowly with dark energy. It has been leeching the basin's positive energy for three weeks and feeding it into the cult's broader network.

**Finding it:** It's visible immediately — the black crystal against the glowing basin is obvious.

**Passive effect while present:** Any creature that touches the basin without the insulating cloth takes 7 (2d6) necrotic damage. The vault's positive energy regeneration is suppressed.

---

### Shard of Severed Dawn

*AC 12 | HP 20 | Vulnerable: Radiant*

**To destroy:** Attack it (with the insulating cloth to avoid the passive damage, or just take the damage), cast Dispel Magic at 3rd level (DC 14 Arcana check) or 5th level (automatic), or use Shatter (deals double damage).

**When destroyed:** It shatters with a burst of necrotic energy. Every creature in the sanctum: DC 14 CON save or take 21 (6d6) necrotic damage, half on success.

Then the vault exhales.

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> The crystal cracks. Then the crack splits. Then it comes apart.
>
> The burst hits you. Then it's gone.
>
> The basin flares — not the sick pulse from before, but a real flare, full and warm, spreading across the floor and up the walls. The dark veins are still there in the stone but they're not moving anymore. Going cold.
>
> The air changes. Warmer. That feeling from the forest — the one that made you feel more awake — comes back, stronger, in here.
>
> And you regenerate 1 HP at the start of each of your turns. The vault is breathing again.

---

### Celaen — Resolution

When the Shard is destroyed, Celaen goes still. The erratic drifting stops. She stands in the air and puts her hands over her face.

After a moment:

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> She lowers her hands. Her light has steadied — warm again, not cold.
>
> "Three weeks," she says. Quiet. Present. "I knew something was wrong. I could feel it pulling at the basin, at me, and I couldn't — I couldn't think around it. It was like trying to speak with someone shouting in your ear."
>
> She looks at the remains of the crystal.
>
> "Who sent them?"

The party can tell her what they know. She listens without interrupting.

**If they mention the Seal of Waking or the disruption to Irian:**

> "Yes. The dawn stuttered. That is the correct word for it. I've felt that pulse six times in eleven centuries, and each time it meant something had gone wrong in the world above. But this — what they placed here — this was not the cause. This was taking advantage of it."
>
> Pause.
>
> "Whatever is happening in Irian is not the cult's doing. They simply found a wound and made it worse."

**If they mention Varaen:**

> "Tell him I am stable. Tell him I am sorry for the patrol."
>
> She doesn't say anything else about it. The grief is there but she's not going to share it.

---

### Option: Destroy the Anchor Instead

If the party decides to free Celaen entirely rather than stabilize her — destroying the basin itself — the mechanic works as written in Session 3. The Shard goes with it.

Destroying the basin: it still requires breaking the Shard first (or destroying the basin and Shard together — AC 14, HP 60 combined if they go after the whole thing at once).

> **DM Note:** If they destroy the Shard first and then offer to free Celaen by destroying the basin, let her decide. She'll consider it.
>
> "Eleven centuries," she says. "I knew I would have to decide this eventually."
>
> She looks at the basin. "Yes. Do it."

The release plays out as written in Session 3.

## Beat 10: Return to Varaen

Raeven is at the vault entrance when they come out. She takes one look at them.

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> "You're alive." She says it like she's noting a weather condition.
>
> She looks past you into the arch. Waiting.
>
> "And her?"

When they report:

*If Celaen is stable:* Raeven's jaw unclenches slightly. "Good." She turns and starts walking. "Come."

*If Celaen is at rest (basin destroyed):* She closes her eyes for a moment. "Was it her choice?" If yes: "Good." If no: her jaw tightens and she says nothing more. The deal still holds, but she notes it.

---

Back at the waystation, Varaen is seated at the carved stone table. He looks up when they enter. He reads their faces before any of them speak.

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> "Tell me."

He listens to the full account. When they finish — the cult, the Shard, the journal, Celaen's condition — he is quiet for a long moment.

> "The Cult of the Severed Word." He says it flatly. "We have seen their markings on the coast for two years. We did not know what they were building toward."
>
> He looks at the table.
>
> "You have done what I asked. More than I asked." He sets the ir'Talorath token on the table.
>
> "Shae Mordai. Eastern gate. Present this. You will be escorted to the manifest zone. You will not be left alone there. You will leave when you are told."
>
> He meets them directly. "Do you agree to these terms?"

If they agree:

> "Then you leave at dawn. Tonight you rest here."

**If they hand over the journal:** He reads it, slowly. When he finishes he sets it down without comment. But when he speaks again, his tone is marginally less formal — not warm, but different. "I will want to speak with you again when your business in the manifest zone is done."
