## 1. The Spy Agent (First Half of the Journey)

### **Agent Name**

**Sereth Vahl**
Publicly: a soft-spoken Zil scholar traveling to Korranberg
Secretly: a probationary observer for **the Cult of the Severed Word** (Chatechysma’s cult)

### **Why She Hasn’t Taken the Vow**

Sereth is being *tested*.
She has not yet “earned silence.” Her task is to **observe, remember, and report**, not interfere.

If she succeeds, she will be given the knife.

---

### **Appearance & Manner**

* Early 30s, human or half-elf
* Well-kept travel clothes, ink-stained fingers
* Speaks carefully, deliberately
* Maintains *too much* eye contact
* Smiles when others look uncomfortable

She sits near the party by coincidence:

> “I hope you don’t mind—this car gets such dreadful drafts.”

She never lies outright—but **selectively withholds truth**.

---

### **How She Approaches the Party**

She engages **one PC at a time**, never the whole group:

* Asks the kalashtar about “dream physiology”
* Compliments the barbarian’s gauntlet *specifically*
* Asks where they’re headed but never says where she’s going

She frames everything as *academic curiosity*.

---

### **What She Wants to Learn**

She is gathering *confirmation*, not discovery.

She wants to know:

* Do they possess something tied to **Dal Quor**?
* Are they aware of **Chatechysma by name**?
* How protected are they, mentally and spiritually?
* Which party member seems most fragile or emotionally reactive?

If she learns:

* **The Seal of Waking exists** → she sends confirmation
* **The kalashtar is key** → cult escalation begins
* **They are uncertain or fractured** → ambush is approved

---

### **What the Party Can Learn From Her**

If pressed, Insight (DC 14–16):

* She *knows* what a quori is
* She flinches when Dal Quor is mentioned
* She has a ritual scar **under her tongue**—unfinished

If magically compelled or tricked:

* The cult reveres a being called **“The Sleepless Word”**
* Silence is holy; speech is a weakness
* “Soon, none of us will dream without permission”

She leaves the train at Starilaskur.
They will not see her again.

## 2. The Broken Coupling Encounter (Second Half)

### **The Event**

Near dusk, as the rail skirts the **foothills of the Seawall Mountains**, the train jolts violently.

Metal screams.
The engine car pulls away.

The party’s car—and 1–2 others—**shear loose**, grinding to a halt on the elevated rail spur overlooking rocky terrain.

A horn sounds once.
Then silence.

---

### **Why This Happened**

The cult does **not** want the party dead yet.

They want:

* The kalashtar tested
* The party weakened
* Proof the Seal can be taken by force

This is a *controlled assault*.

---

### **Battlefield Layout (Text Map Description)**

**Battle Area Size:** ~70 ft x 50 ft

**Train Car**

* 60 ft long, 10 ft wide interior
* Doors at each end
* Windows smashed outward during the decoupling
* Roof accessible via Athletics check (DC 12)

**Surroundings**

* 15–30 ft drop to rocky foothills
* Scattered boulders (half cover)
* Sparse pine trees
* Rail line curves away into mist

**Environmental Hazards**

* Unstable railcar (Dex save DC 13 when shoved or knocked prone)
* Strong mountain wind (ranged attacks beyond 30 ft at disadvantage)

---

### **Enemies**

Designed for **4 level-9 PCs**, dangerous but fair.

#### **Cult of the Severed Word – Assault Team**

**1 Cult Handler (Leader)**

* **Base Stat Block:** Cult Fanatic (MM), upgraded
* **Changes:**

  * +40 HP
  * Multiattack (2 attacks)
  * Psychic Lash (Recharge 5–6): 3d10 psychic, Wis save half
* **Role:** Battlefield control, commands with hand signals

**4 Vowbound Cultists**

* **Base Stat Block:** Thugs (MM)
* **Changes:**

  * Cannot speak
  * Advantage on saves vs charm/frighten
  * On death: psychic backlash (5 ft radius, Wis save or 1d8 psychic)
* **Weapons:** Hooked blades, nets

**1 Dream-Touched Brute**

* **Base Stat Block:** Berserker (MM)
* **Changes:**

  * Resistance to psychic
  * Frenzied Mind: advantage on first attack each round
  * Emits whispering hallucinations (disadvantage on first Wis save per round within 10 ft)

---

### **Tactics**

* Cultists try to **grapple and restrain**, not kill
* Brute pushes PCs toward the edge
* Handler targets the kalashtar aggressively
* If the Handler dies, remaining cultists retreat *without hesitation*

They do not recover bodies.

## 3. Getting the Train Moving Again

Once combat ends, the party is stranded—but not helpless.

### **Option A: Physical Repair**

* Strength or Athletics checks (DC 14–16)
* Requires at least 2 PCs
* Takes ~20 minutes
* Leaves clear evidence someone sabotaged the coupling

### **Option B: Arcane Intervention**

* Spellcasting (Mending, Fabricate, creative use)
* Arcana check lowers DC significantly
* The lightning elemental inside the rail reacts *uneasily*

### **Option C: Orien Failsafe**

* A hidden House Orien rune can be reactivated
* Investigation or Arcana (DC 15)
* Restarts movement at reduced speed

### **Outcome**

The train resumes—but:

* It’s late
* House Orien will have questions
* Someone *wanted* them isolated

And Sereth Vahl will already be reporting.
