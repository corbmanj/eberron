# Session 1: The Iron Whisper

## Seal of Waking — Act I: Sharn to Korranberg

*A session for 4 level-9 characters. Estimated runtime: 3 hours.*

---

## Session Overview

The party boards the Lightning Rail from Sharn bound for Korranberg — the first leg of their journey to Aerenal and the Seal of Waking. Along the way, a cult spy probes their defenses, and a brutal ambush on the mountainside rails reveals the Cult of the Severed Word is done watching.

### Session Flow

| Beat | Type | Est. Time | Description |
|------|------|-----------|-------------|
| 1 | Roleplay | 20 min | Departure from Sharn, boarding the Lightning Rail |
| 2 | Roleplay/Investigation | 40 min | Sereth Vahl — the cult spy |
| 3 | Transition | 10 min | Starilaskur layover, Tam's dream |
| 4 | Combat | 60 min | Broken coupling ambush in the Seawall Mountains |
| 5 | Problem-Solving | 20 min | Getting the train moving again |
| 6 | Roleplay | 30 min | Arrival in Korranberg, meeting Captain Nor |

## Beat 1: Departure from Sharn

### Setting the Scene

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> The Sharn Lightning Rail terminal is actually built from the shell of an old cathedral. Spires of iron and arcanite rise up nearly 100 feet above you. As you step out onto the platform, you can feel the arcane elemental energy vibrating through the ground, giving an unnerving feeling to every step. Porters in House Orien uniforms herd passengers aboard with practiced efficiency.
>
> You enter through the coach car. You can smell that the tables have recently been polished wood but there is still the scent of stale coffee and cigarette smoke from centuries of travel. Rows of worn cushioned benches face forward beneath arched windows. A dwarf conductor punches your tickets without looking up. "Tickets, have your tickets out"
>
> "Starilaskur, then Korranberg. Two days, one transfer. Keep your weapons stowed and your spells to yourself."
>
> A roar and a whoosh fill the cabin and several children jump as the elemental surges. Out the windows you can see the towers of Sharn start to drop away.

### DM Notes

- **Rudy** stayed behind in Sharn (if the party asks).
- The party is heading to Korranberg based on Gendrick's briefing: the Seal of Waking is accessible through a manifest zone in **Shae Mordai** on the island continent of **Aerenal**.
- They know they need to get to Aerenal. They don't yet know exactly how.
- Let the party settle in. Ask what each character is doing on the train. This creates natural hooks for Sereth Vahl's approach.

## Beat 2: The Spy — Sereth Vahl

*Social encounter. Investigation/Insight checks.*

### NPC Profile: Sereth Vahl

| Detail | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Cover** | Zil scholar traveling to Korranberg to study at the Library of Korranberg |
| **Truth** | Probationary observer for the Cult of the Severed Word |
| **Race** | Human or half-elf, early 30s |
| **Appearance** | Well-kept travel clothes, ink-stained fingers, leather journal |
| **Mannerisms** | Mumbles to herself between comments. Avoids eye contact — stares at her journal or her hands. Seems nervous, shy. Asks questions like she's genuinely curious but socially awkward about it. Never lies outright — selectively withholds truth. The scholar act is convincing because she's *actually* nervous — she's terrified of failing this mission. |
| **Motivation** | This mission is her *test*. She has not yet taken the Vow of Silence (cutting out her tongue). Success earns her the knife. |

### How She Approaches

Sereth is already seated nearby when the party boards — hunched over her journal, scribbling. She glances up when they sit down, then quickly looks away.

> **Sereth:** *(half-mumbled, not quite making eye contact)* "Sorry — is this — I can move if you need the bench."

She warms up slowly, engaging **one PC at a time**, never the whole group. Each question comes out like she's embarrassed to be asking:

| Target | Her Approach |
|--------|-------------|
| **Tam** | Asks about his studies. Comments on an "unusual aura" she claims to sense. Watches his reaction to the word "dream." |
| **Ozzy** | Compliments the Dreamrender Gauntlet *specifically* — "What fascinating craftsmanship. Kalashtar-make, isn't it?" |
| **Finella** | Asks about Valenar military traditions. "Are the tales of the Valenar ancestors true? That they literally *ride alongside you*?" |
| **Aerin** | Notices something "different" about them. "You carry yourself like someone who's been more than one person." (She doesn't know Aerin is a changeling — this is a fishing comment.) |

### What She Wants to Learn

She is gathering *confirmation*, not discovery. The cult already suspects the party.

- Are they heading to Aerenal, and do they know why?
- Are they aware of **Chatechesma** by name?
- How protected are they, mentally and spiritually?
- Which party member seems most vulnerable or emotionally reactive?

### What the Party Can Learn

| Method | DC | Revelation |
|--------|-----|------------|
| Insight | 12 | She's asking leading questions — she already knows some answers |
| Insight | 14 | She knows what a quori is. She flinches when Dal Quor is mentioned directly |
| Insight | 16 | Her questions aren't academic — she's *reporting* on them |
| Perception | 15 | She has a ritual scar **under her tongue** — partially healed, unfinished |
| Arcana | 14 | Her journal contains symbols associated with aberrant cults |

### If Magically Compelled or Tricked (Deception DC 17)

She reveals:
- The cult reveres a being called **"The Sleepless Word"**
- Silence is holy; speech is a weakness
- *"Soon, none of us will dream without permission"*
- She was told to watch for "the ones who broke the dreaming door"

### Her Departure

Sereth leaves the train at **Starilaskur** during the layover. She gives the party a polite smile and a small wave.

They will not see her again. But her report is already traveling faster than the rail.

> **DM Note:** Sereth is *not* meant to be caught or confronted violently. She's a mood-setter. If the party does confront her, she breaks down — she's terrified of the cult but more terrified of failing the test. She knows almost nothing actionable. The point is paranoia.

## Beat 3: Starilaskur Layover & Tam's Dream

### The Layover

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> You pull into Starilaskur around midday. It's loud and dusty — the transfer platform is open-air, squeezed between two rail lines. House Orien clerks are shouting departure times over each other. There's a food vendor near the exit selling something out of a cart. He keeps calling it "meat" but won't get more specific than that.
>
> You've got about 12 hours before the Korranberg connection.

- Nothing plot-critical happens here. Let the party shop, rest, or explore briefly.
- Sereth departs. If the party is watching, she exits quickly and vanishes into the crowd.

### Tam's Dream (Long Rest)

When the party sleeps (either at the Starilaskur inn or back on the rail):

> **Read Aloud (to Tam's player only, or whispered):**
>
> You're in Dal Quor again. You recognize it immediately. The colors are off — everything is too bright, too saturated, like someone turned the contrast up on the world.
>
> Fulla is there. She's far away, standing in front of the door. There are tendrils of light wrapped around her arms. She doesn't seem to know you're here.
>
> Your scar starts to burn.
>
> You hear a voice. It's not Fulla's. Not Echo's. Something much older.
>
> *"The dawn is not what it seems."*
>
> You wake up. Your hand is on your scar. It's warm to the touch.

- **No mechanical effect** this time. Just atmosphere.
- If Tam tells the party, great. If not, that's fine too.
- This is the first of recurring dream visions during this arc.

## Beat 4: The Broken Coupling

*Combat encounter. Medium-Hard difficulty for 4 level-9 PCs.*

### Setting the Ambush

The second leg of the journey runs from Starilaskur south through the **Seawall Mountains** toward Korranberg. Near dusk on the first day:

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> You're winding through the foothills of the Seawall Mountains now. The sun is getting low, sitting right on the ridgeline, and the shadows from the scrub pines are getting long across the rocks.
>
> Then the train makes a sound you've never heard before. Metal on metal, shrieking. The whole car lurches hard to one side. Bags fall out of the overhead racks. A window cracks. You can feel the floor tilt. **Everyone make a DEX save.**
>
> *(DC 12 — on a fail, you get thrown into the bench or overhead rack. 1d6 bludgeoning damage.)*
>
> Through the front window, you can see the engine car pulling away from you — the coupling between your car and the engine is dragging on the rail, throwing sparks. It snaps free.
>
> Your car grinds to a stop. Everything goes quiet except for the wind and the smell of hot iron.
>
> Then two of the passengers near the front of the car stand up. They're pulling hoods over their faces and drawing hooked blades from under their cloaks. One of them is holding the severed end of a cable — they cut the coupling from inside.
>
> At the same time, you hear boots on the roof. Something heavy lands up there. And through the smashed windows on the south side, two more hooded figures are climbing in.
>
> From outside, maybe 30 feet down in the rocks, a masked figure in heavier armor raises a hand and makes a sharp gesture. No words. Just signals.

## Beat 5: Getting Moving Again

*Problem-solving. Multiple solutions.*

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> The train car is just sitting on the rail now, tilted slightly downhill. You take a look at the coupling — it's a mangled mess of iron and arcanite, but it's pretty obvious this was cut, not broken. If you put your hand on the floor, you can feel the faintest hum. The bound elemental is still in there somewhere.

### Option A: Physical Repair

- **Requirements:** 2+ PCs working together
- **Checks:** Athletics or Smith's Tools DC 15 (advantage if 3+ PCs help)
- **Time:** 20 minutes
- **Result:** Coupling is jury-rigged. Train moves at half speed.

### Option B: Arcane Intervention

- **Requirements:** Mending (for the coupling) + Arcana DC 13 (to coax the elemental)
- **Alternative:** Fabricate solves it instantly
- **Result:** Full speed restored but the elemental is *agitated* — occasional sparks and lurches

### Option C: House Orien Failsafe

- **Discovery:** Investigation DC 15 (or Perception DC 17) finds a hidden rune panel under the conductor's seat
- **Activation:** Arcana DC 13 to restart the elemental binding
- **Result:** Train moves at reduced speed. House Orien will send investigators at Korranberg.

> **DM Note:** Any solution works. The point is to make the players feel resourceful, not stuck. If they're struggling, have a surviving passenger (a nervous Orien clerk) hint at the failsafe.

## Beat 6: Korranberg & Captain Nor

### Arrival

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> You pull into Korranberg around dawn. It looks like someone took a library and built a fishing village around it. Squat stone buildings line narrow streets and the whole place smells like salt air and old paper. The Library of Korranberg sits up on the hill above everything — big copper dome, green with age. You can't miss it.
>
> The rail terminal here is small. A few gnomes with clipboards are already documenting your late arrival, writing down everything with a level of detail that feels unnecessary. A House Orien official in a coat that's a size too big takes one look at the damaged coupling and starts scribbling furiously.
>
> You're in Zilargo now. Everyone here is smarter than they look.

### Finding a Guide

The party needs passage to **Aerenal** — specifically the port city of **Shae Caerdal**. No major shipping lines run direct routes. They need a private captain.

**How they find Nor** (pick one or let it emerge organically):
- **Ask at the docks:** DC 10 Investigation. A dock worker laughs: "You want *Nor*. Slip 7. Don't say I didn't warn you."
- **Ask at a tavern (The Salty Index):** The bartender says: "Only one captain crazy enough to run to Aerenal this time of year. He's at Slip 7. Probably."
- **House Orien clerk:** "If you're heading to Aerenal, there's a... *captain*... at Slip 7. He owes us money, so technically he's available."

### Captain Noreaul — "Nor"

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> You make your way down to the docks. Slip 7 is all the way at the far end — you walk past the nice boats, then past the not-so-nice boats, then past a boat that looks like it might be on fire but is actually just painted that way. And then you get to *The Fortunate Lapse*.
>
> It's a two-masted sailing ship. You can tell it was probably nice at some point. The sails are patched. The hull is patched. There's a chicken sitting on the bowsprit.
>
> Standing on the dock next to it, apparently in the middle of an argument with the chicken, is a short guy in a captain's coat. Or what used to be a captain's coat.

### NPC Profile: Captain Noreaul ("Nor")

| Detail | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Race** | Half-elf, half-gnome (changes the subject *immediately* if anyone asks about his parents) |
| **Age** | Indeterminate. Could be 30. Could be 55. His face disagrees with itself. |
| **Height** | 5'2" — split the difference |
| **Appearance** | Sun-weathered skin, salt-stiff hair that defies gravity, a coat that was once a captain's coat and is now a concept. One gold tooth. Boots that don't match. |
| **Personality** | Relentlessly confident despite all evidence. Tells stories that can't possibly be true (they are). Genuinely skilled sailor buried under layers of chaos. Changes the subject when cornered. Heart of gold wrapped in a disaster. |
| **Voice** | Quick, warm, slightly too loud. Starts sentences in the middle. |

### Roleplaying Nor

**Key Traits:**
- He calls everyone "friend" or "chief" or "boss" regardless of context
- He never directly answers a question about his competence
- He has a solution for everything — it's just never the *right* solution at first
- He is somehow always eating something

**Sample Dialogue:**

> *When asked if he can sail to Aerenal:*
> "Aerenal? Oh sure, sure, I've been — well, *near* Aerenal. Close enough to see it. Beautiful place. The elves, they don't love visitors, but they *love* a good entrance, and friend, I know how to make an entrance."

> *When asked about his ship:*
> "She's solid. *Solid.* That's not a leak, that's ventilation. Very modern."

> *When asked about his parents:*
> "So anyway, the thing about the Gulf of the Moon is—"

> *When asked his price:*
> "Tell you what. You seem like good people. Good people on an important mission. I can see it in your eyes. Also someone at the dock told me. 200 gold and you help with the sails."

### Negotiation

| Price | Condition |
|-------|-----------|
| **200 gp** | Base price. Party helps with sailing. |
| **150 gp** | DC 14 Persuasion, or if they mention danger (Nor *loves* danger, conceptually) |
| **100 gp** | DC 17 Persuasion, or they offer to fix something on the ship (which there is *always* something to fix) |
| **Free** | If they somehow convince him this trip will make him famous |

### Departure Setup

Once the deal is struck:

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> Captain Nor claps his hands together. Crumbs fly off them — you're not sure when he started eating.
>
> "Right! Aerenal! Shae Caerdal! We leave at dawn. Well — near dawn. Dawn-ish. Bring your own breakfast."
>
> He turns to the chicken on the bowsprit.
>
> "You hear that, Reginald? We're going to Aerenal!"
>
> The chicken doesn't react.

> **DM Note:** This is the end of Session 1. The party should feel the cult breathing down their neck, but also some levity with Nor. The tonal contrast is intentional — it mirrors the arc's journey from paranoia to wonder as they approach Irian.
