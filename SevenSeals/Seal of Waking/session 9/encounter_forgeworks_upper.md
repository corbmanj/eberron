# Encounter: The Upper Forgeworks

---

### Encounter Summary

```
Difficulty:  Hard
Party:       4 players, level 10

Monster Roster:
  4× Cultist of the Severed Tongue — CR 2
  1× Cult Inquisitor — CR 7

The Inquisitor's Silent Ward makes positioning matter: casters within 10 ft cannot
use verbal components. The cultists' directive is to push casters back into range.
This encounter rewards parties who split movement and punish parties who cluster.
The iron grating in the southeast floor is a one-way door to the lower level — a
creature that falls or drops through it cannot easily return and will see the ritual
in progress below. Use this to create urgency.
```

---

**Traits:**

**Severed Vow.** The Inquisitor cannot speak and is immune to any effect requiring it to hear or produce a verbal component.

**Silent Ward.** The Inquisitor radiates a 10-ft radius zone of magical silence at all times. Creatures within the radius cannot speak or cast spells with verbal components. The Inquisitor can suppress this as a bonus action and reactivate it the same way.

**Glyph-Brand.** When the Inquisitor hits a creature with an attack, that creature must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or be silenced until the start of their next turn. When the silence ends, whether from the save or from the duration expiring, the creature takes 7 (2d6) necrotic damage as the glyph burns out.

---

**Actions:**

**Multiattack.** The Inquisitor makes two Ritual Blade attacks.

**Ritual Blade.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 9 (2d6+2) piercing + 9 (2d8) necrotic damage.

**Glyph Pulse (Recharge 5–6).** Each creature within 20 ft must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or take 21 (6d6) necrotic damage and be silenced until the end of their next turn. Half damage and not silenced on a success. *(Spell-like — not counterspellable.)*

---

**Spells:** *(Spell save DC 15, Spell attack +7)*

| Spell | Level | Uses | Counterspellable |
|-------|-------|------|-----------------|
| *Silence* | 2nd | 2/day | ✓ |
| *Bestow Curse* | 3rd | 2/day | ✓ |
| *Blight* | 4th | 1/day | ✓ |

*Silence: Cast on a caster who has moved beyond the Silent Ward's radius. The Inquisitor does not waste a slot if that caster is already within the Ward.*

---

**Tactics:**
- Opens in the center of the room with Silent Ward active. Does not move unless the party forces it.
- Cultists are directed (before the fight, by arrangement) to use Shove actions to push casters back within 10 ft of the Inquisitor. They are functioning as positioning tools, not just damage dealers.
- Ritual Blade targets any caster in reach. The glyph-brand silence + burn damage is more damaging to a spellcaster than a martial.
- Glyph Pulse when 3 or more party members cluster within 20 ft.
- Bestow Curse on the highest-damage martial: advantage on spell attacks against them (chosen effect), or impose disadvantage on their attack rolls.
- Suppresses Silent Ward as a bonus action if a cultist ally needs to position themselves and the party is cornered — then reactivates it next bonus action.
- Does not retreat. Fights until destroyed.

---

**Loot:** A sealed letter (gear-and-flame wax seal — the Inquisitor's operational orders, which mention "the chamber below" and "delivery before moonrise"). A cult cipher key. Iron glyph-tools and ritual components. Roll DMG Individual Treasure (CR 5–10).

## Battle Map Description

> This map must be rendered in a strict top-down, directly overhead bird's-eye view. The camera is directly above looking straight down, as if the map were a floor plan. Not isometric. Not perspective. Not a three-quarter view. Directly overhead.

**Map size:** 80×60 ft. The main working floor of a decommissioned House Cannith forgeworks — cold, dark, repurposed. Stone floors over iron supports. Ceiling height 20 ft.

**Terrain:**

- **Furnaces (4 total):** Two pairs, one on the east wall and one on the west wall. Each furnace unit is roughly 15×10 ft and 8 ft tall — solid stone construction, full cover on all sides. The narrow passages between furnace and wall are tight (5 ft wide) and slightly warmer than the rest of the room, but not dangerous.

- **Cooling canal:** A 10-ft-wide, 5-ft-deep channel running north-south through the center of the room, dividing it into east and west halves. Half-filled with murky standing water. Difficult terrain to wade through. DC 12 Athletics to climb out without assistance. A simple wooden plank bridge spans it near the north wall — 5 ft wide, no railing.

- **Two catwalks:** Iron lattice walkways 10 ft above the floor. One runs along the north wall; one runs along the south wall. A third central catwalk crosses east-west over the cooling canal, connecting the two. No railings anywhere. Half cover for a prone creature. Each corner of the room has a ladder (iron rungs set in the wall) for access. The catwalks creak but are structurally sound.

- **Hanging chains (12 total):** Heavy chains descend from ceiling pulleys at irregular intervals across the room, ranging from 6 to 18 ft in length. Each ends in an iron hook. A creature can grab a chain and swing (DC 12 Athletics check to maintain grip through the swing; failure means falling). The chains can also be used to climb (DC 14 Athletics), anchor ropes, or — if cut — drop 40–80 lbs of iron chain on a 5×5 ft area below (DC 13 DEX save, 2d6 bludgeoning on a failure).

- **Iron grating (southeast section):** A 10×10 ft section of the floor near the southeast corner is iron grating over the lower level — 15 ft below. The pale blue light of the ritual pulses up through it. A creature that drops through falls 15 ft (unless they grab the edge: DC 14 Athletics). Climbing back up from below requires a DC 15 Athletics check or a rope. The lower level can be heard and faintly seen through the grating at all times. The party can see figures down there — three, motionless, and others moving.

- **Lighting:** No windows. Cogs-standard gas lamps on the walls, three of them, providing dim light across most of the room with pockets of darkness near the furnace alcoves. The grating in the southeast casts a faint blue-white light upward, creating an eerie visible patch in the southeast corner.

**Starting positions:**
- Two cultists on the main floor, west side, flanking the western furnace pair.
- Two cultists on the catwalks — one north wall, one central crossing (above the canal).
- One Cult Inquisitor, center of main floor, approximately 10 ft west of the canal. Silent Ward active.

The Inquisitor knew they were coming. The cultists on the catwalks were positioned an hour ago.

> Style: industrial and detailed — cold iron, dark stone, old Cannith engineering. Strict top-down, no perspective, no isometric angle.
