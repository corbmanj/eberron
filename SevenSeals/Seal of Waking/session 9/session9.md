# Session 9: What the City Keeps

## Seal of Madness — Act I: The Breaking

*A session for 4 level-10 characters. Estimated runtime: 3.5–4 hours.*

---

## Session Overview

Sharn has three missing citizens and three fresh craters. The party just survived an ambush in the Eldeen Reaches and came back with nothing — no manifest zone, no answers, and the growing certainty that they were removed from the city on purpose. They were. While they were gone, the ritual moved forward. The investigation is theirs to run — the leads are loose, the Watch is not what it appears, and every thread eventually points down into the Cogs.

---

> **DM Note — Session 8 Recap:** The party debriefed with Gendrick on their return from Irian, delivered the Seal of Waking, received the address of The Tallow & Lens. They descended to the Fallen District, made a deal with the proprietor Davath (a Lords of Dust rakshasa, though they don't know this), delivered a sealed box to Councilor Aldran, and by morning found out what was in it. Aldran is dead. The Watch wants witnesses. The party has coordinates to what Davath told them was a Xoriat manifest zone in the Eldeen Reaches. The coordinates are a trap.

## Beat 0: The Coordinates

*This beat covers what happens when the party follows Davath's instructions to the Eldeen Reaches. Run it at the start of session 9, or — if session 8 ended early enough — as its final scene.*

The party travels to the Eldeen Reaches. Sixty miles east of the Towering Wood, per the envelope. Three hours around midnight, per the note. The journey takes the better part of two days. They arrive at a forest path that leads to a clearing.

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> The path narrows as you go deeper into the wood, the trees growing older and taller until the canopy closes above and the moonlight disappears entirely. The air is cold and still. No wind in the branches. No animal sounds.
>
> The path opens into a clearing — wider than you expected, a rough circle of packed earth and dead leaves with a standing stone at the center. Old. Pre-Galifar. The runes on it are Gatekeeper work.
>
> There is a figure at the stone. An old human in traveler's clothes, crouching, running a hand over the runes. They look up when you enter the clearing.
>
> "Strange night to be out here," they say. "I've been monitoring this site for three weeks. The wards have been behaving oddly." They straighten. "Are you here about the disturbance too?"

The figure is The Watcher. It has been in position for six hours.

> **DM Note:** The Watcher's disguise is a Gatekeeper druid named Adric who the party has never met. DC 20 Insight to sense something is off about this person before the ambush begins. DC 18 Perception (passive) to spot the cultists in the trees or on the ground at the clearing's edge. If no one makes either check, run a surprise round.
>
> The "manifest zone" doesn't exist. There is nothing at this location except the wardstone and the ambush. The party will realize this, if they haven't already, the moment the fight begins.

### After the Fight

The clearing is quiet. The Watcher is down. The Gatekeeper wardstone at the center is exactly what it appeared to be: old stone, dead wards, no planar resonance of any kind.

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> The clearing is still. The trees around the edges hold the shape of the fight for a moment — scorch marks, blood on the leaves, the standing stone unmarked and indifferent.
>
> There is no manifest zone here. There never was. Whatever Davath gave you, it wasn't what he said it was.

The party can examine The Watcher's body and find the journal and sending stone (see encounter file for details). If they spend time searching, they can confirm with an Arcana check (DC 12) that there is no planar resonance at this location — not Xoriat, not anything.

The journey back to Sharn takes two days. During that time, the explosions in the city happen and the three captives are taken. The party arrives to find the crisis already in progress.

## Beat 1: Return to Sharn

The party arrives in Sharn having survived an ambush they weren't prepared for, carrying a dead shapeshifter's journal, and with nothing to show for the last four days of travel. The city greets them with smoke.

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> Sharn comes into view on the horizon the way it always does — too tall, catching the light before anything else. But something is wrong with the skyline today. Two columns of smoke, both rising from the lower districts, already thinning to gray.
>
> The first news crier you pass doesn't stop. You catch pieces: *"—three prominent citizens reported missing — explosion in the Korranath precinct — harbor district dockmaster's office destroyed — Watch requesting witnesses—"*
>
> You were gone four days. Something moved while you were away.

The party can learn from any innkeeper, dock worker, or street conversation:

- Three prominent Sharn citizens vanished while the party was in the Eldeen Reaches: **Savia Patellus** (a lawyer), **Bixum Worthwistle** (a gnome council member), **Umberto Gromwarden** (a bugbear historian).
- Two explosions during their absence: the Korranath Watch precinct (records damaged, two constables shaken but uninjured) and the harbor district dockmaster's office (filing systems destroyed).
- The Aldran assassination from before they left is being discussed in the same breath. The Watch is treating all four as connected.

> **DM Note:** The party may notice, with a DC 13 Intelligence check or just good memory, that the dockmaster's office processed import manifests for the Droaam cargo route — including the shipment they stopped in the early campaign. Someone needed those records gone before anyone started cross-referencing blast sites with old shipping logs.

## Beat 2: Gendrick's Alarm

Gendrick did not send for them. But if they come to him — and they should, because he is the one person in Sharn who might understand what the pattern means — he is already in his study with three books open and a sending stone cooling on the desk.

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> The door opens before you knock. Gendrick's study looks the same as always except for the three books spread open on the center table, each weighted at the corners with different objects. He isn't looking at the books. He's looking at the sending stone in his hand. It's dark.
>
> "I was just trying to reach Umberto Gromwarden," he says. He sets the stone down carefully. "That's the third time."

---

### What Gendrick Knows

Gendrick has been awake most of the night. He has assembled the following:

**The missing persons are connected by what they know, not by who they are.**

- **Savia Patellus** has spent eleven years defending people in Sharn who should not be defensible. She has never been threatened off a case, never been bought out, never lost a client to retribution. In this city, that doesn't happen by accident. It happens because you know things that make you untouchable. Gendrick doesn't know what she knows. That's the point.

> **Gendrick:** "Savia Patellus has been practicing in Sharn longer than most of my contacts have been alive. She's still here. That tells you something about what she's holding."

> **DM Note — Savia's secret:** Her most consequential file, which was emptied from her private cabinet before she was taken: proof that Breland's current Minister of Intelligence orchestrated a false-flag attack during the Last War — a Brelish special unit destroyed a Cyran refugee convoy and pinned it on Karrnath. The incident kept Breland in the war for three additional years. If it becomes public, it ends the current government and possibly restarts the war. Nobody except Savia knew the contents of that cabinet. The party can learn this only from Savia herself, in session 10.

- **Bixum Worthwistle** sits on the City Council and has access to state-level information the party does not have clearance for. Gendrick says carefully: *"What Bixum knows, and what makes him valuable to whoever took him, I can only speculate. He's been on the Five Nations Unity Committee for years. If there were plans being made — quiet ones, about the shape of Khorvaire after the current governments — Bixum would know them."*

> **DM Note:** The specific secret Bixum holds: he knows of a direct living heir to King Galifar, hidden away under a new identity until the political moment is right to claim the throne and reunite the Five Nations. Gendrick suspects this but does not know it for certain — he knows Bixum is more significant than his profile suggests. You can reveal the specifics if the party finds Bixum alive in session 10.

- **Umberto Gromwarden** is a historian who has spent twenty years researching the history of Kas. His most recent work argued there were systematic gaps in the historical record of Kas's service under Vecna — specific periods where Vecna's own chroniclers appear to have edited the accounts.

> **Gendrick:** "Umberto published a paper fourteen months ago arguing those gaps were deliberate. I read it when it came out. I thought it was academic overreach." A pause. "I'm no longer sure."

**The planar disturbance:**

For the past three days, Gendrick has been tracking a low-frequency Mabaran resonance from somewhere in the Cogs. Not from a manifest zone. From inside the city's foundation layer.

> **Gendrick:** "Mabar doesn't resonate like that without a reason. Something is being prepared there. Or opened."

He does not have an address. He has a direction and a depth. The Cogs district. Somewhere near the wall.

> **DM Note:** Gendrick is not performing worry. He is genuinely alarmed — and this is the first time in a long while that he doesn't know what's coming next. Play him focused and efficient, not panicked, but with an edge. The party should feel his urgency even if they don't share it yet.

---

### Starting Points

If the party hasn't started investigating, Gendrick names four entry points:

> **Gendrick:** "The blast sites — the Watch has cordoned them, but that doesn't mean you can't look. The missing persons — someone saw something. The Watch itself — the response has been slower than it should be, and I'd like to know why. And Umberto's office, if it hasn't been searched already. He was careful about his work. He kept copies."

## Beat 3: The Investigation

The party has four leads. They can pursue any of them in any order. Each yields partial information. Finding the forgeworks requires either getting the address from Lieutenant Desh (Leads A or C) or combining the modified Cannith mark (Lead B) with the Cogs district (Lead D).

> **DM Note — Running This Section:** Don't funnel. If the party skips a lead entirely, they miss that information — but multiple paths reach the forgeworks. Lead C is the most direct route to the address. Lead B has the richest human detail. Lead A confirms the explosives connection. Lead D adds planar context. Skipping any one of these is fine; skipping all four is not possible if they're trying to find the captives.

---

### Lead A: The Blast Sites

Three sites: the Korranath Watch precinct (middle Sharn), the harbor district dockmaster's office (lower-mid), and the Tavick's Landing District legal records office (discovered if the party looks for a pattern — the third blast is less publicized because it was smaller and caused no injuries).

The Watch has cordoned all three. Getting past requires a DC 14 Persuasion check (legitimate investigative purpose), DC 16 Deception (false credentials), or creative routing.

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> The Watch cordon is a rope across the street and two constables who look like they haven't slept. Past them, the building is intact on the outside but wrong — a wall leaning that shouldn't lean, windows that are both shattered and present at the same time, the smell of something that isn't fire but burned.

**What investigation reveals:**

- **DC 12 Investigation** (any site): The blast pattern is inward, contained, and precise. Each was placed and triggered remotely. Someone knew the interior layouts.
- **DC 12 Investigation** (any site, different check from the blast pattern): In the debris, a fragment of scorched crate wood — heavy-gauge, iron-banded, with a stenciled mark still legible on one face. Any party member who was present for the warehouse mission in the early campaign recognizes it immediately: this is the same symbol that was on the crates headed for Sharn. The explosives didn't all get stopped. Some of them made it.
- **DC 15 Perception** (harbor site only): Behind a collapsed shelf, a torn square of dark cloth. A symbol pressed into it: a circle of stitching, a tongue partially severed. The Severed Tongue. The cloth is recent.

**Lieutenant Karra Desh:**

At the harbor site, a Watch lieutenant is working the scene quietly, separate from the official team. She's in lieutenant's colors but her badge is turned face-in. She has been running a parallel investigation for six weeks, without a desk, without backup, without authorization.

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> One of the Watch officers near the south wall is working differently from the others — not cataloguing, not supervising, but crouching near the floor with a small lens and writing in a notebook that isn't an official Watch ledger. When she notices you watching her, she straightens. She doesn't call for backup.
>
> "You're not here because the constable at the cordon let you in," she says. "So you're either good at talking to people or you didn't come through the cordon." A pause. "Lieutenant Desh. I've been on this case for six weeks without a desk to sit at. If you have anything useful, I'd rather talk here."

Desh knows:
- The Cult of the Severed Tongue has been active in Sharn's lower districts for at least a year. She filed two reports. Both were buried.
- Six weeks ago, she found a cache of the same explosive material in an abandoned Cogs building — crates with the Droaam shipping mark, still sealed. The deed for that building traces to a decommissioned House Cannith forgeworks three blocks away.
- She has the address: **Old Forge Row, Blackarch Level, the Cogs. Building C.**
- She hasn't gone in alone. She's not stupid.

> **DM Note:** Desh is not asking to come with them. She trusts the party more than her own precinct at this point, and she knows a cult in a forgeworks is not a Watch situation. She'll share everything she has. She wants the captives found. She'll ask them to tell her what they find — she wants to close her case through proper channels if anything survives to be closed.

### Lead B: The Missing

Each of the three captives was last seen somewhere, by someone.

---

**Savia Patellus — Her Law Office, Highest Towers District**

Savia's clerk, **Nessa** (half-elf, thirties, hasn't left the office since it happened), was present when Savia was taken.

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> The office is still unlocked. A half-elf woman sits at the front desk, both hands around a mug that stopped steaming some time ago. She looks up with the expression of someone who has decided to stay because leaving would mean accepting that something happened.

Nessa tells the party:
- Savia was working late — not unusual. Around the second bell of evening, a carriage arrived. Savia went down herself. She didn't take her coat.
- The carriage: dark lacquered wood. A mark on the door — a gear-and-flame, but modified, a second line beneath the flame that didn't belong on any official Cannith device.
- Two figures inside, neither speaking. Savia looked up at the window once before she got in.

> **DM Note:** If the party builds any trust with Nessa (DC 12 Persuasion or just being decent to her), she mentions: "Savia had a file she called the 'insurance cabinet.' She locked it herself every night before she left. Last night she didn't." The cabinet is in Savia's private office — it's been opened and emptied. Whatever was in it is gone.

---

**Bixum Worthwistle — City Council Administrative Wing**

Bixum's aide, **Trix** (goblin, young, professionally alarmed, is still at his desk because he doesn't know what else to do), will talk.

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> The council wing smells like wood polish and mildew. A goblin in clerk's clothing is sitting very straight at a desk twice his height with the expression of someone maintaining composure through sheer force of professional obligation. He says, before you reach him: "I know why you're here. I've been expecting someone."

Trix tells the party:
- Bixum received a sealed letter at yesterday's council session. The wax seal was a gear-and-flame device — the same modified version.
- Bixum read it, pocketed it, and said: *"I'll be back by morning."* He's said that before. He was always back by morning.
- Trix kept the envelope. The inside has a faint pressed impression from the letter: **OLD FORGE.**

---

**Umberto Gromwarden — The Library District**

Umberto's apartment has already been searched by the Watch and left in the state they found it: ransacked. His colleague, **Professor Arwick** (human historian, sixties, mildly paranoid, was Umberto's closest collaborator), can be found at the Morgrave extension library.

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> The door has been replaced on its hinges but not locked. Inside, bookshelves have been pulled apart and restacked badly. Papers on the floor. The desk cleared. Whatever they were looking for, they found it — or they left in a hurry.

Arwick tells the party:
- Umberto had been receiving threatening correspondence for eight months, telling him to stop his research on the gaps in the Kas historical record. He didn't stop.
- Three weeks ago, Umberto told Arwick: *"I think I know why those records were edited. I think whoever did it is still doing it."*
- Umberto mentioned putting a copy of his most sensitive notes somewhere safe — with *"a colleague in the Cogs."* Arwick doesn't know who.

On the floor of Umberto's apartment, half-hidden under an overturned chair, is a torn page from a notebook:

---

> *"...the second account contradicts the first in one specific detail: the Eye was not lost in battle. It was given. The entry is edited — the original is visible if you hold the parchment at an angle — but the name of the recipient has been removed. Who was given the Eye of Vecna willingly? And what did Vecna receive in exchange that was worth that price—"*

---

> **DM Note:** This is a breadcrumb. The party doesn't need to understand it now. It will land harder later when Gendrick's sacrifice happens, if they've been holding this question since session 9.

### Lead C: The Watch

The official investigation runs through the Warden Bridge precinct and **Constable Farris** — competent, well-organized, and three years into a comfortable arrangement with the Boromar Clan. His family is safe. His overtime is paid. He is helpful in a way that produces nothing.

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> The precinct is busy in the way precincts get busy when something has happened that nobody wants to be in charge of. Constable Farris meets you in the main room instead of his office. He has the posture of someone who has learned that standing keeps meetings short.
>
> "I appreciate you coming in. We're treating all three disappearances as connected. We have leads. We're working them." He says this with the confidence of someone who does not expect to be asked what the leads are.

If the party presses, Farris produces procedural language. The explosions appear connected to a known criminal element in the lower districts. He suggests checking back in a day or two. He does not mention the Severed Tongue. He does not mention the Cogs.

**Route around Farris:**

Any junior staff member in the precinct will do, but the most useful is **Constable Pol** — one year on the job, clearly unhappy with how the investigation is being run. DC 12 Insight to notice. DC 13 Persuasion to get him somewhere private.

Pol tells them:
- Lieutenant Desh has been running her own parallel case. She's not at the precinct much anymore because Farris keeps reassigning her calls.
- She'll be at the harbor blast site this morning.

> **DM Note:** If the party already found Desh at the blast site, they don't need this path. If they came here first, Pol is the bridge. Either way, Desh is the most direct route to the address.

### Lead D: Gendrick (Return Visit)

This lead activates if the party returns to Gendrick mid-investigation with new information. He can't give them the address, but he can narrow the picture.

**If they share the modified Cannith mark:**

> **Gendrick:** "A modified gear-and-flame device on a carriage in Sharn. Those were used on secondary Cannith facilities — overflow forge sites licensed to sub-contractors during the Last War, mostly decommissioned after. If the modification is what you're describing, it's likely one of the Old Forge Row sites. In the Cogs. Near the outer support wall."

**If they share Umberto's torn page:**

> **Gendrick:** *(reading slowly, then again)* "Umberto found something that changes the known history. If the Eye was given willingly — given to someone Vecna trusted — that means Vecna had a plan for it from the beginning. And it means Umberto knew something that Vecna does not want known." He sets the page down. "We need to find him before whatever they've taken from him is used."

**On the planar disturbance:**

If the party asks Gendrick to triangulate against location information they've found — Old Forge Row, the Cogs, the outer wall — he can confirm:

> **Gendrick:** "Yes. That's consistent with what I'm detecting. That's the place."

## Beat 4: The Upper Forgeworks

**Old Forge Row, Blackarch Level, the Cogs.**

The Cogs are the industrial underbelly of Sharn — a network of foundries, warehouses, and service tunnels that sustains everything above. Building C on Old Forge Row is indistinguishable from its neighbors: steel-frame construction, intake vents sealed from the inside, a loading door with a chain. The modified Cannith gear-and-flame is stamped above the main entrance — the same mark from the carriage, from the envelope, from the cloth fragment at the blast site.

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> The Cogs smell like hot metal and coal smoke and the mineral dampness of water running over stone for a long time. The lights down here are gas, not magical — yellow and close, heavy shadows. Old Forge Row is four buildings pressed against the outer support wall, their backs against solid rock.
>
> Building C is in the middle. The chain on the loading door has been cut and replaced with a newer lock — someone's living here. The intake vents above are sealed from inside. There's a sound coming through the walls: low, rhythmic, sustained. Not mechanical. Something else entirely.

The sound is the ritual, already in progress.

**Entry options:**

- **Loading door:** The new lock requires a DC 18 Thieves' Tools check or DC 20 Strength check to force.
- **Side ventilation shaft:** DC 15 Acrobatics to navigate without alerting the nearest cultist. Failure triggers immediate awareness.
- **Coal intake hatch (rear):** Small opening — DC 12 to fit through for Medium creatures, automatic for Small. Leads directly into the coal storage room, adjacent to the main forge floor.
