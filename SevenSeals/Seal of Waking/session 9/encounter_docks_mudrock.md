# Encounter: Low Tide

*Cliffside District — the harbor docks, leaving the Dockmaster's Office.*

---

### Encounter Summary

```
Difficulty:  Easy
Party:       4 players, level 10

Monster Roster:
  10× Mudrock Crawler — CR 1/4
   3× Mudrock Brute   — CR 1

Minor elemental creatures that form in the silt and refuse of Sharn's harbor
bottom, animated by the low-frequency elemental energy running through the
city's foundation. They don't hunt. They aggregate toward disturbance —
and the dockmaster's office explosion left a strong one. The crawlers pour
through the dock planks and up over the pilings in a low wave. The brutes
haul themselves up last. The real threat is the Brutes' shove — the water
is right there, and it's a long swim back up.
```

---

## Monster Stat Blocks

### Mudrock Crawler

*Small Elemental, unaligned*

*A minor harbor elemental formed from silt, refuse, and the ambient elemental resonance of Sharn's foundation. It doesn't think. It moves toward disturbance.*

|---|---|
| **AC** | 11 (natural armor) |
| **HP** | 13 (3d6+3) |
| **Speed** | 20 ft, Swim 30 ft |
| **STR** | 12 (+1) |
| **DEX** | 8 (–1) |
| **CON** | 12 (+1) |
| **INT** | 3 (–4) |
| **WIS** | 7 (–2) |
| **CHA** | 3 (–4) |

**Damage Vulnerabilities:** Fire
**Damage Resistances:** Bludgeoning from nonmagical attacks
**Condition Immunities:** Prone
**Senses:** Darkvision 30 ft, passive Perception 8

---

**Traits:**

**Mudborn.** The crawler moves through water and mud without impediment. It can squeeze through gaps as small as 6 inches — including the gaps between dock planks.

**Splatter.** When the crawler is reduced to 0 hit points, it bursts. Each creature within 5 ft must succeed on a DC 10 Dexterity saving throw or be blinded until the end of their next turn.

---

**Actions:**

**Mud Slam.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +3 to hit, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d6+1) bludgeoning damage.

---

**Tactics:**
- Crawlers move in a wave — they don't spread out, they pile toward the nearest creature.
- No coordination, no priority. They mob whoever is closest.
- They don't flee. When one explodes, the others don't react — they just keep moving forward.

---

**Loot:** None.

### Mudrock Brute

*Medium Elemental, unaligned*

*A larger, denser harbor elemental with a hardened stone core. Slower than the crawlers and not much smarter, but significantly harder to drop.*

| | |
|---|---|
| **AC** | 13 (natural armor) |
| **HP** | 34 (5d8+10) |
| **Speed** | 20 ft, Swim 25 ft |
| **STR** | 16 (+3) |
| **DEX** | 7 (–2) |
| **CON** | 15 (+2) |
| **INT** | 3 (–4) |
| **WIS** | 8 (–1) |
| **CHA** | 3 (–4) |

**Damage Vulnerabilities:** Fire
**Damage Resistances:** Bludgeoning from nonmagical attacks
**Condition Immunities:** Prone
**Senses:** Darkvision 30 ft, passive Perception 9

---

**Traits:**

**Mudborn.** The Brute moves through water and mud without impediment.

**Rocky Hide.** When a creature hits the Brute with a melee attack, the attacker takes 2 (1d4) piercing damage from the stone protrusions on its body.

---

**Actions:**

**Multiattack.** The Brute makes two Mud Slam attacks.

**Mud Slam.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d10+3) bludgeoning damage. On a hit, the target must succeed on a DC 13 Strength saving throw or be pushed 5 ft in any direction the Brute chooses.

---

**Tactics:**
- Brutes haul themselves up at the dock's edge at the end of round 1, after the crawlers have already engaged.
- Priority: whichever party member is closest to the water's edge. The push is the point.
- A Brute that successfully pushes someone into the harbor uses both Slam attacks on the same target next round.
- They don't retreat. They've never retreated from anything.

> **DM Note — Falling in the Harbor:** A creature pushed into Sharn's harbor falls 10 ft into dark, cold water (1d6 bludgeoning, DC 13 Athletics to stay afloat). Climbing back up a dock piling requires a DC 12 Athletics check and costs all movement for that turn. The water is not a hazard beyond that — this is flavor, not a death sentence.

---

**Loot:** None.

## Battle Map Description

> This map must be rendered in a strict top-down, directly overhead bird's-eye view. The camera is directly above looking straight down, as if the map were a floor plan. Not isometric. Not perspective. Not a three-quarter view. Directly overhead.

**Map size:** 80×60 ft. A working dock section in Sharn's Cliffside district — weathered planking, heavy use, built to last rather than to look good.

**Terrain:**

- **Main dock platform:** A wide wooden plank platform running east-west, roughly 30 ft wide, occupying the northern half of the map. Planks are heavy and close-set but not sealed — dark water is visible through the gaps in several places. The surface is damp and slightly slick (no mechanical effect unless pushed prone, in which case sliding 5 ft requires a DC 10 Acrobatics check to stop).

- **Harbor water (south edge):** The southern 20 ft of the map is open harbor — dark, opaque water. The dock's southern edge is a straight drop, no railing. Creatures can be pushed off this edge. The water is difficult terrain for swimmers and blocks line of sight from above to anything submerged.

- **Dock extensions (two finger piers):** Two narrower pier extensions jut 20 ft south from the main dock into the harbor, each 10 ft wide. They have no railing. The space between them is open water. These are the likely emergence points for the Mudrock Brutes.

- **Cargo crates (three clusters):** Three clusters of stacked crates — each cluster 10×10 ft, 5 ft tall, climbable. One cluster near the northwest corner, one near the center of the dock, one near the east dock edge. Full cover against ranged attacks from the opposite side. Climbable (DC 10 Athletics, or just use 5 ft of movement if adjacent).

- **Mooring posts and rope lines:** Eight mooring posts (2 ft diameter, 4 ft tall) spaced along the southern dock edge and along the finger pier edges. Rope lines run between them at 3 ft height — difficult terrain for running creatures crossing a line at speed (DC 12 Acrobatics or stop movement for the turn).

- **Dock crane (northeast corner):** A cargo crane with a horizontal arm extending 15 ft over the water, 10 ft above the dock surface. The arm is walkable (DC 12 Balance/Acrobatics to move along it). The base provides half cover. A creature at the end of the arm is directly above the water.

- **Lighting:** Late afternoon — the harbor is in direct light. The water below the dock is in shadow. No darkness or visibility issues on the dock surface itself.

> Style: painterly and detailed, similar to a professional tabletop RPG battle map. Weathered wood, dark harbor water, rope and cargo, overcast harbor light. Rendered strictly from directly above — top-down, no perspective, no isometric angle.
