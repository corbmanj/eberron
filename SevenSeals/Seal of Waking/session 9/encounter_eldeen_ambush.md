# Encounter: The Coordinates

*The Eldeen Reaches — sixty miles east of the Towering Wood, three hours before midnight.*

---

### Encounter Summary

```
Difficulty:  Deadly (with surprise)
Party:       4 players, level 10

Monster Roster:
  1× The Watcher — CR 10 (Lords of Dust operative)
  1× Cult Inquisitor — CR 7
  4× Cultist of the Severed Tongue — CR 2

The party arrives at the coordinates looking for a manifest zone. There is no
manifest zone. There is a disguised shapeshifter at a standing stone in the
middle of a forest clearing, playing the role of a wandering Gatekeeper druid
who has been "monitoring the area." The ambush is designed to kill the party
or delay them long enough that they cannot interfere with the ritual in Sharn.

The Watcher has been observing the party for months. It knows their spellcasters
by name, knows who the most dangerous martial is, knows which party members
tend to scatter and which tend to cluster. The ambush is not random. It is
targeted.

The cultists initiate as a surprise round (DC 18 Perception to spot them in
the tree line before they move). The Watcher uses its opening to devastating
effect against an isolated spellcaster. The Inquisitor moves in on round 2 with
Silent Ward already active.
```

**Traits:**

**Shapechanger.** The Watcher can use its action to polymorph into any Small or Medium humanoid it has observed for at least 1 hour, or back into its true form. Its statistics are the same in all forms. Equipment does not change.

**Studied Quarry.** The Watcher has observed the party for months. When it hits a creature that has used a spell, class feature, or named ability that The Watcher has previously witnessed, it deals an extra 14 (4d6) psychic damage. Describe this as the Watcher anticipating the target's positioning rather than reacting to it — it already knew where they'd be.

**Ambush Master.** When The Watcher attacks a creature that has not yet taken a turn in the current combat, it deals an extra 28 (8d6) damage and the target must succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw or be Frightened of The Watcher until the end of their next turn.

---

**Actions:**

**Multiattack.** The Watcher makes two Shadow Blade attacks and one Off-Hand Dagger attack.

**Shadow Blade.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +8 to hit, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 11 (2d6+4) piercing + 9 (2d8) psychic damage.

**Off-Hand Dagger.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +8 to hit, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d4+4) piercing damage.

---

**Bonus Action:**

**Vanish (Recharge 5–6).** The Watcher takes the Hide action. If successful, it can move up to 20 ft without provoking opportunity attacks.

---

**Tactics:**
- Begins the encounter disguised as an aged Gatekeeper druid, crouching near the wardstone, apparently studying it. Greets the party with concern: "You shouldn't be out here tonight — something is wrong with the wards." Gets them oriented toward the wardstone and away from the tree line before the ambush begins.
- Triggers the ambush when the party is clustered or when a spellcaster is within reach: drops the disguise and attacks in the same turn. Ambush Master + Studied Quarry together mean the opening attack can deal 50–60 damage to a single target before defenses are active.
- Priority target: the party's most dangerous spellcaster. If Tam or Ozzy is within melee range when the ambush starts, The Watcher spends both Shadow Blade attacks and the Off-Hand Dagger on them.
- Casts Mirror Image early (round 1 or 2) if it takes significant return damage.
- Vanish when below half HP; re-engages from a different angle.
- Casts Mislead when cornered below 40 HP — the illusory duplicate stands ground while The Watcher moves invisible into the trees.
- It does not flee. The Lords of Dust sent it here to end this, and it intends to.

---

**Loot:** A leather-bound journal in a tight cipher (contains months of observation notes on each party member: abilities used, spell slots estimated, behavioral patterns, known allies). A sending stone, warm to the touch and faintly pulsing — someone is on the other end, listening. Roll DMG Individual Treasure (CR 11–16).

> **DM Note on the journal and stone:** The journal is too useful to keep purely as flavor. If the party deciphers it (DC 18 Intelligence check, or a language specialist can do it with tools in a few hours), they learn that they've been watched since Korranberg, that Davath's name appears twice, and that the journal ends with: *Awaiting at coordinates. Anticipating a short evening.* The sending stone is keyed to a contact who goes silent the moment The Watcher falls — whoever is on the other end knows it failed. The stone is cold by the time the party examines it.

## Battle Map Description

> This map must be rendered in a strict top-down, directly overhead bird's-eye view. The camera is directly above looking straight down, as if the map were a floor plan. Not isometric. Not perspective. Not a three-quarter view. Directly overhead.

**Map size:** 100×100 ft — a forest clearing in old-growth woodland, wider than it looks from the entry path. The Towering Wood presses in on all sides.

**Terrain:**

- **The clearing:** A roughly circular open space, 60 ft in diameter, of dry packed earth covered in a layer of dead leaves. Flat, no difficult terrain in the open center. The leaves rustle if walked through quickly (DC 12 Perception to hear movement in the clearing from the tree line).

- **The Gatekeeper wardstone:** A 4-ft tall standing stone at the exact center of the clearing — Dhakaan-era construction, older than the Last War by a millennium, covered in faded Gatekeeper wards that no longer function. Half cover for a creature crouching behind it. The stone is cold to the touch. It is not magical. This is where The Watcher waits in disguise.

- **Tree line (all edges):** Old-growth trees begin at the clearing's edge. Trunks are 3–5 ft in diameter — full cover. The space between trunks contains root systems and undergrowth: difficult terrain throughout the tree line and beyond it. The canopy begins at 15–20 ft and is complete — no moonlight penetrates.

- **Elevated cultists (east and west trees):** Two trees at the east and west edges of the clearing have cultists positioned in the branches 15 ft up. These squares count as elevated platforms (no railing, difficult to stand on without Athletics). A creature climbing these trees requires DC 14 Athletics; each tree provides half cover from the canopy for a creature in the branches.

- **Entry path (south edge):** A narrow gap in the tree line — the path the party followed to reach the clearing. Roughly 10 ft wide. The two ground-level cultists are on either side of this gap, invisible, waiting.

- **Lighting:** The clearing has no natural light at midnight under complete forest canopy. Creatures without darkvision are in darkness. The wardstone is the only landmark visible to darkvision within the clearing. The Watcher and all cultists have darkvision — they chose this location and this time for exactly this reason.

- **The surrounding forest:** Beyond the clearing, the Towering Wood extends in all directions. A creature who retreats into the tree line more than 30 ft from the clearing edge is effectively in total darkness (even darkvision loses usefulness at 120 ft in dense undergrowth) and must navigate by feel. The Watcher knows this forest. The party does not.

**Starting positions:**
- The Watcher: at the wardstone, center of clearing, disguised as an elderly Gatekeeper druid.
- Cult Inquisitor: northwest tree line, 50 ft from the center, visible only with a DC 18 Perception check.
- Two ground-level cultists: flanking the south entry path, 5 ft inside the tree line on each side. Invisible (potion), DC 18 Perception to detect before round 1.
- Two elevated cultists: east and west trees, 15 ft up. Invisible (potion), DC 18 Perception to detect before round 1.

> Style: dense old-growth forest, ancient stone, deep shadow. No light except what the party brings. Strict top-down, no perspective, no isometric angle.
