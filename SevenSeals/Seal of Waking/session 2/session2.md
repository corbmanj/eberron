# Session 2: The Fortunate Lapse

## Seal of Waking — Act II: Korranberg to Shae Caerdal

*A session for 4 level-9 characters. Estimated runtime: 2-3 hours.*

---

## Session Overview

The party searches for a captain willing to sail to Aerenal, discovers nobody else is that crazy, and ends up back at Slip 7 with Captain Nor and his chicken. The voyage aboard *The Fortunate Lapse* is equal parts comedy and dread — culminating in Nor's obsession with a living spell he calls "Gretchen" nearly getting everyone killed.

### Session Flow

| Beat | Type | Est. Time | Description |
|------|------|-----------|-------------|
| 1 | Roleplay | 20 min | Shopping for captains at Korranberg docks |
| 2 | Roleplay | 20 min | Setting sail — the ship, the chicken, the captain |
| 3 | Roleplay/Skill | 30 min | The voyage — Nor's stories, ship mishaps, Tam's dream |
| 4 | Combat/Skill | 50 min | Gretchen — the living spell |
| 5 | Transition | 10 min | Aftermath and first glimpse of Aerenal |

## Beat 1: Shopping for a Captain

### The Docks

The party already met Nor at the end of Session 1 and negotiated 200 gp (150 gp if they help with Gretchen). But they want to see what else is available first.

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> Morning in Korranberg. The docks smell like salt, tar, and whatever the gnomes are smoking in those long-stemmed pipes. There are maybe a dozen ships moored along the waterfront — fishing boats, a couple of Lyrandar cargo haulers, a Zilargo customs cutter. It's not a big port. The Library of Korranberg looms over everything from the hill above, copper dome catching the sun.
>
> You figure there's got to be a better option than the guy arguing with a chicken.

### Other Captains

Let the party ask around. Every lead is a dead end:

| Captain | Ship | Response |
|---------|------|----------|
| **Captain Delara Tannis** (human, Lyrandar) | *The Gray Gale* — sleek, well-maintained | "Aerenal? We don't run passenger routes past the Straits. The elves don't want visitors, and I don't want arrows in my hull. Try the navy." |
| **Captain Voss** (dwarf, independent) | *Hammertide* — heavy cargo hauler | "I did the Aerenal run once. *Once.* Broke an axle on a reef the charts said wasn't there. Spent three days becalmed while things bumped the hull at night. You couldn't pay me enough." *(He means it.)* |
| **Captain Lirra d'Orien** (half-elf, Orien) | *Watchful Eye* — fast scout ship | "Oh, I could get you there. 800 gold. Each. Non-refundable if we die." *(She's serious. She'll actually do it for 800 gp per person, but she won't negotiate and she won't fight anything — strictly delivery.)* |

> **DM Note:** If the party tries Lirra, let them. 3,200 gp total is steep but technically possible. If they bite, she's professional, boring, and the voyage is uneventful — no Gretchen, no dream shard, no Nor. Adjust Session 3 accordingly. But most parties will balk at the price.

### Dockside Shopping — Brell's Maritime Surplus

Between captains (or after settling on Nor), the party can browse the docks. The main outfitter is a gnome named **Brell** who runs a cluttered warehouse at the end of the pier. Hand-painted sign reads: *"Brell's Maritime Surplus — If We Don't Have It, You Don't Need It."*

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> The shop looks halfway between a maritime theme restaurant and like a ship exploded inside a warehouse. Anchors hang from the ceiling, and coils of rope are stacked to head height, and a display case full of things that are either tools or weapons depending on who's holding them. A gnome in an oil-stained apron is sitting behind the counter, reading a book about knots called "What's knot to love".
>
> "Heading out to sea? You look like you're heading out to sea. Everyone who comes in here is heading out to sea or lost."

## Beat 2: Setting Sail

### The Fortunate Lapse — Ship Details

| Detail | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Type** | Two-masted caravel, roughly 60 ft long |
| **Condition** | Seaworthy. Barely. Every surface has been repaired at least twice. The patches have patches. |
| **Hull HP** | **120 HP**, AC 15 (reinforced wood) |
| **Speed** | 3 mph (magic-assisted sails — minor bound air elemental, very old, very tired) |
| **Crew** | Captain Nor and Reginald (the chicken) |
| **Quarters** | One cabin below deck with four hammocks. Smells like salt and chicken. There is straw on the floor. It's unclear if the straw is for the party or for Reginald. |
| **Galley** | A single iron stove bolted to the wall. Nor does all the cooking. It's... edible. He puts orange peel in everything. |
| **Cargo Hold** | Half-full of random salvage — rope, a cracked lantern, three mismatched boots, a crate labeled "DO NOT" (the rest of the label is torn off) |

### Departure

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> Captain Nor unties the mooring line, kicks the gangplank into the water instead of pulling it in, shrugs, and grabs the wheel.
>
> "Sails! Someone get the sails!"
>
> He's looking at you. There is no other crew. Reginald is perched on a coil of rope, watching.
>
> It takes about twenty minutes of Nor yelling directions that may or may not be real nautical terms, but the sails catch wind, the old elemental hums to life somewhere below the deck, and *The Fortunate Lapse* pulls out of Korranberg harbor.
>
> The Zilargo coastline slides past on the port side. The water is calm. The sky is clear. Nor starts whistling.

### Sailing Skill Checks

During the first few hours, Nor needs help. These are light and comedic — not punishing.

| Situation | Check | DC | Failure |
|-----------|-------|----|---------|
| The mainsail knot comes undone | Athletics or Sleight of Hand | 12 | The boom swings around. DEX save DC 12 or take 1d4 bludgeoning and get knocked prone. Nor says "That happens." |
| The compass spins wildly | Arcana or Investigation | 13 | The bound elemental is confused. The ship drifts in a wide circle for 10 minutes. Reginald seems unbothered. |
| A plank in the deck cracks underfoot | Perception or Survival | 11 | Someone's foot goes through. Stuck until a friend pulls them out (Athletics DC 10). Nor offers to "patch it later." |
| The stove catches fire during lunch | Dexterity or Survival | 12 | Lunch is ruined. Nor serves cold oranges instead. He seems pleased about this. |

> **DM Note:** These aren't meant to be threatening. They're establishing the ship as a character and giving everyone a moment to interact with Nor. Let the party riff.

## Beat 3: The Voyage

### Travel Time

Korranberg to Shae Caerdal is a 3-day voyage across the Thunder Sea.

| Day | Events |
|-----|--------|
| **Day 1** | Depart Korranberg. Ship mishap skill checks. Nor's stories over dinner. |
| **Day 2** | Calm sailing. Cult paranoia bird (optional). Nor gets quieter toward evening — he knows these waters. Tam's dream that night. |
| **Day 3** | Gretchen. Aftermath. First sight of Aerenal. Arrive Shae Caerdal by dusk. |

### Nor's Stories

Pick 1-2 of these for the voyage. Nor tells them unprompted, usually while cooking.

> **"The Time I Outran a Lyrandar Galleon"**
>
> "So there I was, right, two years ago, maybe three — time's funny on the water — and I'm running a shipment of, let's call it 'specialty goods,' out of Stormreach. Totally legal. Mostly legal. And this Lyrandar galleon, full navy rig, comes out of nowhere. Three masts, elemental ring, the whole thing. And they're *fast*."
>
> He pauses to flip something in the pan.
>
> "But here's the thing about Lyrandar ships. They're fast in a straight line. You put a reef in front of them and they've got to go *around* it because the elemental can't handle the turn radius. Me? I don't have that problem. I just... go through the reef."
>
> He gestures at a patched section of hull.
>
> "I mean, we made it."

> **"The Island That Wasn't There"**
>
> "You ever sail past the Lhazaar Principalities? No? Don't. But if you do — there's an island about two days northeast of Clifftop. Only it's not always there. I've seen it three times. First time, I thought it was fog. Second time, there were *lights*. Third time, I anchored and went ashore."
>
> Long pause.
>
> "I don't talk about the third time."
>
> He absolutely will talk about the third time if anyone asks. The story involves a talking crab and a door that opened into a hallway that went straight down. He did not go through the door. He says Reginald told him not to.

### Reginald

Reginald is a chicken. Nor treats him as first mate.

- Nor consults Reginald on navigation decisions. ("What do you think, Reg? Northeast or due east? ... Yeah, I agree.")
- Reginald sleeps on the wheel at night. Nor insists the chicken knows how to hold course.
- If anyone casts Speak with Animals on Reginald, the chicken says: "He's a good captain. Don't tell him I said that." And nothing else useful.

### Tam's Dream (Night 2 or 3)

When the party takes a long rest on the ship:

> **Read Aloud (to Tam's player only):**
>
> You're in Dal Quor again. But this time it's different. The colors aren't over-saturated — they're washed out. Everything is pale, almost white.
>
> You're standing on water. It's warm. There's light coming from below the surface — not sunlight. Something else. It pulses like a heartbeat.
>
> Fulla is there again. Closer this time. She's standing on the water too, maybe fifty feet away. The tendrils of light around her arms are brighter. She's not struggling — but she's not moving either. Like she's holding something in place.
>
> Your scar burns. Worse than last time.
>
> From below the water, a voice. The same one.
>
> *"The seal remembers what was taken from it."*
>
> You wake up. Your hand is on your scar. The hammock is soaked with sweat. Through the porthole, you can see the moon on the water. It's too bright.

- **No mechanical effect.** Still atmosphere.
- The pulsing light from below the water is Irian bleeding through. Tam doesn't know that yet.
- If Tam tells the party: good RP fuel. If not, fine.

### Cult Paranoia (Optional)

If the pacing needs tension between comedy beats:

- **Perception DC 15:** Someone notices a bird that's been following the ship for hours. It's not a seagull. It circles at a fixed distance, always at the edge of visibility. By the next morning, it's gone.
- **Nor's reaction:** "Birds follow ships all the time, friend. It's the ones that *stop* following you should worry about."
- This is flavor. No encounter. Just keeping the itch alive.

## Beat 4: Gretchen

### The Approach

Day 4 or 5 of the voyage. The water has changed color — darker, colder. The Thunder Sea is deep here.

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> You're at the wheel when Nor comes up from below deck. He's not eating anything. That's the first sign something's wrong.
>
> He walks to the bow and stands there for a long time, staring at the water. Reginald hops up to the railing next to him.
>
> "She's close," he says. Quiet. Not joking.
>
> He turns to face you. There's something in his eyes you haven't seen before. He's serious.
>
> "Gretchen. I've been hunting her for six years. She took my first ship — the *Lucky Draft*. Dragged it under in thirty seconds. I was the only one who made it out."
>
> He looks back at the water.
>
> "I see her about once a year. She follows me. I think she knows I'm looking for her. I think she likes it."
>
> The water goes still. Not calm — *still.* Like glass. No waves, no chop. Even the wind dies.
>
> "There she is."
>
> About 200 feet off the starboard bow, something pale rolls just below the surface. It's big. The shape of a whale, but the color is wrong — it's too white, almost luminous, and the edges of it shimmer like a heat mirage.
>
> Nor's knuckles are white on the railing.
>
> "50 gold off the fare. That was the deal. You ready?"

### What Gretchen Is

**Gretchen** is a **living spell** — a magical anomaly from the Mournland that somehow drifted into the Thunder Sea years ago. She takes the form of a massive white whale, but she's not a creature in the biological sense. She's a spell that became self-aware: a fusion of conjuration and evocation magic given shape and hunger.

In her whale form, she looks almost natural — just too white, too smooth, too perfect. When she takes enough damage, the illusion cracks and her true form emerges.

### Phase 1: The White Whale

*Huge monstrosity (living spell), unaligned*

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| **AC** | 14 (natural armor) |
| **HP** | 152 (Phase change at 76 HP) |
| **Speed** | 0 ft, Swim 50 ft |
| **STR** | 20 (+5) |
| **DEX** | 10 (+0) |
| **CON** | 18 (+4) |
| **INT** | 6 (-2) |
| **WIS** | 14 (+2) |
| **CHA** | 6 (-2) |

**Saving Throws:** CON +7, STR +8

**Damage Resistances:** Bludgeoning from nonmagical weapons

**Damage Immunities:** Poison

**Condition Immunities:** Poisoned, Exhaustion, Charmed

**Senses:** Blindsight 60 ft, passive Perception 15

**Languages:** None

**Actions:**
- **Bite.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +8 to hit, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 18 (3d8+5) piercing.
- **Tail Slam.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +8 to hit, reach 15 ft. Can target a creature OR the ship's hull. *Creature hit:* 14 (2d8+5) bludgeoning; target must succeed on a DC 16 STR save or be knocked prone (or pushed 10 ft if on the ship). *Hull hit:* 14 (2d8+5) bludgeoning to the ship; every creature within 10 ft of the impact point must succeed on a DC 14 DEX save or take 7 (2d6) bludgeoning and be knocked prone.
- **Ram (Recharge 5-6).** Gretchen charges the ship. *Hit:* 22 (4d8+4) bludgeoning damage to the ship's hull. Everyone on deck: DEX save DC 14 or be knocked prone and take 7 (2d6) bludgeoning damage. On a natural 1, a creature is knocked overboard.

**Tactics — Phase 1:**
- Round 1: Surfaces 60 ft from the ship. Circles.
- Round 2: Rams the ship (if recharge allows) or bites anyone at the railing.
- Round 3+: Alternates between Tail Slam (to knock people overboard) and Bite (to punish anyone in the water). Uses Ram whenever recharged.
- She targets the *ship*, not the party — she wants to sink it. She remembers sinking Nor's last one.

### Ship Damage Tracking

| Ship Status | HP Remaining | Effect |
|-------------|-------------|--------|
| **Intact** | 120-91 | No effect |
| **Damaged** | 90-61 | The deck is cracking. Movement on deck costs +5 ft (difficult terrain in some squares). Water seeping in below. |
| **Badly Damaged** | 60-31 | A section of railing is gone. The ship is listing. Ranged attacks from the ship have disadvantage. Below deck is ankle-deep in water. |
| **Critical** | 30-1 | The ship is sinking. It will go under in 1d4+1 rounds unless the party stops the damage. Below deck is waist-deep. Nor is screaming. |
| **Sunk** | 0 | *The Fortunate Lapse* goes under. Everyone in the water. Gretchen wins. |

**Repairing the Ship (during combat):**
- **Mending:** Restores 5 HP per casting (takes an action).
- **Athletics DC 14 + materials (rope, wood):** Restores 10 HP. Takes a full round (action + movement).
- **Fabricate or similar:** DM's discretion. Could fully stabilize the ship.

### The Phase Change

When Gretchen drops to **76 HP or below:**

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> Gretchen rolls sideways in the water and something is wrong with the shape. The whale outline is *flickering* — like a candle in a draft. Her skin cracks along the spine and light pours out. Not sunlight. Raw arcane energy. White-hot, humming.
>
> The whale shape collapses. What's underneath is bigger.
>
> She unfolds. Tentacles of solid light whip out of the mass — four, six, eight of them — and the central body is just a core of churning magic. No eyes. No mouth. Just a roiling sphere of conjuration and evocation energy wrapped in tendrils, pulling itself through the water toward the ship.
>
> It's not a whale. It was never a whale.
>
> Nor stares. His mouth is open. The orange he was holding rolls across the deck.
>
> "That's... that's new."

### Phase 2: The Living Spell (True Form)

*Huge monstrosity (living spell), unaligned*

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| **AC** | 15 (arcane shell) |
| **HP** | Remaining from Phase 1 (76 max) |
| **Speed** | 0 ft, Swim 40 ft, **Fly 20 ft (hover)** |
| **STR** | 16 (+3) |
| **DEX** | 14 (+2) |
| **CON** | 18 (+4) |
| **INT** | 8 (-1) |
| **WIS** | 14 (+2) |
| **CHA** | 10 (+0) |

**Saving Throws:** CON +7, WIS +5

**Damage Resistances:** Bludgeoning, Piercing, Slashing from nonmagical weapons

**Damage Vulnerabilities:** Dispel Magic (cast at 4th level or higher deals 4d10 force damage; at 3rd level, DC 14 ability check — success deals 2d10)

**Damage Immunities:** Poison, Psychic

**Condition Immunities:** Poisoned, Exhaustion, Charmed, Frightened, Paralyzed

**Senses:** Blindsight 60 ft, Tremorsense 30 ft, passive Perception 15

**Multiattack:** 3 attacks: 2 Arcane Lash + 1 Engulf or Pulse.

**Actions:**
- **Arcane Lash.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +7 to hit, reach 20 ft. *Hit:* 11 (2d6+4) force damage. On hit, target is pulled 10 ft toward Gretchen.
- **Engulf (1/round).** One creature within 5 ft must succeed on a DC 15 STR save or be restrained inside Gretchen's arcane mass. A restrained creature takes 14 (4d6) force damage at the start of each of its turns. Escape: DC 15 STR or DEX check (action). Another creature can attack the tendril holding the target (AC 13, 15 HP — damage to the tendril doesn't reduce Gretchen's HP).
- **Arcane Pulse (Recharge 5-6).** Gretchen releases a burst of raw energy. Every creature within 30 ft: DC 15 CON save. *Fail:* 21 (6d6) force damage. *Pass:* Half. This also hits the ship (counts as an attack against hull HP).

**Spell Siphon (Passive).** When a spell of 1st level or higher is cast within 30 ft of Gretchen, she regains HP equal to the spell's level x 3.

**Tactics — Phase 2:**
- She can now hover above the water. She'll rise up to deck level.
- Priority: Engulf whoever has done the most damage. Use Arcane Lash to pull ranged attackers off the ship into the water.
- Uses Arcane Pulse whenever recharged — this is the big ship-killer.
- **Spell Siphon** punishes the party for casting near her. Smart parties will figure out to use weapon attacks and cast from distance. Dumb parties will heal her by accident.
- She does NOT retreat. Living spells don't have survival instincts.

### Nor During Combat

Nor is not a combatant. He's a **sailor**. During the fight:

**Phase 1:**
- He's at the harpoon (ship has one mounted harpoon, +4 to hit, 2d6 piercing, range 60 ft, takes an action to reload).
- He's shouting directions: "Port side! PORT SIDE! That's left! YOUR OTHER LEFT!"
- He's useful. Barely.

**Phase 2:**
- He freezes when Gretchen transforms. One full round of doing nothing.
- After that, he snaps out of it and starts trying to keep the ship together (repair checks, bailing water).
- **If the ship hits Critical:** Nor is below deck trying to save Reginald. If the party doesn't help or intervene, the ship sinks and Nor goes under with it. **This is the moment that earns the Dream Shard.**

### Saving Nor

If the ship is sinking and Nor is below deck:

- **Getting to him:** Below deck is waist-deep (later chest-deep) water. Movement is halved.
- **Finding him:** Perception DC 12. He's holding Reginald above the water with one hand and clinging to a beam with the other.
- **Getting him out:** Athletics DC 13 to pull him free, or any creative solution.
- **If they save him:** He's soaked, shaking, and holding a wet chicken. He doesn't say anything for a while. Then: "I think she's gone." Reginald clucks once.

> **DM Note:** Nor doesn't need to almost drown if the party crushes the fight and the ship stays above Critical. The Dream Shard reward triggers if Nor genuinely feels they saved his life — sinking ship, Gretchen was about to kill him, or they put themselves in danger to protect him. Use your judgment.

### Combat Resolution

**If they kill Gretchen:**

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> The arcane mass shudders. The tendrils go rigid, then limp. Light bleeds out of the core in threads, dissolving into the air like embers. The whole thing collapses in on itself — slowly, almost gently — and sinks below the surface.
>
> The water where Gretchen was glows for about ten seconds. Then it fades. The waves come back. The wind picks up.
>
> Nor is standing at the bow. He doesn't move for a long time. When he finally turns around, his eyes are wet.
>
> "Six years," he says. That's all.

**If they drive her off (reduced to 15 HP or fewer, she retreats):**

> The mass of light shudders and pulls away from the ship. The tendrils retract. Gretchen drops below the surface and the glow fades south, deeper, further, until it's gone.
>
> Nor watches it go. He doesn't celebrate.
>
> "She'll be back."

**If the ship sinks:**

> *The Fortunate Lapse* groans and goes under. It's fast — bow first, then the stern tilts up, and then it's gone. You're in the water. The sea is cold and dark and you can feel the pull of the ship going down.
>
> Nor is treading water nearby, holding Reginald above his head. The chicken is screaming.

- Party needs to survive in open water. Swim checks, exhaustion rules.
- Tam's Tiny Hut can float (DM call). Wild Shape into aquatic forms. Improvised rafts from debris.
- Shae Caerdal is close enough to reach with magic or a day of swimming. An Aerenal patrol ship picks them up if needed (but they arrive looking like drowned rats, which affects Session 3's first impression).

### Encounter Adjustments

| Situation | Adjustment |
|-----------|------------|
| **Party is fresh** | Gretchen's Ram recharges on 4-6 instead of 5-6 |
| **Party is struggling** | Phase 2 starts at 50 HP instead of 76 |
| **Ship is sinking too fast** | Reduce Ram damage to 3d8+4 (17 avg) |
| **Fight is too easy** | Gretchen can use Arcane Lash as a reaction when a creature moves within reach |
| **Party ignores Spell Siphon** | Have Nor shout "Stop casting near it! It's *eating* your magic!" |

## Beat 5: Aftermath & Aerenal

### Loot

|------|-------------|

### Gretchen's Lash — Spell for Tam

When Gretchen's core collapses, something lingers on the surface of the water:

> **Read Aloud (to Tam's player):**
>
> As the arcane mass sinks, the glow doesn't fully fade. The water where Gretchen was is still lit from below — and on the surface, the light is *moving*. Tracing lines. Curves and angles, intersecting, branching. It's a pattern. A diagram.
>
> You recognize it immediately. It's a spell formula. The living spell's energy is dissipating, but for a few seconds, the raw structure of what held it together is visible — like seeing the skeleton of a building before it collapses.
>
> It's already fading at the edges.

- **Arcana DC 13** to transcribe the formula before it dissolves (~30 seconds).
- Costs **150 gp** in inks to complete the transcription into Tam's spellbook (can be done during the remaining voyage or at Shae Caerdal).
- On a failure, the pattern fades. Tam remembers enough to attempt again during a long rest (same DC, same cost) — the formula is burned into his memory, he just didn't capture it cleanly.

#### Gretchen's Lash

*3rd-level evocation (wizard)*

| Detail | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Casting Time** | 1 action |
| **Range** | 30 ft |
| **Components** | V, S |
| **Duration** | Instantaneous |

A tendril of raw arcane energy lashes out at a creature you can see within range. Make a ranged spell attack. On a hit, the target takes 4d8 force damage and must succeed on a STR save against your spell save DC or be pulled up to 15 feet toward you.

### First Glimpse of Aerenal

Whether the ship is intact, limping, or gone — the party reaches sight of Aerenal.

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> You see Aerenal before you reach it. The coastline rises out of the morning haze like something out of a painting that got the scale wrong. White cliffs. Trees so tall they don't look real — soarwood, you think, the kind they use to build airships. The canopy is dark green and gold and it runs unbroken along the coast as far as you can see in either direction.
>
> There's no harbor. No docks. No signs of civilization from out here. Just the cliffs and the trees and a silence that feels deliberate. Like the island is watching you decide whether to come closer.
>
> Then you see the city. Shae Caerdal. It's built into the cliff face — white stone towers connected by bridges so thin they look like spider silk. Elven ships sit in a natural harbor carved into the rock, their sails furled, their hulls pale wood. Everything is elegant and precise and ancient.
>
> Nor lets out a low whistle. "Every time," he says.
>
> Reginald is quiet. Even the chicken is impressed.
