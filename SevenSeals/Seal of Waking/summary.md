# Seal of Waking — Arc Summary

## Overview

The party travels from Sharn to Aerenal to retrieve the **Seal of Waking** from **Irian, the Eternal Dawn**, accessed through a manifest zone in **Shae Mordai** (the City of the Dead) on the island continent of Aerenal. After collecting this seal, the party levels to 10.

**Estimated Length:** ~5 sessions (flexible based on pacing)

---

## Arc Structure

### Session 1: The Iron Whisper *(Lightning Rail to Korranberg)*
- **Status:** Written
- Departure from Sharn via Lightning Rail
- Sereth Vahl — cult spy probes the party (social/investigation)
- Starilaskur layover; Tam's first recurring dream vision
- Broken coupling ambush by the Cult of the Severed Word (combat)
- Arrival in Korranberg; meeting Captain Nor

### Session 2: The Fortunate Lapse *(Sea Voyage)*
- **Status:** Written
- Shopping for captains at Korranberg docks (nobody else is crazy enough)
- Sailing aboard *The Fortunate Lapse* — Nor, Reginald the chicken, no other crew
- Skill checks for ship mishaps (comedic), Nor's stories, Tam's second dream vision
- **Gretchen** — Nor's white whale, actually a living spell from the Mournland
  - Phase 1: White whale form (152 HP, rams the ship)
  - Phase 2: True form at half HP — arcane mass with tentacles, hover, Spell Siphon
  - Ship has 120 HP and can sink — damage tracking table included
  - Dream Shard reward if party saves Nor's life (not offered upfront)
- First glimpse of Aerenal; arrival at Shae Caerdal
- Cult paranoia: bird following the ship (optional, flavor only)

### Session 3: The City of the Dead *(Shae Mordai)*
- **Status:** Not yet written
- Travel from Shae Caerdal to Shae Mordai
- **Aerenal elves as gatekeepers** — Thranduil-style nobility roleplay
  - Finella's Valenar heritage creates tension (Valenar and Aerenal elves don't get along)
  - Party must bluff/charm their way to the manifest zone
- **Shae Mordai descriptions:** City of the deathless, ancestors walking among the living, positive energy so thick the air glows at dawn — play this up hard with vivid detail
- Nor drops them at Shae Caerdal and departs

### Session 4: The Eternal Dawn *(Irian)*
- **Status:** Not yet written
- Enter Irian through the manifest zone
- **Gleaming Knights** — celestial guardians who judge the party's worthiness (combat-oriented)
- Irian's nature: everything renewed, light overwhelming, the dawn that never ends
- Trials or encounters themed around worthiness and renewal

### Session 5: The Seal *(Claiming the Seal of Waking)*
- **Status:** Not yet written
- Final trial/guardian encounter
- Acquiring the Seal of Waking
- Return to Eberron
- **Level up to 10**

## Key Decisions

- **Cult of the Severed Word:** Paranoid background tension throughout. No specific encounters planned (DM can drop one in at any point if pacing needs it).
- **Tam's Dream Visions:** Recurring each long rest. Sees Dal Quor, Fulla holding the door, scar burns. Slow burn for later payoff.
- **Seals are with Gendrick:** The party left collected seals with Gendrick. The cult knows the party is going after the next one.
- **Format:** All session documents formatted as professional D&D adventure guides with read-aloud sections, stat blocks, battle maps, and DM notes.
- **Korranberg:** Waypoint only. Find Nor, get on the boat, move on.
- **Tone arc:** Paranoia (train) → Comedy (Nor/sea) → Wonder (Aerenal/Shae Mordai) → Awe/Challenge (Irian)
