# 🔥 Primary Antagonist

## **Jasper Phiarlan — Main Threat**

### **Base Stat Block**

**Veteran (MM p. 350)**
⬆️ *Heavily Modified*

### Why Veteran?

* Martial, disciplined, human-scale
* Designed to fight PCs, not monsters
* Easy to upscale without breaking balance

### Jasper’s Upgrades (Recommended)

These are **simple numeric tweaks**, not a homebrew rewrite.

* **HP:** 120–135 (he’s prepared, paranoid)
* **AC:** 18 (breastplate + cloak or defensive training)
* **Multiattack:** 3 attacks (instead of 2)
* **Weapon:** Rapier (1d8+5) + dagger offhand (1d4+5)
* **Bonus Action:** *Cunning Action* (as Rogue-lite mobility)
* **Once per Fight:** Action Surge–style extra action

📌 **Threat Level:**
Jasper alone is a **hard fight**, but survivable. With terrain, poison, or minions, he becomes **deadly**.

# 🧠 Secondary Family Members (Optional Combatants)

These should **not all engage at once** unless the party makes truly catastrophic choices.

---

## **Elaria Phiarlan (Shadow Operative)**

### **Stat Block**

**Spy (MM p. 349)**

### Why Spy?

* High Dexterity
* Sneak Attack
* Infiltration-focused, not a frontliner

### Behavior

* Joins combat only if:

  * Jasper is clearly winning
  * Or the party threatens the House
* Targets casters, then disengages

📌 **Danger Level:**
Moderate. Adds tactical pressure, not raw damage.

---

## **Tavian Phiarlan (Disgraced Blade)**

### **Stat Block**

**Bandit Captain (MM p. 344)**

### Why Bandit Captain?

* Emotion-driven combatant
* Strong presence but poor discipline
* Good “wild card” enemy

### Behavior

* Might attack Jasper instead of the party
* Reckless, drinks before fighting
* Likely to go down early

📌 **Danger Level:**
Low-to-moderate, unpredictable.

---

## **Lady Maelis Phiarlan (Social Assassin)**

### **Stat Block**

**Noble (MM p. 348)**
(*No upgrades*)

### Why Noble?

* She is **not a combatant**
* Her power is political, not physical

### Behavior

* Will not fight unless directly attacked
* If harmed, House Phiarlan becomes a long-term enemy

📌 **Danger Level:**
Narrative, not mechanical.

---

## **Lord Arven Phiarlan (Patriarch)**

### **Stat Block**

**Knight (MM p. 347)**

### Why Knight?

* Old-school authority
* Defensive, honorable
* Strong presence, limited offense

### Behavior

* Tries to stop the fight
* If forced in, protects Jasper
* Will retreat rather than die

📌 **Danger Level:**
Moderate, defensive obstacle.

---

## **Seren Phiarlan (Archivist & Moral Compass)**

### **Stat Block**

**Commoner (MM p. 345)**

### Why?

* She is not meant to fight
* Her danger is **emotional stakes**

📌 **DM Note:**
If Jasper targets her, the table *will* turn on him.

---

## **Kaelen Phiarlan (Teen Witness)**

### **Stat Block**

**Commoner**

* Flees immediately
* Screams, hides, or accidentally reveals secrets

# ⚔️ If Combat Escalates Badly

Jasper + Elaria + Lord Arven

This becomes a **Deadly+ encounter**, but still survivable for level 9 PCs if:

* PCs control the battlefield
* Or neutralize Jasper early

# 🎭 Encounter Design Philosophy

* **Jasper should feel dangerous but human**
* The family adds **pressure, not HP sponges**
* Players should feel:

  > “We *could* kill them all… but should we?”

Give Jasper:

* A **smoke bomb**
* A **hidden passage**
* Or **illusion cover**

Even if he escapes wounded, the party wins narratively.
