## 🍷 The Feast at House Phiarlan

### Scene Tone

Elegant. Polite. **Uncomfortably restrained**.
Everyone is smiling—but not everyone is relaxed.

This is not a loud or explosive scene. It’s a pressure cooker.

---

## 🕯️ Arrival & Setting (Read Aloud)

> The Phiarlan estate rises from the mist like a carefully composed illusion—arched balconies, soft lanternlight, and musicians playing somewhere you cannot see. Servants relieve you of weapons with practiced courtesy, though their eyes linger just a moment longer on the breastplate you brought.
>
> You are led into a long dining hall of darkwood and velvet drapes. Illusory candlelight drifts lazily above the table, never dripping wax, never flickering.
>
> At the head of the table sits **Lord Arven Phiarlan**, his face lined with grief and gratitude. To his right is **Jasper Phiarlan**, immaculate in dress and posture. To Arven’s left sits **Seren**, Captain Dimple’s former fiancée—hands folded, eyes distant.
>
> The seat at the far end of the table is conspicuously empty.

*(That seat belonged to Captain Dimple.)*

---

## 🧍 Key NPC Behavior at the Table

### Lord Arven Phiarlan (the father)

* Genuinely grateful.
* Emotionally raw.
* Keeps steering conversation toward **closure** and **honor**.

**Sample Line:**

> “Captain Dimple was… loyal beyond measure. You’ve done us a kindness returning what remains of his service.”

---

### Jasper Phiarlan (the murderer)

* Perfectly polite.
* Watches the party constantly—but **never stares**.
* Flinches subtly when:

  * The Demon Wastes are mentioned
  * The roc nest is mentioned
  * Seren speaks unexpectedly

**Sample Lines:**

> “We owe you more than words. Please—eat. Tonight, you are guests of House Phiarlan.”
>
> “The wastes are… unpredictable. Many good people were lost there.”

**DM Note:**
If the party presses Jasper on *details*, he answers confidently—but always vaguely.

---

### Seren (the emotional fuse)

* Says little at first.
* Drinks wine she doesn’t seem to taste.
* Watches the party with cautious curiosity.

**Sample Line (mid-meal, quietly):**

> “Did… did he still wear the breastplate?”

If they answer yes:

* Her composure cracks slightly.
* Jasper’s jaw tightens.

---

## 🍽️ Mid-Meal Tension Escalation

After the second course, Lord Arven raises a glass.

**Read Aloud:**

> “To those who did not return—and to those who ensured they were not forgotten.”

As glasses rise:

* Jasper hesitates half a second before drinking.
* Seren does not raise hers at all.

---

## 🔍 Opening the Cracks (Player Prompts)

Let the players ask questions. Use these **pressure points**:

### If they mention:

* **Roc nest** → Jasper didn’t know about it (he claims scavengers found the bodies)
* **Lack of bodies** → Jasper says the land was “too dangerous to recover them”
* **Dimple’s leadership** → Seren contradicts Jasper *slightly*

**Seren, softly:**

> “Dimple wouldn’t have charged ahead. He was… careful. Always.”

Jasper immediately responds:

> “Circumstances change in the wastes.”

**DM Note:**
Insight checks here should reveal:

* Jasper is controlling the narrative
* Seren is holding back—afraid, but not ignorant

---

## 🗡️ The Moment Before Violence

Dessert is served. Music resumes. Servants begin clearing plates.

**Read Aloud:**

> As the last dish is taken away, Lord Arven rises.
>
> “You will, of course, stay the night. Tomorrow we can speak of proper recompense—”

Jasper cuts in smoothly:

> “Father, if I may—there is one more matter.”

He gestures subtly.

The doors close.
The music stops.

Servants do **not** turn back around.

---

## ⚠️ Jasper’s Move (Transition to Combat or Escape)

### Jasper’s Accusation (Optional)

If the party seems suspicious or hostile:

> “These adventurers know more than they should. I believe they intend to extort our house with half-truths and relics stolen from the dead.”

Lord Arven is stunned. Seren goes pale.

### Or Jasper Goes Straight to Murder

* Poison gas via illusory candles
* Glamoured servants draw blades
* Jasper slips away during the chaos if needed

**Seren’s Reaction:**

> “Jasper—what are you doing?!”

This is the moment she **chooses the party**.

---

## 🕯️ Aftermath Hooks

Depending on outcome:

* Seren privately tells them **what Dimple suspected**
* Lord Arven realizes Jasper lied—but needs proof
* Jasper escapes, becoming a recurring antagonist

* **Slow burn tension**
* Players feel clever for noticing cracks
* Jasper isn’t mustache-twirling—he’s *plausible*
* Seren becomes emotionally anchored to the party
