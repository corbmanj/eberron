## Seren of Phiarlan – The Woman Between Truth and Lies

**Public Persona:**
Seren is gracious, poised, and visibly relieved to finally have *something* of Captain Dimple returned to the house. She thanks the party sincerely and insists they are honored guests. She appears to genuinely mourn Dimple.

**Private Reality:**
Seren loved Dimple deeply — not as a political match, but as a partner who *saw her*. She is perceptive, emotionally intelligent, and haunted by the gaps in Jasper’s story, even if she hasn’t yet allowed herself to fully confront them.

She does **not** know Jasper betrayed Dimple — but she knows *something was wrong*.

## What Captain Dimple Confided in Seren

These are things he told her in confidence before leaving for the Demon Wastes — details that don’t *prove* Jasper’s guilt on their own, but create a growing web of suspicion when the party starts asking questions.

### 1. Doubts About the Mission

Dimple confided that the mission made no sense:

> “We were sent as diplomats… but with no clear counterpart. No treaty drafts. No agreed meeting point. It feels like theater.”

He believed the trip was **unnecessarily dangerous** and pushed forward by Jasper personally, not House Phiarlan leadership.

---

### 2. Jasper’s Jealousy (Unspoken but Obvious)

Dimple never accused Jasper outright, but Seren could tell he was uneasy:

* Jasper lingered too long during farewells
* He made passive remarks about “duty over affection”
* He insisted on traveling *ahead* or *alone* at times

Dimple once said:

> “If anything happens out there, watch Jasper. Not because he’s cruel — but because he wants something too badly.”

---

### 3. A Last Gift / Promise

Dimple gave Seren something small before leaving:

* A signet ring
* A ribbon from his uniform
* Or a coded phrase only they used

He told her:

> “If I don’t come back, trust what *is missing* more than what you’re told.”

This line becomes incredibly important later.

---

### 4. The Roc Omen

Dimple mentioned seeing a roc days before disaster — not attacking, just *watching*.

> “Rocs don’t scavenge. If one nests over the dead, it means the bodies were left… deliberately.”

This directly contradicts Jasper’s version of events, where he claims they were overwhelmed and barely escaped.

## Seren at the Feast

### How She Interacts with the Party

Seren is warm and curious — especially toward anyone wearing or carrying the breastplate.

She asks:

* *“Where exactly did you find it?”*
* *“Was it… damaged?”*
* *“Were there others?”*

She doesn’t interrogate — she listens.

If a PC mentions:

* **The roc nest**
* **No other bodies**
* **Signs of betrayal or ambush**

She grows quiet. Thoughtful. Troubled.

---

### What Seren Says That Unsettles Jasper

At one point during the feast, Seren casually says something like:

> “Dimple always said if he died on duty, it would be because someone wanted him *out of the way*, not because he was careless.”

Jasper reacts badly:

* A forced laugh
* A sharp interruption
* Changing the subject too quickly

This is the **first visible crack**.

---

## How Jasper Plans to Kill the Party (Using Seren as Cover)

Jasper *cannot* act openly at the feast — too many witnesses, too much scrutiny. Instead, he uses House Phiarlan subtlety.

### The Plan

After the feast, Jasper insists the party stay the night as honored guests.

That night:

* **Phiarlan shadow agents** attempt to kill the party
* The method depends on tone:

  * Poisoned wine meant for “late-night hospitality”
  * Collapsing walkways
  * Illusionary assassins posing as servants

Jasper intends to:

* Blame it on **outside agents** (Dreaming Dark, Demon Wastes survivors)
* Or claim the party attempted to steal something

---

### Seren’s Role in Disrupting the Plan

Seren unknowingly saves the party in one of these ways:

* She notices the poison because it smells wrong (Dimple taught her)
* She hears movement in the walls (Dimple showed her Phiarlan secret passages)
* She gives a PC Dimple’s parting phrase, which triggers a memory or Insight check

She doesn’t accuse Jasper yet — but she **chooses to protect the party**.

That choice puts her in danger.

---

## Emotional Payoff

When the truth finally comes out, Seren doesn’t scream or collapse.

She simply says to Jasper:

> “You didn’t kill him because you loved me.
> You killed him because you wanted his life.”

And walks away.
