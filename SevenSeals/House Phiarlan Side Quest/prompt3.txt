## 1. What Really Happened

### The Mission

House Phiarlan dispatched a **small diplomatic and reconnaissance mission** to the Demon Wastes:

* **Captain Dimple**, trusted commander and bodyguard
* **Jasper Phiarlan**, youngest heir, officially to observe and learn
* A handful of operatives and guides

The *true* purpose was to establish quiet contact with a Carrion Tribe intermediary regarding future intelligence routes—not peace, but information.

### The Secret Motivation

Jasper was already:

* Deeply in love with **Seren**, Captain Dimple’s fiancée
* Consumed by jealousy and entitlement
* Convinced that if Dimple died “honorably,” Seren would eventually turn to him

Jasper believed the Demon Wastes would provide *plausible deniability*.

### The Betrayal

While traveling near a roc nesting ground:

* Jasper secretly sabotaged warding glyphs and sent false scouting information
* He intentionally steered the group closer to the roc territory under the guise of a “safe pass”
* When the roc attacked, Jasper **fled immediately**, taking a pre-arranged escape route

Captain Dimple:

* Held the line to buy time for the others
* Was gravely injured but **not killed by the roc**
* Jasper returned *after* the fight, under the pretense of searching for survivors
* He found Dimple barely alive and **finished him off**, either:

  * With poison
  * Or by pushing him into the ravine beneath the nest

Jasper then:

* Took only *some* identifying items
* Left the breastplate behind, believing the roc would claim it
* Returned alone

The roc later incorporated the breastplate into its nest—where the party found it.

## 2. Jasper’s Official Story

Jasper’s version is polished, emotional, and rehearsed.

### The Public Narrative

* The party was ambushed by an *unexpectedly large* Carrion Tribe force
* A roc was drawn by the battle
* Captain Dimple died heroically covering Jasper’s escape
* Jasper claims he tried to save him but was dragged away by the chaos

Key details he emphasizes:

* Dimple’s bravery
* His own helplessness
* The overwhelming nature of the attack

### What He Avoids Saying

* Exact locations
* The sequence of events
* Who gave the orders to move through that area
* Why no bodies or equipment were recovered

To most listeners, the story feels tragic but believable.

## 3. The Gaps & Contradictions (Leverage Points)

These are the *pressure cracks* where the party can push.

---

### 1. **The Breastplate Shouldn’t Be There**

* Jasper claimed the roc arrived *during* the battle
* But the breastplate was found **intact in the nest**, not damaged or scattered
* This suggests:

  * Dimple survived long enough after the fight
  * Or the roc encountered him *after* the main conflict

Seren knows:

* Dimple never removed his armor unless ordered
* He would not have abandoned it while fleeing

---

### 2. **Jasper Knows Too Much—or Too Little**

Depending on the questioning:

* Jasper knows the roc’s nesting behavior **in suspicious detail**
* Or he can’t answer basic questions about terrain features the party later confirms

Examples:

* Misremembering distances
* Describing the wrong approach path
* Incorrectly recalling the number of attackers

Under stress, his story subtly shifts.

---

### 3. **No Injuries, No Trauma Marks**

Jasper returned:

* With minimal wounds
* No signs of exhaustion consistent with fleeing a massacre
* No blood on his clothing or gear (or claims it was “lost”)

Seren remembers:

* Dimple returned from *minor* skirmishes half-dead
* Jasper’s condition doesn’t match his story

---

### 4. **Dimple’s Last Words (via Seren)**

This is powerful.

Dimple confided in Seren before leaving:

* He suspected **Jasper was reckless and untrustworthy**
* He said:

  > “If I don’t come back, don’t believe the story they tell too quickly.”

Seren dismissed this as stress at the time—but now, with the breastplate, it haunts her.

---

### 5. **The Timeline Doesn’t Add Up**

Jasper’s travel time:

* Doesn’t match known routes
* He arrived back **too quickly** for someone fleeing wounded through the Wastes

The party, having *been* in the Demon Wastes, may recognize this immediately.

## Jasper’s Next Move (Foreshadowing)

He decides:

* The truth is too close
* The party must die **quietly**

This sets up:

* The poisoned feast
* Framed assassins
* Or a staged “retaliation” by Demon Wastes agents during the night

## Seren’s Role Going Forward

Seren is:

* Torn between grief, suspicion, and fear
* Subtly helping the party by:

  * Asking leading questions at dinner
  * Providing private details about Dimple’s habits
  * Becoming visibly shaken when Jasper speaks

She doesn’t accuse—yet.
But she’s the emotional fulcrum that can bring Jasper down.
