# The Truth vs. Jasper’s Story

## What *Actually* Happened in the Demon Wastes

Captain **Dimple** was loyal, perceptive, and increasingly uneasy.

* The mission was officially a **diplomatic overture** to a demon-wastes faction via intermediaries.
* Dimple discovered:

  * Jasper had secretly **arranged a second meeting** with unknown contacts.
  * Supplies were being subtly rerouted.
  * Jasper carried a **House Phiarlan blade**, not standard diplomatic gear.
* On the night of the massacre:

  * Jasper slipped away from camp, claiming illness.
  * A demon-wastes raiding force attacked — **far too precisely**.
  * Dimple realized *too late* that the attack was staged.
  * He ordered the guard to buy time while trying to get Jasper to safety.
* Jasper **never returned to help**.
* Dimple died defending the retreat, his breastplate torn away and later taken by a roc scavenging the battlefield.

Before the final stand, Dimple entrusted **letters and private worries** to Seren (his fiancée), implying he feared betrayal *from within*.

---

## What Jasper Told Everyone

Jasper’s official story is clean, tragic, and rehearsed:

* The party was ambushed unexpectedly.
* Dimple ordered Jasper to flee as the sole survivor.
* Jasper “tried to return” but was driven off by overwhelming forces.
* He barely escaped with his life, traumatized and broken.

### The Gaps & Cracks

These are the things the party can notice or press on:

1. **Timing Inconsistencies**

   * Jasper returned *too quickly* for someone fleeing on foot.
   * His injuries were superficial.

2. **The Breastplate**

   * Jasper claimed Dimple was “buried with honor.”
   * Why was the breastplate missing?
   * Why didn’t Jasper mention scavengers or rocs?

3. **Dimple’s Reputation**

   * Dimple *never* would have ordered a single noble heir to flee alone without an escort.
   * He was meticulous — the kind who planned redundancies.

4. **Seren’s Letters**

   * She knows Dimple feared betrayal but never named Jasper outright.
   * Jasper doesn’t know how much Dimple confided in her.

---

# Seren at the Feast

Seren is composed, grieving, and watchful.

### What Dimple Confided in Her

* He feared **someone close** was endangering the mission.
* He suspected Jasper was hiding a *personal motive*.
* He promised to return and explain everything.
* He wrote, chillingly:

  > “If I do not return, look to who benefits most from my absence.”

She has never voiced this publicly — until now, when the party arrives with proof Dimple died far from any proper burial.

# The Feast: A Tense Roleplay Scene

### The Setting

House Phiarlan’s dining hall is elegant, warm, and theatrical:

* Illusory lanterns cast soft shifting light.
* Music from unseen performers hums faintly.
* Long polished table, silverware arranged with perfect symmetry.

**Lord Arven** sits at the head.
**Jasper** sits close to his father — too close.
**Seren** sits opposite Jasper.

The breastplate has already been presented privately; this dinner is meant to “celebrate closure.”

---

## Scene Beats

### Opening Toast (Lord Arven)

> “To Captain Dimple — loyal servant of House Phiarlan. May his courage be remembered.”

Jasper raises his glass immediately.
Seren hesitates half a heartbeat before doing the same.

📌 *Insight checks here reveal Jasper’s smile never reaches his eyes.*

---

### Subtle Pressure

As courses are served:

* Lord Arven asks the party to recount **where they found the breastplate**.
* Jasper interrupts frequently:

  > “The Demon Wastes are cruel places. Scavengers take what they will.”

Seren quietly asks:

> “Did you find… anything else?”

She’s watching Jasper when she says it.

---

### The Cracks Show

At some point, Seren says:

> “Dimple once told me he feared someone in his party was lying to him.”

The table goes still.

Jasper laughs too quickly:

> “Captain Dimple was always suspicious. A good trait in a soldier.”

Lord Arven frowns — this is the first time he’s heard this.

---

### Jasper’s Fear

If the party presses too much or mentions:

* The *roc nest*
* The *lack of burial*
* The *precision of the attack*

Jasper begins to unravel:

* He drinks more.
* He grows sharp, sarcastic.
* He tries to redirect blame to the Demon Wastes themselves.

📌 *This is when he decides the party must die.*

---

# Jasper’s Plan to Kill the Party

He will not strike openly at the table — House Phiarlan’s reputation matters too much.

### His Plan:

1. **After-Dinner Performance**

   * The party is invited to a private showing — an exclusive Phiarlan illusion.
2. **Poisoned Wine or Illusory Gas**

   * Non-lethal at first: paralysis, confusion, or dreamlike stupor.
3. **House “Security”**

   * Loyal bladesingers and disguised assassins intervene.
   * Jasper plans to make it look like:

     * The party attacked him.
     * Or were thieves attempting blackmail.

Seren suspects something is wrong — she may:

* Slip a warning to one PC.
* “Accidentally” spill a drink to interrupt Jasper’s timing.

---

# Why This Works Dramatically

* Jasper isn’t a mustache-twirling villain — he’s desperate.
* Lord Arven is a tragic figure who **wants** to believe his son.
* Seren is the emotional fulcrum.
* The party holds the truth but must choose **when and how** to reveal it.
