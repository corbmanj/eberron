# House Phiarlan Side Quest - Summary

## Overview
A 2-session murder mystery and political intrigue arc (Jan 3-10, 2026) set at the **House Phiarlan estate in Sharn**. The party returned Captain Dimple's breastplate (found in a roc nest during the Demon Wastes arc) and uncovered that **Jasper Phiarlan** murdered Dimple to pursue his fiancee Seren. Jasper had married Seren just a month before the party arrived.

## The Crime
- **Captain Dimple ir'Phiarlan** was the decorated captain of House guard, engaged to **Seren**
- **Jasper ir'Phiarlan** (youngest heir, dragonmark of the eye) was secretly obsessed with Seren
- During a mission to the Demon Wastes, Jasper altered the route to pass near roc nesting grounds, getting Dimple killed
- Jasper returned as the sole survivor with a fabricated hero story
- He married Seren one month before the party arrived

## Session 1 (Jan 3, 2026): The Dinner & The Show
- Party arrived at the Phiarlan playhouse in Sharn; returned the breastplate to **Lord Arven**
- Met the family: Lord Arven, Jasper (stressed), **Seren** (still wearing her wedding band, now married to Jasper), **Lady Maelis** (uninterested), **Alaria** (stone cold, picking fingernails with a dagger), **Tavian** (shifty, kept looking between Jasper and Seren)
- Everyone is dragonmarked
- Attended Seren's performance of **"Dance of the 7 Swords"** — she was incredible but cut her hand (unusual); Jasper gave excessive standing ovations; Tavian noted he'd never seen her cut herself before
- Tam and Ozzy learned Jasper married Seren a month ago; Jasper's story had inconsistencies and he seemed shifty when Dimple was mentioned
- Finella followed **Alaria** to her room — chest full of knives (Finella thought it was cool); Alaria later gave Finella a dagger
- Feast afterward; Lord Arven implied House Cannith was responsible for Dimple's death
- Party went to bed; **Tam dreamed** of being in Dimple's camp — discovered Jasper set the route, found a warning letter about aggressive tribes, saw the roc nest wasn't on Jasper's map
- **Finella woke up strapped to a table** in the basement with a hooded figure interrogating her

## Session 2 (Jan 10, 2026): Rescuing Finella
- Lord Arven, Lady Maelis, and Tavian at breakfast; Finella missing
- Tam found a secret staircase in a fireplace leading to the basement (Lord Arven said the basement was "closed")
- Tam sent a magical eye to scout; found a shadowy figure (Alaria) and Jasper interrogating Finella
- **Ozzy disguised as Lord Arven** and confronted them
- Alaria (reading: *"What is Father doing? I didn't think he knew about the dungeons!"*) was confused
- Jasper (reading: *"She's going to bring us all down!"*) was panicking
- Tam cast Hypnotic Pattern to give Finella time to grab her gear
- **Finella stabbed Jasper**; 2 suits of armor animated
- Alaria told the party to run — the suits wouldn't stop; she said she'd "take care of Jasper"
- Combat: Tam's Storm Sphere, Aerin chased Jasper, Ozzy cut down armor, Finella shot heads off
- **Jasper's wife** (another robed figure) entered
- Chase led to the main hall; **Jasper confessed** — he was convinced his secret of getting Dimple killed would come out
- **Lord Arven cut off Jasper's dragonmark with a knife** and disowned him
- He offered the severed dragonmark to Finella ("Is this what you want?"); she said "Eww no"; **Ozzy took it** and put it in the Bag of Holding
- The Reel Heroes left House Phiarlan immediately

## Resolution
- Jasper exposed and disowned; dragonmark physically removed
- House Phiarlan relationship: complicated but not hostile — Lord Arven acted justly
- Party left quickly; Ozzy has Jasper's severed dragonmark (Mark of Shadow) in the Bag of Holding
- Alaria gave Finella a dagger — possible future ally
