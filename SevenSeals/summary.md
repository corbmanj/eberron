# The Seven Seals Campaign — Overall Summary

## Campaign Overview

A D&D 5e campaign set in **Eberron** following the **Reel Heroes** — a party of 4 level 10 PCs collecting the **Seven Seals** that lock away **Chatechesma**, an ancient Daelkyr. The deeper mythos: Chatechesma, Vecna, and the Daelkyr broadly are all faces of **Nyarlathotep** — the manipulator, the messenger, the entity that wears countless masks to maneuver mortals into serving **Azathoth**. Azathoth is the true horror: formless, blind, existing at the center of everything. If it enters this world, the world ends immediately. It cannot act directly — it needs proxies to open the way.

### Seal of Madness (Level 10) — NOT YET STARTED
*See DM Notes below for the true shape of this arc.*

The party believes the 7th seal is in Xoriat (Plane of Madness) and must find a manifest zone to get there. They do not yet know where one is — research will be required.

**What actually happens:** When the party reaches the Xoriat manifest zone and attempts to pass through, they are not transported to Xoriat. They are pulled into **purgatory** — a liminal space between planes. Vecna is there. He has captured Gendrick and is in the process of taking the six collected seals from him. The party can only watch as Gendrick casts a final spell, sending the six seals hurtling through the weakened membranes of the multiverse — scattering them across the planes. This opens Gendrick to a killing blow from Vecna, but weakens Vecna enough for the party to fight him briefly. They cannot kill him. They are thrust out of purgatory and land at the beginning of **Vecna: Eve of Ruin** (levels 10-20).

The Rod of Seven Parts in Vecna: Eve of Ruin is the scattered seals. The party is collecting things they already held, now knowing what they truly are.

## DM Notes — True Arc (Things the Party Does Not Know)

**The seals are the Rod of Seven Parts.** The party has been collecting the pieces of a powerful artifact without knowing it. Vecna engineered the entire collection quest. The thinning planar membranes are not a side effect of his ritual — they are part of it. When the seals scatter through purgatory, they pass through the weakened membranes into other planes. This sets up Vecna: Eve of Ruin's core quest: retrieve the rod pieces from across the multiverse.

**There is no 7th seal to collect.** The Plane of Madness is Azathoth's home. No mortal returns from it with their sanity. The quest as the party understands it is impossible. The attempt to reach it is what triggers the purgatory encounter.

**"Mordenkainen" is Kas.** He has no knowledge of Azathoth and does not understand the true scale of what he is trying to intercept. He believes he can use the Rod of Seven Parts to disrupt Vecna's ritual and claim the power for himself. His guidance cannot be trusted, but he is not lying — he is simply operating with an incomplete picture.

**Vecna, Chataschesma, and the Daelkyr are all faces of Nyarlathotep.** Every villain the party has defeated was the same entity in a different mask. Defeating Vecna does not end the threat. It only closes this face.

**Gendrick's fate is sealed.** He is captured by Vecna in purgatory and killed in the act of scattering the seals. His death is the emotional hinge of the transition. The party loses their guide at the moment they need one most.

**The purgatory encounter structure:**
1. Party attempts to pass through the Xoriat manifest zone
2. They are pulled into purgatory — a liminal space between planes
3. Vecna has Gendrick and is taking the seals; party cannot intervene
4. Gendrick scatters the seals through the planar membranes — this opens him to Vecna's killing blow
5. Vecna is weakened; party fights him briefly but cannot kill him
6. Party is thrust out of purgatory into the opening of Vecna: Eve of Ruin
