### Room-by-Room Descriptions & Features

---

#### **Room 1: Grand Hall Entrance (Opulent)**

* **Visual**: Marble floors, gilded columns, enchanted chandeliers casting dancing lights.
* **Encounter**: 2 *Phiarlan Bladesingers (CR 5)* act as subtle guardians.
* **Trap**: Stepping off the red carpet triggers a silent alarm (Perception DC 15).

---

#### **Room 2: Hall of Masks (Opulent Gallery)**

* **Visual**: Gallery walls lined with ornate, enchanted masks.
* **Puzzle**: Only the “truthful mask” (no reflection) opens the next door. Investigate DC 16.
* **Consequence**: Wrong mask causes psychic damage (WIS Save DC 15 or 2d8 + Confusion 1 round).

---

#### **Room 3: Arcane Conservatory (Lush Transition)**

* **Visual**: Glass-domed greenhouse with glowing psychic flora.
* **Trap**: Psychic spores—DC 15 CON save or suffer hallucination (disadvantage on all attacks for 1 round).
* **Challenge**: Need Druidcraft or Arcana (DC 14) to prune vine blocking exit.
* **Enemy**: 2 *Verdant Illusions* (reskinned Will-o’-Wisps, CR 2).

---

#### **Room 4: Hall of Echoing Dreams (Surreal Transition)**

* **Visual**: Corridor of mirrors reflecting distorted party images.
* **Trap**: Three mirror portals send characters into psychic labyrinths (WIS Save DC 15 or lose 1 minute in stasis).
* **Enemies**: 3 *Psi-Hounds (CR 3)* that phase through mirrors to ambush.

---

#### **Room 5: Shadow Dance Hall (Shadowy)**

* **Visual**: Dimly lit practice hall with shifting illusions and mirrored floors.
* **Encounter**:

  * 2 *Shadow Dancers (CR 5)* using illusion-enhanced moves.
  * 1 *Phiarlan Magewright (CR 6)* casting supportive spells.
* **Dynamic**: Illusory duplicates (Mirror Image effect) active at battle start.

---

#### **Room 6: Treasury Antechamber (Shadowy)**

* **Visual**: Vault-like room with statues in dramatic poses.
* **Puzzle**: Set statues to correct theatrical poses indicated by inscription (Investigation DC 17).
* **Consequence**: Incorrect alignment triggers psychic shockwave (DEX Save DC 15, 3d8 Psychic).

---

#### **Room 7: Sealed Vault Chamber (Deep Shadow)**

* **Visual**: Circular chamber, low lighting, magic circle and pedestal with Dream Shard.
* **Encounter**:

  * 1 *Phiarlan Magewright (CR 6)* with 2 *Animated Armors (CR 1 each)*.
* **Puzzle**: Removal of shard requires dispelling the circle (Arcana DC 18), or the shard shatters into motes, requiring a short rest for repair.

---

#### **Room 8: Emergency Passage (Foreshadowing Exit)**

* **Visual**: Narrow, dark corridor descending deeper, dim torchlight.
* **Hook**: The Inspired Assassin phasing in—visible briefly through wall as warning for Session 2.

## How to Use This Map

### VTT Version

* Use the provided map image or a custom art inspired by it.
* Overlay with grid (5 ft. squares).
* Create fog-of-war revealing deeper rooms as players progress.
* Adjust lighting: bright in Rooms 1–3; flickering and dim by Rooms 5–8.

### Printable Version

* Print the layout on multiple A4 pages or a fold-out.
* Offer player maps for Rooms 1–3; GM-only map reveals deeper areas as players move.
* Annotate trap/puzzle locations lightly or via separate note page.
