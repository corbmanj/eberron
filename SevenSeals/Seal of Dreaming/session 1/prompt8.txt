## **Expanded Vault Layout**

**Theme:** Illusion magic, psychic defenses, elegant traps.
**Size:** 8 rooms connected by hidden corridors and magical barriers.
**Goal:** Recover the Dream Shard before Inspired spies intervene.

---

### **Room 1: Grand Hall Entrance**

* **Purpose:** Entry point; ornate and disarming.
* **Challenge:**

  * Illusion of a gala performance; guests (illusions) may "notice" intruders if they interact incorrectly (Insight DC 14 to realize illusion).
* **Guardian:** 2 *Phiarlan Bladesingers (CR 5)* patrol subtly.
* **Trap:** Stepping off red carpet triggers Silent Alarm (Perception DC 15 to see faint shimmer).

---

### **Room 2: Hall of Masks**

* **Purpose:** Gallery of enchanted masks whispering secrets.
* **Puzzle:** Correct mask must be worn to open next door. Wrong masks impose random psychic effects (Wisdom Save DC 15 or 2d8 Psychic damage and Confusion for 1 round).
* **Clue:** Investigation DC 16 reveals the “truthful mask” has no reflection.

---

### **Room 3: Hall of Echoing Dreams**

* **Challenge:** Mirror maze with moving reflections.
* **Trap:** Some mirrors lead to psychic mazes (Wisdom Save DC 15 or lose 1 minute in dream stasis).
* **Enemies:** 3 *Psi-Hounds (CR 3)* phase in and out of mirrors.

---

### **Room 4: Arcane Conservatory**

* **Purpose:** Magical greenhouse with psychic flora.
* **Trap:** Psychic spores (Con Save DC 15) cause temporary hallucinations (disadvantage on attacks).
* **Enemies:** 2 *Verdant Illusions* (homebrew or reflavored Will-o’-Wisps CR 2) feed on psychic energy.
* **Puzzle:** Must prune a psychic vine blocking door; requires Druidcraft or Arcana DC 14.

---

### **Room 5: Shadow Dance Hall**

* **Purpose:** Training hall for Shadow Dancers.
* **Encounter:** 2 *Shadow Dancers (CR 5)* plus one *Phiarlan Magewright (CR 6)* practice combat illusions.
* **Dynamic Combat:** Illusions duplicate enemies (each enemy has 1 mirror image active at start).

---

### **Room 6: Treasury Antechamber**

* **Puzzle:** Four statues of masked figures hold different theatrical poses.
* **Challenge:** Must match correct pose sequence based on an inscription (Investigation DC 17).
* **Consequence:** Incorrect sequence triggers psychic shockwave (Dex Save DC 15, 3d8 Psychic).

---

### **Room 7: Sealed Vault**

* **Purpose:** Dream Shard sits on levitating pedestal within magic circle.
* **Encounter:**

  * *Phiarlan Magewright* (CR 6) & 2 *Animated Armors (CR 1)* guard the shard.
* **Puzzle:** Removing shard without dispelling circle (Arcana DC 18) shatters it into psychic motes.

---

### **Room 8: Emergency Passage**

* **Purpose:** Escape tunnel or confrontation with Inspired Agent.
* **Hook:** As party exits, they glimpse a **Dreamshade Assassin (Inspired Operative)** phasing through the wall — foreshadowing Session 2.
