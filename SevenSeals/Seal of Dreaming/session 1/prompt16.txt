Prompt: what is the stat block for the Radiant Myrmidons

Response: Here's a custom **Radiant Myrmidon** stat block designed for CR ~5, keeping the feel of a magical guardian similar to a Helmed Horror but infused with radiant energy:

---

### Radiant Myrmidon

*Medium Construct, Lawful Neutral*

**Armor Class** 18 (plate armor)
**Hit Points** 93 (11d8 + 44)
**Speed** 30 ft.

---

**STR** 18 (+4)
**DEX** 11 (+0)
**CON** 18 (+4)
**INT** 10 (+0)
**WIS** 10 (+0)
**CHA** 10 (+0)

---

**Saving Throws** Con +7, Wis +3
**Damage Resistances** Radiant; Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from Nonmagical Attacks
**Damage Immunities** Poison, Psychic
**Condition Immunities** Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Frightened, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned
**Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
**Languages** Understands the languages of its creator but can't speak
**Challenge** 5 (1,800 XP)

---

### Traits

**Magic Resistance.** The Radiant Myrmidon has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

**Illuminating Aura.** The Myrmidon sheds bright light in a 20-foot radius and dim light for an additional 20 feet. Creatures that start their turn within 10 feet of the Myrmidon take **5 (1d10) radiant damage**.

**Immutable Form.** The Myrmidon is immune to any spell or effect that would alter its form.

---

### Actions

**Multiattack.** The Myrmidon makes two longsword attacks.

**Radiant Longsword.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 10 (1d10 + 5) slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) radiant damage.

**Solar Flare (Recharge 5–6).** The Myrmidon emits a burst of radiant energy. Each creature of its choice within 15 feet must make a DC 15 Constitution saving throw, taking 21 (6d6) radiant damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one. Creatures that fail are also blinded until the start of their next turn.
