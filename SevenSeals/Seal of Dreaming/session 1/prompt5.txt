Prompt: why is she being hunted by the Inspired Assassin?

Response: ### Why the Inspired Assassin Hunts Her

The **Dreaming Dark** (the Quori conspiracy in Dal Quor) wants to maintain total control over the Plane of Dreams. Kalashtar like her are living rebellions — bonded with rogue Quori spirits that oppose the Dreaming Dark. Eliminating a Kalashtar not only removes a threat but also weakens the resistance.

---

### **Specific Reasons for the Hunt**

1. **She Holds Forbidden Knowledge**

   * Her Quori spirit remembers the location of ancient gateways into Dal Quor — including where the **Seal of Dreaming** was hidden.
   * If the Dreaming Dark kills her, that knowledge dies with her.
   * If they capture her, they can extract it and destroy the seal before the party can retrieve it.

2. **She Is Spiritually Unstable**

   * Her Quori spirit has been damaged by repeated nightmare incursions.
   * The Dreaming Dark hopes to corrupt it, turning her into an Inspired — a willing or unwilling host for a Quori of their choosing.

3. **She Was Part of a Failed Resistance Mission**

   * Years ago, she and a small cell of Kalashtar tried to retrieve another artifact tied to the seals.
   * The mission failed. The others were killed or captured. She escaped.
   * The assassin was assigned to “finish the job.”

---

### **The Assassin’s Unique Role**

* The assassin is not just a killer — they’re also a Dreaming Dark agent capable of infiltrating her dreams to weaken her mentally before attacking physically.
* They may appear in multiple guises:

  * A whispering shadow in her dreams.
  * An assassin in the waking world.
  * A charming, seemingly innocent NPC who gets close to her before striking.

---

### **Adventure Hooks from the Hunt**

1. **The Assassin is Tracking Her to the Party**

   * The longer the party spends around her, the closer the assassin gets.
   * Creates urgency to secure the Dream Anchor and perform the ritual before the assassin strikes.

2. **Dream Duel**

   * In one session, the assassin drags the party into a shared dream combat where reality bends.
   * If they fail, the Kalashtar suffers permanent psychic damage — weakening the ritual or dooming her entirely.

3. **Waking Confrontation**

   * Final encounter before Dal Quor entry: a deadly fight against the assassin and summoned nightmare beasts.
   * If the assassin is killed, the Dreaming Dark still knows someone is coming, but loses its best tracker.
