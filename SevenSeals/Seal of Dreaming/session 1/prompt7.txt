House Phiarlan's enclave is a blend of elegance and secrecy. On the surface, it appears to be a grand performing arts hall and gallery — a place of fine culture, music, and drama. ## **2. Ways to Gain Access to the Hidden Vault**

### **A. The Social Way – Infiltration by Invitation**

* The vault is opened only for high-value buyers or House operatives.
* The party can:

  * Pose as wealthy collectors or agents for a powerful patron (Deception/Persuasion DC 17–20).
  * Use forged credentials (Forgery Kit + Performance/Deception checks).
  * Perform a service or gift (rare art piece, performance, or information about a rival house) to earn a private showing of “special artifacts.”

### **B. The Subtle Way – Backstage Access**

* Phiarlan theaters contain trapdoors and shifting illusion panels.
* A sympathetic NPC (Lethyn) can smuggle the party backstage (Stealth DC 15) where a service tunnel leads to the vault’s outer door.
* They must avoid detection by:

  * **Mage-Sight Wards** (Arcana DC 16 to identify).
  * **Illusory Doppelgangers** patrolling hallways.

### **C. The Direct Way – Breaking & Entering**

* Locate the vault entrance via magical detection or interrogation of a minor Phiarlan agent.
* Requires bypassing:

  * A **Glyph of Warding** (Perception DC 18, Dispel Magic DC 15, or Thieves’ Tools DC 20 to disable).
  * Silent but swift neutralization of two guards (Stealth DC 17 or quick combat).
