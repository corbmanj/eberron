Prompt: what monster stat block would you recommend for the phiarlan bladesingers in room 1?

Response: For **House Phiarlan Bladesingers** (elite elven duelists), you can build from existing stat blocks with light modifications to fit their mix of grace, magic, and deadly precision.

### **Base Stat Block Recommendation**

* **Starting Point:** *Swashbuckler* (Volo’s Guide to Monsters, p. 217)
* **Adjustments:**

  * Add **1–2 minor spells** (Mirror Image, Misty Step, Shield).
  * Give them a **Rapier** or **Elven Thinblade** with finesse and possible poison.
  * AC slightly higher (AC 17–18) due to magical agility or light magical armor.
  * Hit Points: ~85–100 (CR 5–6) for elite duelists.

### **Custom Bladesinger (CR 6)**

* **AC:** 18 (Mage Armor + Dex + Bladesong)
* **HP:** 95 (10d8+40)
* **Speed:** 40 ft.
* **STR 12 / DEX 18 / CON 14 / INT 16 / WIS 12 / CHA 14**
* **Saving Throws:** DEX +7, INT +6
* **Skills:** Acrobatics +7, Stealth +7, Perception +4
* **Multiattack:** 3 rapier attacks (+7 to hit, 1d8+4 piercing).
* **Bladesong (Recharges on Short Rest):** Bonus Action; lasts 1 minute; adds +2 AC, +10 speed, advantage on Acrobatics.
* **Spellcasting:** DC 14; At will – *Mage Hand, Minor Illusion*; 3/day – *Mirror Image, Misty Step, Shield*.
* **Parry:** Reaction; +3 AC against one melee attack.
