# 🧠⚔️ ENCOUNTER 3: **THE KEEPER OF THE DREAM KEYSTONE**

## Core Concept Recap (Refined)

* A **minion of Chatechysma** is draining the dreaming minds of **12 captive Kalashtar**.
* Each Kalashtar is bound to a stone dream-table, eyes pinned open, tethered to a massive **Dream Keystone Door**.
* The party must:

  * **Fight the minion**
  * **Enter individual dreams**
  * **Successfully align 5 dreamers**
  * **Before 8 die**
* Each failed alignment:

  * Kills the dreamer
  * Deals **2d10 psychic damage** to the dreaming PC
* Once **5 are aligned**, the door *almost* opens—but requires **one final psychic anchor**, triggering Sah’reth’s sacrifice.

---

# 🏛️ THE BOSS ARENA (Quick Layout)

* Room Size: **80 ft x 80 ft**
* Features:

  * 12 Dream Tables arranged in two semicircles
  * Central platform where the **Minion of Chatechysma** floats, tethered by fleshy psychic cords to all 12 dreamers
  * A massive sealed **Dream Keystone Door** behind it
* Environmental Effect:

  * Every round on Initiative 20:
    **Psychic Pulse** — anyone concentrating must make a **DC 14 CON save** or lose concentration

---

# 👁️ THE DREAMERS MECHANIC (MAIN OBJECTIVE LOOP)

### Entering a Dream

* A PC must use **their Action** while adjacent to a dream table.
* Their body goes limp, eyes glowing silver.
* They vanish into the dream for **1 round**.

### The Alignment Check (Next Round)

At the start of their next turn, they make **one skill check of their choice**, flavored as how they stabilize the dream:

Examples:

* Persuasion – Convince the dreamer they aren’t alone
* Arcana – Stabilize the psychic resonance
* Religion – Call on ancestral quori spirits
* Insight – Identify the fear shaping the nightmare
* Performance – Re-write the dream’s tone
* Athletics – Physically fight the nightmare form

**DC Scaling:**

* First 2 dreams: **DC 13**
* Next 2 dreams: **DC 15**
* Final required dream: **DC 17**

---

### 🎯 Outcomes

✅ **Success**

* The dreamer exhales, eyes gently close
* A silver thread snaps free from the boss
* That dreamer now:

  * **Projects disadvantage on the boss’s next saving throw**
  * Grants the party **1 Dream Charge**

❌ **Failure**

* The dreamer screams once, then falls silent
* Their body disintegrates into drifting ash
* The PC is violently ejected:

  * **2d10 Psychic Damage**
  * **Disadvantage on their next saving throw**
* The boss immediately regains **15 HP**
