# ✅ **3. Dream Infiltration Mechanics (Your System – Cleaned & Balanced)**

You already have a great core idea. Here’s a rules-tightened version:

### 🔹 Entering a Dream:

* Requires **1 Action**
* You vanish from the battlefield until your next turn
* Your body is helpless while inside

### 🔹 Inside the Dream (Next Round):

Player makes **one skill check** based on their approach:

* Persuasion – Emotional reconciliation
* Insight – Understanding the trauma
* Performance – Rewriting the dream narrative
* Religion – Spiritual grounding
* Medicine – Mental stabilization
* Intimidation – Dominating the nightmare

**DC starts at 15 and increases by +1 for every failed dream attempt so far**

---

### ✅ **Success:**

* Dreamer is **aligned**
* They are gently freed
* Player returns safely
* Boss immediately:

  * Loses 1 power tier
  * Takes **20 psychic damage**

---

### ❌ **Failure:**

* Dreamer **dies**
* Player is **ejected violently**
* Takes **2d10 psychic damage**
* Boss immediately:

  * Heals 25 HP
  * Gains advantage on its next attack

---

### 🧮 **Victory & Failure Conditions**

* ✅ **Party wins when 5 dreamers are aligned**
* ❌ **If 8 die before that → Instant failure state**

  * Seal is corrupted or lost
