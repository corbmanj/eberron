## ⚔️ **Inverted Battle Encounter: “Reflected Host”**

### 💀 Composition (Balanced for 5 level 8 PCs)

* **2 × Helmed Horrors** (MM p. 183) → *Reflavored as Mirror Golems*
* **1 × Wraith** (MM p. 302) → *Reflavored as Inverted Wraith*
* Optional: **1d4 Shadows** (MM p. 269) entering in Round 3 from the “mirrors” to increase tension if the party breezes through.

This should create a **medium-to-hard** encounter depending on tactics and terrain.

---

### 🪞 **Mirror Golems (Helmed Horror variant)**

Use the **Helmed Horror** stat block as-is but change the following for flavor:

**Resistances:** Instead of choosing any three spells to ignore, the Mirror Golems have:

* **Resistance:** Psychic; Nonmagical bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing
* **Immune:** Fire, Poison
* **Special:** *Reflective Plating.* When targeted by a ranged spell attack, the caster must make a DC 15 Wisdom save or the attack instead targets themselves.

**Appearance:** Their armor is a mosaic of shifting mirror shards, reflecting distorted versions of the heroes. Their movements leave trails of refracted light and echoing whispers of the PCs’ voices.

**Dream Realm Flavor:** When struck, they shatter momentarily into a thousand shards before reforming, screaming with the player’s own voice.

---

### 🌑 **Inverted Wraith (Wraith variant)**

Use the **Wraith** stat block with these adjustments:

**Resistances:**

* Swap **resistance to necrotic** with **vulnerability to necrotic**
* Gains **resistance to radiant**

**New Trait — Reversal Aura (10 ft.):**
Creatures starting their turn within 10 ft. of the Inverted Wraith must make a DC 15 Wisdom save or suffer *inverted senses* until the start of their next turn (disadvantage on attack rolls, but advantage on Insight/Perception).

**Flavor:**
The Inverted Wraith appears as a flickering silhouette in reverse colors — light where there should be shadow, eyes like glowing voids. It speaks in backwards echoes of the players’ words.

**Tactics:**

* Begins the fight by draining a Mirror Golem’s essence (regaining HP).
* Uses **Life Drain** primarily on inverted characters (those with low CON or Wisdom).
* Focuses on isolating a single target while illusions of it flicker around the battlefield.

---

### 🧩 **Dream Terrain Features (for the 60×60 arena)**

* **Mirrored Floor:** Ranged attacks that miss by 5 or less ricochet; roll again to see if they hit another creature (1–3 enemy, 4–6 ally).
* **Gravity Flux Zones:** 10-ft-radius areas where gravity flips vertically each round.
* **Mirror Pillars (AC 15, HP 30):** Breaking one reduces the Wraith’s AC by 1 but also deals 10 psychic damage to all nearby creatures as reality distorts.

---

### 🌙 **End of the Battle**

As the Inverted Wraith dissolves, the mirrored floor liquefies into rippling silver light. The reflections of the heroes bow, then melt into their feet — returning them to their true forms.
A whisper echoes:

> “To know yourself, you must first be another.”

They awaken in a circular platform suspended in void, ready to face the **Trial of Emotion** or **Dream Logic**, whichever comes next.
