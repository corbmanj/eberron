Response: Below is a **tight, fast-running encounter** designed to minimize how long the players must run each other’s characters, while still delivering the “Inversion” theme and providing a fun mix of combat + skill challenges.

---

# **Encounter: The Hall of Reversed Selves**

## **Environment**

A circular, mirrored chamber (40–60 ft across). Every wall is a reflection, but each mirror shows **a different party member**, not the one who is looking into it.
When the PCs step inside, their reflections **step out of the mirrors**, and the characters abruptly find their minds inside another party member’s body (swap sheets).

Ambient effects:

* Sound echoes backward.
* Reflections move half a second *too fast*.
* Footsteps feel delayed.

The moment the swap happens, **four crystalline mirror-pillars** around the room hum to life.
Each contains a swirling shard of dreamstuff and acts as a **switch/spawn point**.

---

# **Objective**

🔹 **Disable all four switches** (skill checks)
🔹 **Defeat any remaining minions**
🔹 **Break the inversion**

Minions spawn every round until their switch is disabled, ensuring urgency and keeping the fight short.

---

# **The Four Switches (Skill Challenges)**

Each switch is a mirrored pillar with glowing runes. Disabling one requires a PC (in someone else’s body!) to use one of the following checks:

### **Switch 1 — Insight (DC 15): The Mirror of Truth**

The pillar reads your “reflected thoughts.”
A correct interpretation dispels it.
Fail by 5+ → you take 1d8 psychic damage.

### **Switch 2 — Athletics (DC 16): The Mirror of Force**

Must physically shatter a psychic barrier.
Disadvantage if the body you’re in has low STR.
Fail → stunned until the end of your next turn.

### **Switch 3 — Arcana (DC 14): The Mirror of Logic**

Reality “glitches.”
You must attune to the dreamweave and invert the runes.
Fail → wild magic-like surge (GM chooses a harmless glitch).

### **Switch 4 — Persuasion (DC 15): The Mirror of Identity**

You must convince the mirror who you “really” are.
Perfect for swapping sheets.
Fail → mirror creates a temporary illusion of an enemy (no damage, but wastes time).

**Each switch disabled removes its corresponding spawn point.**

---

# **Minion Spawn Points**

Each active switch spawns **1 minion at initiative 20 (top of round)**.
Minions step out of the mirrors like reflections walking through glass.

Use existing MM stat blocks to keep it simple:

### **Spawn 1: 1 × Shadow (MM p. 269)**

Associated with the Insight switch.
Fast, annoying, low HP.

### **Spawn 2: 1 × Flying Sword (MM p. 20)**

From the Athletics switch.
Represents weaponry coming alive.

### **Spawn 3: 1 × Specter (MM p.279)**

From the Arcana switch.
Dream-ghost flickering between reflections.

### **Spawn 4: 1 × Mirror Mephit (reskinned *Magma Mephit*, MM p.216)**

From the Persuasion switch.
No fire—swap to psychic damage.

This keeps the fight dynamic without overwhelming the swapped PCs.

---

# **Combat Flow (Fast 3–5 Round Encounter)**

### **Round 1**

* Swap characters.
* First set of 4 minions spawn.
* PCs scramble to understand new abilities.
* 1–2 switches likely disabled this round.

### **Round 2**

* Remaining switches spawn new minions.
* Players begin to settle into the chaos.
* 1–2 more switches disabled.

### **Round 3**

* Final switch spawns.
* After it’s disabled, **no more minions spawn**.
* Last few minions get mopped up.

### **End**

All remaining mirrors shatter inward like imploding glass. The character swaps end instantly, and they return to their own bodies in a swirl of dreamstuff.

---

# **Encounter Narrative Ending**

As the final mirror-pillar cracks apart, every reflection in the chamber freezes, stares directly at its corresponding PC, and whispers:

**“Know yourself. Know another. Know the dream.”**

Then the mirrored walls cascade into liquid silver, draining into the floor.
Bodies and minds snap back into alignment with a jarring lurch.
The path to the next chamber reveals itself as the room dissolves.
