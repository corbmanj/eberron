Prompt: one of the characters died in the dream world. I would like him to come back with some kind of effect, can you provide some options?

Response: Here are **clean, GM-friendly options** for a character who *died in the Dream Realm and returns changed.* These range from mild flavor to meaningful mechanical impacts. Pick whichever best matches the tone you want.

---

# ✅ **Category A: Small, Flavorful Boons**

### **1. Echo of the Dream**

Once per long rest, he may ask a single yes/no question before rolling a d20.
On a **15+**, he receives a vague, dreamlike hint about the outcome.

### **2. Residual Dreamstuff**

His eyes briefly glow while concentrating or sleeping.
He no longer needs somatic/material components for **one cantrip** of your choice.

### **3. Softened Ego**

He remembers dying in a place where identity fades.
Advantage on **Insight checks** for 24 hours.

---

# ✅ **Category B: Mechanical Shifts With Upside + Downside**

### **4. Dreamscarred**

**+1 AC for 1 round** after taking psychic damage,
but he also takes **+1 psychic damage** from all sources.

### **5. Dal Quor’s Breath**

Gain **resistance to psychic damage**,
but he suffers **disadvantage on Wisdom saves vs. fear** as nightmare echoes linger.

### **6. Lucid Fragility**

He can cast **Sanctuary** on himself once per long rest.
However, the first time he drops below half HP in a fight, he becomes **vulnerable to psychic** until healed.

---

# ✅ **Category C: Larger Dream-Themed Transformations**

### **7. Returned With a Third Eye (invisible except in dim light)**

Gain **darkvision 30 ft** or +30 ft if he already has it.
Once per day, can use **Detect Thoughts** (save uses WIS).
NPCs may find him unsettling.

### **8. Dream Anchor Tether**

Once per long rest, when he falls to 0 HP, he instead drops to **1 HP** as dreamstuff holds him together.
After using this, he suffers **one level of exhaustion**.

### **9. Nightmare Infusion**

He gains a **Dreamstrike**:
Once per turn, add **1d4 psychic damage** to one attack.
But during long rests, he must succeed a DC 14 WIS save or gain no benefits due to nightmares.

---

# ✅ **Category D: Purely Narrative But Powerful Hooks**

### **10. The Dream Remembers Him**

He occasionally hears whispers in languages he doesn’t know.
These might provide:

* warnings,
* clues,
* or ominous foreshadowing.

### **11. The Quori Mark**

He now leaves faint footprints in dreamstuff, even in the real world (seen only with Detect Magic).
Quori may now **notice him more easily** in future dream encounters.

### **12. Time Lag**

He occasionally experiences 1–2 seconds of déjà vu.
Once per session, he may **reroll one ability check** because “he’s seen this before.”

---

If you want, I can **tailor the effect specifically to that PC’s class, backstory, or personality** to make it more meaningful.
