### **d12 Dreamscape Rooms**

1. **Gravity Well Chamber**

   * Floor and ceiling constantly switch places.
   * **Hazard:** Dex save DC 14 to avoid falling damage (2d6).
   * **Encounter:** 1–2 Dreamspawn clinging to the "walls" for ambush.

2. **Infinite Staircase**

   * A spiral staircase looping endlessly. PCs must make an **Investigation DC 15** to find the “real” landing.
   * **Complication:** On a failure, they lose 1 hour of dream-time (may trigger more Quori patrols).

3. **Mirror Corridor**

   * Hall lined with mirrors that distort reality.
   * **Puzzle:** Correct sequence of mirrors must be touched (Arcana DC 16).
   * **Trap:** Wrong mirror releases a psychic echo (2d10 psychic damage).

4. **Shifting Garden**

   * Fragments of Sah’reth’s memories bloom and wither.
   * **Roleplay:** PCs can learn lore about her fears/dreams.
   * **Encounter:** Dreamspawn vines restrain anyone who lingers (DC 15 Str to break).

5. **Endless Library**

   * Stacks of books with moving ladders.
   * **Puzzle:** Find a single book of prophecy. **Investigation DC 17** or suffer psychic overload (3d6 psychic damage, disadvantage on next Wisdom save).

6. **Hall of Masks**

   * Floating masks show alternate versions of the PCs (temptation visions).
   * **Will Save:** Wis DC 16 or gain one level of exhaustion (dream-corruption).

7. **Broken Clock Room**

   * Giant floating gears rotate at random intervals.
   * **Hazard:** Must time jumps (Dex DC 14) or take 2d8 bludgeoning.
   * **Reward:** Success provides clue to Dreaming Dark’s location.

8. **Reverse Ocean**

   * Ceiling is a swirling ocean, floor is glass.
   * **Hazard:** Waves crash downward when gravity flips. Con save DC 14 or be knocked prone and pulled into ceiling-water.

9. **Labyrinth of Whispers**

   * Walls whisper secrets and lies.
   * **Puzzle:** Wis DC 15 to distinguish truth. Failure imposes confusion (1 min).

10. **Astral Menagerie**

    * Dream-formed versions of celestial beasts roam.
    * **Encounter:** 1–2 Quori “ride” these creatures as mounts.

11. **The Forgotten Nursery**

    * Crumbling dream-memory of Sah’reth’s childhood.
    * **Roleplay & Horror:** A twisted “imaginary friend” (Dreamspawn) hunts them here.

12. **The Nightmare Core**

    * Heart of the dreamscape, swirling with psychic energy.
    * **Boss Ambush:** Inspired Assassin manifests here if not yet encountered.
