## **Dreamrender Gauntlet**

*Rare, requires attunement by a Barbarian or Kalashtar*

### **Description**

This sleek gauntlet of silver and dark crystal pulses faintly, resonating with the dream-essence of Dal Quor. Faint whispers echo in the wielder’s mind when they grip it tightly.

---

### **Properties**

1. **Psychic Strike**

   * Once per turn, when you hit with an unarmed strike or melee weapon attack while raging, deal an additional **1d6 psychic damage**.

2. **Dreamstep (1/Short Rest)**

   * As a bonus action, you can partially phase into the dreamscape for 6 seconds.
   * During this time, you gain **resistance to all damage except force** and can move through creatures as if they were difficult terrain.

3. **Quori Recoil**

   * When you take psychic damage, the gauntlet stores part of it.
   * On your next successful melee hit, you can release this energy to deal extra damage equal to **half the psychic damage taken** (rounded up).

4. **Awakened Rage** *(optional upgrade if they complete a future quest)*

   * When raging, advantage on Wisdom saving throws against dream or fear effects.

---

### **Flavor**

* Etched with glowing patterns that shift like flowing ink.
* When the barbarian enters rage, the patterns flare with purple light, echoing Dal Quor’s energy.
