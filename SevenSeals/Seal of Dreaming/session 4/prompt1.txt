Prompt: The party defeated the evil quori and at the last second grasped hands with the new Kalashtar in the party and were teleported to Dal Quor. Describe for me what they see when they first arrive in Dal Quor.

Response: Here’s a vivid arrival description you can narrate to immerse your players as they first set foot in **Dal Quor, the Region of Dreams**:

---

The moment your vision clears, silence falls — vast, absolute, and wrong. You stand on a surface that looks like glass and feels like memory: smooth, faintly warm, and rippling with faint echoes of color beneath your feet. Overhead, the sky churns with slow-moving constellations that rearrange themselves like living thoughts.

A pale horizon stretches infinitely in every direction, yet distances feel deceptive — what looks a mile away seems, at a blink, to drift closer, or farther, or vanish entirely. The air hums with a low vibration, as if the world itself is dreaming.

The ground beneath you shivers, and shapes begin to coalesce around you — fragments of dreams bleeding together: a half-built tower that spirals downward instead of up; trees with lanterns instead of leaves; a silver river flowing uphill into a dark cloud. Every few heartbeats, the landscape flickers and rearranges, like a restless sleeper shifting beneath the sheets.

Off in the distance, a faint light pulses — steady, rhythmic, the only constant in a realm where even your own footprints fade when you look away. The Kalashtar’s dream anchor glows softly in her hand, pulling toward that beacon, and her expression is grim.

“This is the border,” she murmurs, voice echoing twice — once in the air, once inside your mind. “The Quori will know we’re here. The dreaming dark is never far behind.”

Then the ground exhales, and a mist of luminous motes rises around you — memories, maybe, or lost dreams. A thousand whispering voices drift by, murmuring snatches of forgotten lives. You are in Dal Quor now, where thought shapes reality, and every fear or desire has weight.

---
