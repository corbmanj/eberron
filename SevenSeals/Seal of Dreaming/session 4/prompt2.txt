### 🎭 Round-by-Round Flow

**Round 1:**

* Four guards and two thugs confront the party from both directions, claiming they’re “under arrest for gambling fraud.”
* They draw clubs and batons, not blades (yet). If the party doesn’t surrender, combat starts.

**Round 2:**

* Reinforcements arrive — one more thug and the two dog handlers (with dogs) appear from the alley or rooftops.
* The Enforcer blows his whistle, giving all allies +2 to hit this round.

**Round 3:**

* Once **any PC is restrained**, Grelnas himself steps into view.

  * Half-elf, dressed in clean but worn officer’s garb, a dull silver badge glinting.
  * Sneers: “You should’ve stayed in your hole, Ozzy.”
  * He doesn’t fight yet — just watches and taunts until the end of this round.

**Round 4+:**

* If the Enforcer drops or the tide turns, Grelnas joins the fight, using **Gladiator** (MM p. 346) stats with the following tweaks:

  * **AC 17, HP 130.**
  * Damage type: nonlethal baton strikes (bludgeoning).
  * **Reactions:** “Commanding Presence” – when an ally misses an attack, he can yell a command to let them reroll once per round.

### 🪞Dream Adjustments

Because this is **a dream-memory**, you can lean into surreal touches as the battle escalates:

* Each time a PC takes damage, the world flickers between now and the past — glimpses of what “really” happened.
* When the first dog dies, it dissolves into black smoke.
* If Ozzy is knocked unconscious, the dream begins to “rewind,” forcing the others to protect him or risk the memory looping endlessly.
