Prompt: There are 5 lvl 8 PCs now, how should I scale it up

Response: Excellent — with 5 level 8 PCs, this ambush can and *should* feel bigger, tenser, and more chaotic.
We’ll scale it by adding more attackers, giving the enemies slightly better tactics, and adding one new “elite” element (a corrupt mage supporting the lawmen).

Here’s the adjusted and rebalanced version:

---

## ⚖️ Encounter Balance Overview

**Party:** 5 level 8 PCs (medium–hard difficulty goal)
**Total Enemies:** 10 humanoids + 2 dogs
**XP Budget:** ~11,000–12,000 XP total (Hard)

---

## ⚔️ The Crooked Watch — Scaled for 5 PCs

| Enemy Type                       | Count                   | Role / Notes                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| -------------------------------- | ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Grelnas’s Enforcer (Captain)** | 1                       | Veteran (MM p. 350), HP 90, AC 17. Add *Whistle Command* (bonus action, allies gain +2 to hit until his next turn).                                                                                                  |

| **Corrupt Mage**                 | 1                       | Use *Mage* (MM p. 347) but change spell list to feel “lawful but cruel”: **hold person, shield, scorching ray, counterspell, invisibility**. HP 50. The mage focuses on locking down 1–2 PCs while the guards swarm. |

### 🩸 Pacing and Arrival

**Round 1:**

* 4 Guards + 2 Thugs block the party’s path.
* The Enforcer shouts: “By order of the Crown Watch, you’re under arrest!”
* Dogs growl from the shadows — you can hint they’re there but not release them yet.

**Round 2:**

* 2 more Guards arrive from behind.
* The Corrupt Mage appears on a rooftop or balcony, casting *Hold Person* or *Web*.
* Dogs and handlers rush in from the sides.

**Round 3:**

* Once the first PC is restrained, Grelnas himself steps out.

  > “You’re a hard man to kill, Ozzy. Let’s see if you bleed this time.”
* Grelnas doesn’t enter melee until Round 4 unless attacked directly.

**Round 4+:**

* Grelnas joins the fray.
* If morale drops (3+ enemies down), the Enforcer calls for retreat — but Grelnas refuses, shouting:

  > “No one leaves until the demon’s dead!”

---

### 🪞 Dream-Specific Adjustments

* Every time the party kills an enemy, they dissolve into *ash and memory threads*. Their faces blur into those of strangers.
* If a PC falls to 0 HP, they see flashes of *Rudy burning* — the dream threatens to reset. Another PC can spend their action to “anchor” the memory (DC 15 Wisdom check).
* If Ozzy lands the killing blow on Grelnas, time slows — the party sees the “real” Grelnas, terrified, whispering *“We were told you’d live forever…”* before fading away.

---

### 🧩 Optional Scaling Levers (if the fight goes too easy)

* Give the Corrupt Mage *Mirror Image* already active.
* Add one **Guard Reinforcement** at the start of Round 3 (just a Thug or Bandit Captain).
* Add “moral panic” — when 3+ enemies die, the Enforcer shouts for help and *2 more guards* arrive next round.
