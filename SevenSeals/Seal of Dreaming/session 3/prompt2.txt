## 🏰 The Boss Room: Chamber of Fractured Dream

* **Size:** 60-ft cube.
* **Description:** The walls are not stone, but flowing panes of dreamstuff — like liquid mirrors that ripple with distorted reflections. Gravity flickers every so often, briefly pulling creatures sideways or upward before snapping back (cosmetic, unless you want it as a mechanic). The floor is tiled glass, beneath which shadows writhe as if things move just out of sight.
* **Features:**

  * **Dreamlight Pillars (4 of them):** At the corners, crystalline pillars pulse with violet-blue energy. A creature within 10 ft. of a pillar has advantage on saving throws against being frightened, but disadvantage on saving throws against charm effects.
  * **Unstable Reality:** At initiative 20 (lair action slot), the room destabilizes. Roll 1d4:

    1. **Gravity Shift:** Everyone must succeed on DC 14 Dex save or be knocked prone.
    2. **Echo of Fear:** Random PC makes a DC 14 Wis save or be frightened until the end of their next turn.
    3. **Nightmare Pulse:** Each creature takes 1d6 psychic damage.
    4. **Calm Respite:** Everyone regains 1d6 HP.

*Medium humanoid (kalashtar), lawful evil*

**HP:** 120 (16d8+48)

**STR** 14 (+2) | **DEX** 18 (+4) | **CON** 16 (+3) | **INT** 14 (+2) | **WIS** 12 (+1) | **CHA** 18 (+4)

## 👾 Phase 2 Boss: **Tsoreva Quori Nightmare Stalker**

*Large aberration (quori), lawful evil*

**AC:** 17 (natural armor)
**HP:** 165 (22d10+44)
**Speed:** 40 ft., fly 40 ft. (hover)

**STR** 18 (+4) | **DEX** 16 (+3) | **CON** 15 (+2) | **INT** 14 (+2) | **WIS** 16 (+3) | **CHA** 20 (+5)

**Saving Throws:** Dex +7, Wis +7, Cha +9
**Skills:** Intimidation +9, Perception +7
**Damage Resistances:** Psychic; Bludgeoning, Piercing, Slashing from nonmagical weapons
**Condition Immunities:** Charmed, Frightened, Exhaustion
**Senses:** Darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 17
**Languages:** Quori, telepathy 120 ft.
**CR:** 10

---

### Traits

* **Aura of Dread.** Any creature that starts its turn within 20 ft. of the Quori must succeed on DC 16 Wis save or be frightened until the start of its next turn.
* **Dream Predator.** While a creature is frightened, the Quori has advantage on attack rolls against it.
* **Summon Tsucora (Recharge 5–6).** The Quori summons 1 Tsucora Quori, which acts immediately after the boss on the same initiative.

---

### Actions

* **Multiattack.** Two Claw attacks.
* **Claw.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +8 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 15 (2d10+4) slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) psychic damage.
* **Nightmare Blast (Recharge 4–6).** The Quori unleashes psychic energy in a 30-ft cone. Each creature in the area must make a DC 16 Wis save, taking 27 (6d8) psychic damage on a fail, or half on success. On a failed save, targets are also restrained by shadowy dream-chains until the end of their next turn.

---

### Bonus Actions

* **Slip Through Dreams (Recharge 3–6).** The Quori teleports up to 40 ft. to an unoccupied space it can see.
