# Seal of Dreaming - Summary

## Overview
A 6-session arc (Aug 30 - Dec 12, 2025) where the party (level 8, later 5 PCs) traveled to **Dal Quor, the Plane of Dreams** to retrieve the **Seal of Dreaming**. **Fulla**, a Kalashtar hunted by the Dreaming Dark, joined as their guide.

## Session 1 (Aug 30, 2025): Meeting Fulla & The Phiarlan Vault Heist
- Met **Fulla** at Gendrick's tower — a Kalashtar whose Dream Anchor had been stolen and sold to House Phiarlan
- Infiltrated the **House Phiarlan vault** in Thrane (a playhouse with a hidden vault)
  - **Lethin**, a performer, helped Finella sneak in via a stage performance (Finella killed it on stage)
  - Fancy man **Broggle** ("means potato") led them into the vault, then went unconscious stepping off the carpet
  - 11-room dungeon: mask puzzle (white/black/clown masks), arcane conservatory, shadow dancers, clockwork room with dragon, Beetlejuice-desert sun room with radiant armor
  - Dragon-head ring puzzle using collected items (rose, mask, pocketwatch, quill, knife)
- Fulla grabbed her Dream Anchor; **Ozzy grabbed a Dreamrender Gauntlet** (triggering an alarm)
- Entered Fulla's dreamscape — a labyrinth of her memories (bookshelf room, nursery with fake babies/Burbalangs, carousel with quori, gravity-flip ocean room, seasonal memory doors, mask room showing possible futures)

## Session 2 (Sep 21, 2025): Entering Dal Quor
- Dream effects: Ozzy aged (lost 1 hit die), Aerin had disadvantage on initiative, Tam had concentration penalty, Finella got psychic damage from a Quori whisper, Fulla got inspiration
- Fought 4 ambushers while sleeping in dreamscape
- Found a library with key books; Fulla's Dream Anchor led them through her mindscape
- Fought a **multi-phase Quori boss** in a pillar room with acid; Finella went down but Fulla saved her
- Fulla's pendant activated and transported them out

## Session 3 (Oct 8, 2025): Dal Quor — The Rite of Lucid Dreaming
- Arrived in Dal Quor proper — clear solid rock with swirling black/purple in a white void
- Met **Echo** — a mysterious figure at a desk (first appeared as bunnies) who administered the rites:
  1. **Rite of Lucid Dreaming**
  2. **Rite of Inversion**
  3. **Rite of Negotiation**
- **Rite of Lucid Dreaming (Ozzy's Memory):** Relived Ozzy's past as "Ned Heershan" (Ed Sheeran lookalike alias) at a bar in Zarash'ak
  - Rudy (donkey #1) was there; bartender Bernard called Ozzy "Ned"
  - **Grelnas** was the corrupt sheriff who ran Ozzy out of town and killed Rudy
  - Party was arrested by the "crown watch" — combat ensued
  - Ozzy **successfully changed his memory** — the past was altered

## Session 4 (Nov 9, 2025): The Rite of Inversion
- Party entered a mirrored room and **swapped bodies** (players swapped character sheets):
  - Josh played Finella, Kate played Ozzy, Dustin played Tam, Noelle played Aerin
- Destroyed 4 pillars: Arcana (Ozzy), Persuasion (Ozzy), Strength (Fulla), Insight (Aerin)
- **Tam died** during the combat (nat 1 on second death save; Finella also went down but Fulla saved her with a potion)
- Completed the Rite of Inversion

## Session 5 (Nov 20, 2025): Tam's Resurrection & The Dream Conductor
- Echo walked Tam through a contract for resurrection — Tam gained a scar and lost spell slots
- Echo noted something was wrong with his system; the Rite of Negotiation was replaced
- Fulla discovered a hallway with **12 Kalashtar with their eyes pinned open**
- **The Dream Conductor** (tongueless, voice projected psychically) was enslaving Kalashtar to align dreams and unlock the seal for Chatechesma
- Party freed dreamers one by one via skill checks:
  - Finella did a TikTok dance, Aerin used dungeon knowledge, Tam used river knowledge, Ozzy helped a child, etc.
  - Fulla tried two at once and took psychic damage
- Dream Conductor declared they had no living dream anchor
- **Fulla volunteered** — tendrils wrapped around her, the door opened
- Seal appeared as a **glowing blue d8**; floated to Tam
- Party woke up in Gendrick's library

## Session 6 (Dec 12, 2025): Return to Gendrick
- Woke in the library where they first met Gendrick
- Tam found a book (Krampus Clause); shared Candlenights memories
- Fought snakes in the stacks (Aarakocra librarian **Samala** gave Finella dirty looks)
- Reported to Gendrick about their adventures; handed over the seal
- Gendrick concerned about Tam's death scar — "I've seen this before, in the Mournlands. It's not good."
- Received a **dragonshard** from Gendrick
- **Leveled up to 9**
- Gendrick briefed them: Seal of Waking is next (Shae Mordai, Aerenal); Plane of Madness comes last

## Key Themes
- Dreams vs. reality and the fragile boundary between them
- Sacrifice and duty (Fulla's willing sacrifice)
- Confronting personal trauma (Ozzy's memory of Grelnas/Rudy)
- Identity and empathy (body-swapping, playing each other's characters)
- Death and its cost (Tam's resurrection contract, the scar from the Mournlands)
