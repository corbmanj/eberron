# Encounter: The Tunnel Patrol

---

### Encounter Summary

```
Difficulty:  Easy (this is a choice point, not a challenge)
Party:       4 players, level 11

Monster Roster:
  4× Devotee of the Spider Queen — CR 1/8

This encounter exists to offer a choice, not a threat. Four low-ranking cultists
walk into a dark tunnel where the party is already hidden. Whether the party
hides, ambushes, or fights openly matters much more than whether they win —
they will win. What changes is what they're carrying afterward.
```

---

## Monster Stat Block

### Devotee of the Spider Queen

*Medium humanoid (any race), chaotic evil*

*A low-ranking member of the Lolth cult that maintains Web's Edge — robes, a spider-shaped talisman, and not much else notable about them individually.*

---

**Traits:**

**Pack Tactics.** The devotee has advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the devotee's allies is within 5 ft of the creature and isn't incapacitated.

---

**Actions:**

**Scimitar.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +3 to hit, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d6+1) slashing damage.

---

**Tactics:**
- These four are unremarkable and don't know they're being watched. If surprised, they don't get to act in the first round.
- If a fight starts and it's going badly, the last one standing tries to flee back toward Web's Edge rather than fight to the death — let the party decide whether that's a problem.

---

**Loot:** Each carries robes and a spider-shaped talisman — together, enough disguise material for four people to pass as devotees (see "Impersonating Worshipers," Web's Edge). A coil of rope and two empty sacks between them (they were headed out to meet Lolth's embedded agents at a nearby mining operation, carrying word of a planned assault — see area W7). A few silver pieces each, nothing more.
