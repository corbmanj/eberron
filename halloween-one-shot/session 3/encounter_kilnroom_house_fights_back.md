# Encounter: The House Fights Back — The Kiln Room

---

### Encounter Summary

```
Difficulty:  Medium
Party:       4 players, level 8

Monster Roster:
  2× Carving Tool Swarm — CR 3 (estimated)
  Every Bound Doll not yet freed — CR 1/2 each, arriving from upstairs
  The kiln — hazard

The party has to dig while the house tries to stop them. The fight ends the moment the bones
are uncovered, which takes five successful digging checks. The swarms go after whoever is
digging. The kiln belches fire across the room every round. The dolls pour down the cellar
stairs from round 2 on. The key tension is how many characters dig versus how many defend.
```

---

### Scaling

The number of dolls depends on how many souls the party has already freed. Check the ledger.

| Dolls Still Bound | Adjustment |
|---|---|
| 10 or more | Only the first 8 dolls come. The rest are too far away. |
| 4 to 9 | Use them all. |
| 0 to 3 | Add a third Carving Tool Swarm. |

---

### Digging Rules

- **Five successes uncover the bones.** A character within 5 feet of the grave can take an action to dig. They make a **DC 12 Strength (Athletics)** check. On a success, mark one success.
- **Tools matter.** A character using the shovel or the pickaxe digs normally. A character digging by hand, or with a weapon, has Disadvantage.
- **Magic helps.** *Mold Earth* counts as one success each time it's cast on the grave, with no check. So does *Shape Water* or any similar spell the table agrees moves loose dirt. A summoned creature or familiar can dig using its own Strength.
- **When the fifth success is marked, the fight ends.** Every doll drops where it stands. The tools fall out of the air and clatter to the floor. The kiln door slams shut, and the fire inside goes out. Go to Beat 4 in the session document.

**Damage Immunities:** Poison, Psychic

**Traits:**

**Swarm.** The swarm can occupy another creature's space and vice versa. It can move through any opening large enough for a Tiny object. It can't regain hit points or gain temporary hit points.

**Tools, Not Monsters.** When the swarm drops to 0 hit points, the tools fall to the floor and lie still. They're ordinary tools again.

---

**Actions:**

**Carve.** *Melee Attack Roll:* +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 14 (4d6) Slashing damage, or 7 (2d6) Slashing damage if the swarm has half its hit points or fewer.

---

**Tactics:**
- The swarms go straight for whoever is digging. They move into the digger's space and attack every turn.
- If nobody is digging, they attack whoever is closest to the grave.
- They never retreat.

---

**Loot:** None. The tools are ordinary once they fall.

### Bound Doll

*Small Construct, Neutral*

*A toymaker's doll with a dead person's soul trapped behind its glass eyes. It attacks because the soul inside is in pain and because the man who made it still pulls the strings.*

| | |
|---|---|
| **AC** | 13 (porcelain and cloth) |
| **HP** | 22 (5d6 + 5) |
| **Speed** | 30 ft, Climb 30 ft |
| **STR** | 8 (−1) |
| **DEX** | 16 (+3) |
| **CON** | 12 (+1) |
| **INT** | 8 (−1) |
| **WIS** | 10 (+0) |
| **CHA** | 10 (+0) |

**Damage Immunities:** Poison, Psychic
**Condition Immunities:** Exhaustion, Frightened, Poisoned
**Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft, passive Perception 10
**Languages:** Understands Common. Speaks only in fragments, in the voice of the person inside.

---

**Traits:**

**Bound Soul.** When the doll drops to 0 hit points, it falls still but isn't destroyed. It gets back up the next time the grandfather clock strikes midnight, in a random room of the house. A fallen doll's soul can be freed in one of two ways:
- A creature within 5 feet takes an action to crush the doll's glass eyes.
- A creature speaks the doll's name over it, as written in the ledger. This doesn't take an action.

When a soul is freed, a small point of light rises out of the doll and fades. The doll never moves again.

**False Appearance.** While the doll is motionless, it looks like an ordinary doll.

**Swarm.** The doll has Advantage on attack rolls against a creature if at least two other Bound Dolls are within 5 feet of that creature.

**Whispers.** A Marked creature within 10 feet of the doll hears it whispering: its name, a memory, or a plea.

---

**Actions:**

**Sewing Needle.** *Melee Attack Roll:* +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Piercing damage.

**Clamber.** The doll climbs onto a Medium or larger creature within 5 feet. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 13. *Failure:* The doll clings to the target and moves with it. While clinging, the doll has Advantage on Sewing Needle attacks against that target. The target or an adjacent creature can take an action to pull the doll off, which requires a DC 13 Strength (Athletics) check. *Success:* The doll falls into an adjacent space.

---

**Tactics:**
- The dolls arrive at the start of round 2, coming down the cellar stairs. Two more arrive each round after that until they're all here.
- They go for the diggers first. They climb onto them and try to pull them away from the grave.
- They sing the rhyme while they fight.
- If the ledger is handy, remind the players that speaking a fallen doll's name frees it without an action. That's a big deal in this fight.

---

**Loot:** None. Each doll is porcelain, cloth, wood, and paint.

### The Kiln (Hazard)

The kiln door bangs open when the first brick is lifted, and the kiln fills with fire.

- **Heat.** A creature that starts its turn within 5 feet of the kiln takes 7 (2d6) Fire damage.
- **Flame Belch.** On initiative count 20, losing initiative ties, the kiln belches fire out of its door in a 15-foot Cone. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 14. *Failure:* 10 (3d6) Fire damage. *Success:* Half damage. The grave is inside the cone's area at its far edge. Anyone digging is in the line of fire.
- **Shutting the door.** A creature within 5 feet of the kiln can take an action to slam the iron door shut, with a **DC 15 Strength (Athletics)** check. On a success, the kiln skips its next Flame Belch. The door bangs open again at the start of the following round.

### Battle Map Description

> This map must be rendered in a strict top-down, directly overhead bird's-eye view. The camera is directly above looking straight down, as if the map were a floor plan. Not isometric. Not perspective. Not a three-quarter view. Directly overhead — the viewer is looking straight down at the ground.

**Map size:** 25×30 ft. The map covers a small stair hall in the north third and a kiln room in the south two-thirds of a stone basement.

**Terrain:**

- **Stair hall (north, 25×13 ft):** A plain stone floor. A wooden staircase, 8 ft wide, runs up along the east wall and out of the map at the north edge. There's an open doorway in the west wall leading off the map. An open doorway in the south wall, 5 ft wide, leads into the kiln room.

- **Kiln room (south, 25×17 ft):** A floor of old red bricks, worn smooth. The walls are rough stone.

- **The kiln:** A brick dome kiln against the east wall, about 8×10 ft and 8 ft tall. It has a heavy black iron door facing west, standing open, with bright orange fire roaring inside. The bricks around the door are scorched black. The kiln blocks movement.

- **The grave:** By the west wall, a 7×6 ft patch where bricks have been pried up and stacked neatly beside it, with dark packed dirt showing underneath. A shovel and a pickaxe lie next to it.

- **Carving tool pegs:** A row of empty wooden pegs along the south wall.

- **Workbench:** A small, low wooden bench along the south wall near the corner, about 6×2 ft. It provides half cover.

- **Coal chute:** A square iron hatch high on the east wall, north of the kiln, with a small spill of black coal on the floor below it.

- **Color and light:** The kiln fire is the only light in the kiln room. It throws strong orange light across the bricks and long shadows toward the west wall. The stair hall is dim, lit faintly from the doorway at the top of the stairs.
