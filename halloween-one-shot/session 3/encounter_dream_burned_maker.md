# Encounter: The Burned Maker — The Nightmare Nursery (Second Dream)

---

### Encounter Summary

```
Difficulty:  Hard
Party:       4 players, level 8 (only the characters who are asleep)

Monster Roster:
  1× The Burned Maker — CR 9 (estimated)

With his bones out of the ground, Tobias's soul is loose, and he's at full strength. He hunts
the Marked character first, teleports through the giant furniture with Dream Shift, and
reshapes the dream every round with a dream action. He can be hurt this time. When he drops to
0 hit points, he burns away and the dream ends. The party wins by driving him off. Watch
Nightmare Visage: a Frightened character in a dream that's closing in on them is in real trouble.
```

> **DM Note — Dream Rules:** Damage, spell slots, and item charges spent here are real. A character who drops to 0 hit points wakes up screaming, stable at 1 hit point, with 1 level of Exhaustion, and gets no benefit from the rest. Characters still standing when the Burned Maker is driven off get the full benefits of a long rest. See Dreams and Rest in the [README](../README.md). If every sleeper drops, the dream ends, and the party wakes without a long rest. He isn't destroyed either way.

### The Burned Maker

*Medium Undead, Neutral Evil*

*The soul of Tobias Marrowick, trapped in the dreams of anyone who sleeps in his house. He believes it's still the night of the fire and that everyone he meets is the mob.*

---

**Traits:**

**Master of the Dream.** The Burned Maker can't die in a dream. When he drops to 0 hit points, he burns away to ash, and the dream ends.

**Knows the Marked.** The Burned Maker always knows where the Marked character is, and he has Advantage on attack rolls against them.

**Legendary Resistance (2/Day).** If the Burned Maker fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.

---

**Actions:**

**Multiattack.** The Burned Maker makes three Carving Glove attacks.

**Carving Glove.** *Melee Attack Roll:* +8, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 13 (2d8 + 4) Slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) Psychic damage.

**Nightmare Visage (Recharge 5–6).** *(Spell-like — not counterspellable.)* The Burned Maker shows his burned face. *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 16, each creature in a 30-foot Cone. *Failure:* 27 (6d8) Psychic damage, and the target has the Frightened condition until the end of its next turn. *Success:* Half damage only.

---

**Bonus Actions:**

**Dream Shift.** The Burned Maker teleports up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space he can see, or through a wall or a piece of giant furniture to the space on its other side.

---

**Tactics:**
- He opens with Nightmare Visage on as many characters as he can catch in the cone.
- He goes for the Marked character with Carving Glove every turn he can reach them.
- He uses Dream Shift every turn. He appears behind the bed, on top of the toy chest, or right behind whoever just hurt him.
- He saves Legendary Resistance for anything that would stop him from acting, like a stun, a Hold spell, or Banishment.
- He talks the whole fight. "Put the torches down. Please. They're happy." "Rosie, go to your room." "Why are you in my house?"
- He never retreats. When he drops to 0 hit points, he burns away in a rush of smoke.

---

**Loot:** None. He's a dream.

---

### Dream Actions

On initiative count 20, losing initiative ties, the dream changes. Choose one of these. Don't use the same one two rounds in a row.

**The Walls Close In.** The walls of the nursery slide 5 feet inward on every side. Any creature within 5 feet of a wall is pushed 5 feet away from it. If there's no room, it takes 11 (2d10) Bludgeoning damage instead. After three uses, stop using this one. The room is small enough.

**The Toy Soldier Marches.** The giant wooden toy soldier in the corner swings its rifle in a 30-foot line, 10 feet wide. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 15, each creature in the line. *Failure:* 16 (3d10) Bludgeoning damage, and the creature has the Prone condition. *Success:* Half damage only.

**The Music Box.** The music box on the nightstand plays a lullaby. *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 15, each creature that can hear it, except the Burned Maker. *Failure:* The creature's Speed is 0 until the end of its next turn, as its eyes grow heavy. *Success:* No effect.

**The Drawings Watch.** The scribbled-out face of the man in the crayon drawings turns toward one creature. That creature has Disadvantage on its next attack roll or saving throw before the end of its next turn.

---

### How the Dream Ends

When the Burned Maker drops to 0 hit points:

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> He stops. He looks down at himself. Smoke is pouring off him now, thick and grey, like wet wood on a fire.
>
> "The torches," he says. "They're coming back with the torches."
>
> He comes apart into smoke and ash. The ash drifts down onto the enormous bed. The music box stops playing.
>
> The room goes dark.

Go to Beat 6 in the session document.

### Battle Map Description

> This map must be rendered in a strict top-down, directly overhead bird's-eye view. The camera is directly above looking straight down, as if the map were a floor plan. Not isometric. Not perspective. Not a three-quarter view. Directly overhead — the viewer is looking straight down at the ground.

**Map size:** 50×50 ft. A child's bedroom seen at giant scale, as if the viewer were the size of a mouse.

**Terrain:**

- **Ground surface:** Giant wooden floorboards, each about 5 ft wide, with gaps between them 1 ft across. A huge braided rag rug covers the middle of the room, about 25×20 ft, in faded reds and blues.

- **The bed (north side):** The leg and edge of a giant child's bed takes up the north third of the map. The bed frame is dark wood. A white quilt embroidered with huge yellow stars hangs down over the edge, touching the floor. The bed blocks movement. The space under it is a dark gap 5 ft high.

- **The toy chest (east side):** A giant wooden toy chest, about 15×10 ft and 10 ft tall, with its lid half open and toys spilling out. It provides full cover. The top is a climbable platform.

- **Wooden alphabet blocks (4):** Scattered giant wooden blocks, each about 5×5 ft and 5 ft tall, with painted letters on them. Each provides half cover.

- **The toy soldier (southwest corner):** A giant painted wooden toy soldier, lying on its side, about 20 ft long. It's in a red coat, with a wooden rifle. It provides full cover.

- **The nightstand (northwest):** The leg of a giant nightstand, with a wind-up music box sitting on the floor beside it. The music box is about 5×5 ft, painted blue, with its lid open and a tiny dancing figure on top.

- **The drawings (walls):** Along the west and south edges, the bottoms of enormous crayon drawings on paper, pinned to the wall. They show a big man and a small girl holding hands. The man's face is scribbled out in thick black crayon.

- **Color and light:** Dim blue moonlight falls across the floor from an unseen window to the east, casting long shadows from every giant object. The edges of the map fade into deep darkness. Faint smoke hangs in the air near the floor.

> Style: painterly and detailed, similar to a professional tabletop RPG battle map. Rendered strictly from directly above — top-down, no perspective, no isometric angle.
