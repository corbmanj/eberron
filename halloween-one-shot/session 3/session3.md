# Session 3: Under the Kiln Room

## The Maker's House — Part 3 of 4

*A session for 4 characters of 8th level. Estimated runtime: 3 to 3.5 hours.*

---

## Session Overview

The party explores the basement and gathers what the ritual needs: the mortar, the chalk, and one last pair of glass eyes. Then they dig up Tobias Marrowick's bones from under the kiln room floor. The house fights back. When the bones come out of the ground, his soul is loose, and the dreams get worse. The second dream isn't a memory. It's the Burned Maker at full strength, in a nightmare version of his daughter's room, and this time he can be driven off.

> **DM Note — Session 2 Recap:** The party explored the house, read Tobias's journal and the ritual page, and found the ledger of the dead. They found Rosie's empty doll and the cellar key. In the first dream, they watched the town burn Tobias, fought their way out of the mob, and saw the dolls bury his body under the kiln room floor. When they woke, ash footprints led them to the workshop, where the faceless doll stands still. Under its mask is a blank porcelain face with empty eye sockets.

## Beat 1: The Basement

Use [house_basement.svg](../maps/house_basement.svg). The party starts in the workshop, beside the faceless doll.

### The Workshop (Room 18)

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> The workshop is big, maybe thirty-five feet by forty-five, with a stone floor swept clean. A long workbench runs along the north wall, covered in tools laid out in neat rows. Chisels, knives, small saws, pots of glue. In the middle of the bench is a heavy stone mortar with a pestle standing in it.
>
> A wooden lathe stands against the east wall. A rack of porcelain arms and legs hangs from hooks by the door. In the southeast corner there's a tall cabinet with dozens of small drawers, each with a brass pull.
>
> The faceless doll stands in the middle of the floor, where you left it. It hasn't moved.

**What's here:**

| Item | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Mortar and pestle** | Workbench | Heavy stone. There's a faint grey film in the bottom of the bowl. The ritual needs it. |
| **Chalk** | Workbench drawer | A box of white chalk sticks, plenty for the ritual circle. |
| **The last glass eyes** | Drawer cabinet | Most drawers hold loose glass eyes, sorted by color. One drawer holds a single pair wrapped in soft cloth. A paper tag on the cloth says, in Tobias's handwriting, **"for the tall one."** |
| **Tinker's tools** | Workbench | Tobias's personal set, beautifully kept. See Treasure below. |
| **Paint and brushes** | Workbench | Fine brushes and small pots of paint, in case the party wants to paint a face on the doll. It isn't required. |

**Other drawers.** A **DC 13 Wisdom (Perception)** check finds a drawer at the bottom of the cabinet with fifteen small twists of paper in it. Each has a name written on it, matching the ledger. They're all empty.

**The faceless doll.** It still can't be damaged. It weighs about 400 pounds. Moving it takes two strong characters. There's no need to move it, because the ritual can be done right here.

> **DM Note:** By the end of this beat, the party should have everything the ritual needs except the bones: the vessel, the mortar, the chalk, and the eyes. If they miss the eyes, the Beckoning Man appears beside the cabinet, opens the right drawer, and is gone.

### Mold Storage (Room 20)

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> A small room off the workshop, lined floor to ceiling with shelves. The shelves are full of white plaster molds: doll heads, doll hands, doll feet, hundreds of them, all sizes.
>
> On a shelf by itself, at eye level, is one mold with a paper label. It's a girl's face, with round cheeks and a gap in the front teeth. The label says **Rosie.**

The mold matches Rosie's doll exactly. It was cast from her face while she was alive.

### The Root Cellar (Room 17)

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> A cold room with a dirt floor. Wooden shelves hold jars of pickled vegetables and preserves. There are barrels of apples along one wall and sacks of plaster stacked in the corner. Garden tools lean in the other corner: a rake, a hoe, a shovel, and a pickaxe.

The shovel and pickaxe are what the party needs to dig.

## Beat 2: The Kiln Room (Room 19)

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> The kiln room has a floor of old red bricks, worn smooth. The kiln takes up most of the east wall. It's a brick dome taller than you, with a heavy iron door in front. The door is shut. The kiln is cold.
>
> Carving tools hang on pegs along the south wall. There's a square iron hatch high on the east wall, a coal chute.
>
> By the west wall, the bricks look exactly like all the others.

Anyone who saw the first dream knows which bricks to lift. A **DC 10 Intelligence (Investigation)** check also shows that the mortar between those bricks is loose, and they're stacked a little unevenly.

The coal chute loops to the foyer like every other exit.

## Beat 3: Encounter: The House Fights Back

When anyone lifts the first brick, the house reacts.

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> The brick comes up easily. Under it is dark, packed dirt.
>
> Behind you, the iron door of the kiln swings open with a bang. There's fire inside it, roaring, where there was nothing a moment ago.
>
> The carving tools on the south wall start to shake on their pegs. Then they come off the wall all at once and hang in the air, points turned toward you.
>
> Upstairs, small feet start running.

Roll initiative.

**The House Fights Back.** See [encounter_kilnroom_house_fights_back.md](encounter_kilnroom_house_fights_back.md) for the stat blocks, the digging rules, and the battle map.

> **DM Note:** This fight ends when the bones are uncovered, not when the enemies are dead. The digging rules are in the encounter file: five successful digging checks. Split the party's attention between digging and defending. It's a much more interesting fight than a straight brawl.

## Beat 4: The Bones

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> About three feet down, the shovel hits something that isn't dirt.
>
> It's a skeleton, lying on its side with its knees pulled up. The bones are black and brittle, burned all the way through. There are scraps of scorched leather around the ribs. Over the skull is a red wool scarf, faded almost to brown.
>
> As the last of the dirt comes off, you hear the grandfather clock upstairs. It doesn't strike. It just stops ticking.
>
> Then it starts again.

The bones are Tobias Marrowick. The red scarf is Dorrie's, from the first dream. His soul is now **newly loosed**, just as the ritual page says.

**Taking a bone.** Any small bone works. A finger bone comes away easily.

> **DM Note — How Long Is "Newly Loosed"?** The journal says a soul lingers "only a short while." Rule that the ritual works if it's done within 24 hours of lifting the bones. That leaves time for one long rest. The party will want it after this fight. The ritual page says so directly: "a day, no more."

### Speak with Dead

A cleric or other caster may use *Speak with Dead* on the bones. It works now that they're out of the ground. The spell doesn't call up Tobias's soul. It animates what his remains knew. The bones answer in an old man's gentle voice, and they still believe what he believed.

Answer up to five questions. Use these, or improvise in the same voice:

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Who are you? | "Tobias Marrowick. I make toys. The best in Wickford." |
| Why did you bind the dead? | "So the children would have a friend who could laugh back. They were happy. They were happy at first." |
| How do we free the souls? | "Free them? They're not prisoners. They're...". A long pause. "Their names. The eyes. The window works both ways." |
| How do we get out? | "When I'm finished, the door will open. I'm not finished. They're coming back with the torches." |
| What about Rosie? | "I couldn't. Not my girl. I sat by her all night. I couldn't." |
| What is the tall one? | "He was going to walk them home. So no one would be afraid on Ashen Lane." |
| Are you dead? | "Don't be silly. I have work to do." |

## Beat 5: The Second Dream

When the party long rests, the dream takes them again. This one is different. His soul is loose now, and he's angry.

Ask who's keeping watch before starting.

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> You fall asleep, and you're standing in a child's bedroom.
>
> It's Rosie's room, but it's huge. The bed is the size of a house. The toy chest is taller than you. The crayon drawings on the walls are fifty feet high. In them, the big man and the little girl are holding hands, and the man's face has been scribbled out in black.
>
> Somewhere, a music box is playing. Somewhere, someone is humming along.
>
> *"One, two, the Maker's making you..."*

**Encounter: The Burned Maker.** See [encounter_dream_burned_maker.md](encounter_dream_burned_maker.md) for the stat block, dream actions, and battle map.

This time he can be hurt. When he drops to 0 hit points, he burns away and the dream ends. The party has driven him off, not destroyed him. He can only truly be ended in the waking world.

> **DM Tip — If They Try to Talk to Him:** He's too deep in his memory here. Any attempt to talk him down makes him pause for a moment and say something human. "Rosie? Is that you?" or "I only wanted them to be happy." Then he attacks again. Don't give any mechanical benefit. It's a preview of Session 4, where talking will work.

## Beat 6: Waking Up

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> You wake up. Your hands are shaking, and there are fine cuts on your arms that weren't there when you lay down.
>
> Upstairs, the grandfather clock strikes midnight. Twelve slow chimes. Small feet start moving through the house above you.

**The dolls get back up.** Any unfreed dolls get up and scatter through the house again. They don't attack.

**Rest.** Sleepers who survived the dream get the benefits of a long rest.

**The faceless doll.** When the party goes back to the workshop, the faceless doll has turned. It's facing the kiln room door, toward the bones.

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> In the doorway of the workshop, the old man in the cardigan is standing with his lantern. He looks at the faceless doll. Then at the bones. Then at you.
>
> He nods, once.
>
> He's gone.

**End of Session 3.**

> **DM Note — Session Wrap-Up:** The party has the bones, the vessel, the mortar, the chalk, the glass eyes, and the ritual page. Session 4 opens with the ritual. Before next session, count the freed dolls on the ledger. Tobias's hit point maximum is 210 minus 10 for each freed soul. Every doll that isn't freed will rise to fight beside him.

## Treasure

Treasure found in this session. The party can use any of it in Session 4.

- **Tobias's Tinker's Tools.** A beautifully made set of tinker's tools. A character proficient with tinker's tools has Advantage on checks made with them. They grant Advantage on step 2 of the ritual.
- **Glass eyes, one pair.** "For the tall one." Needed for the ritual.
- **Rosie's mold.** No mechanical use. A player who brings it to the final fight can use it as evidence in the talk-down, the same as Rosie's doll.
