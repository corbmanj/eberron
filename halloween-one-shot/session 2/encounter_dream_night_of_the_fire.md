# Encounter: Night of the Fire — The Front Yard (First Dream)

---

### Encounter Summary

```
Difficulty:  Medium
Party:       4 players, level 8 (only the characters who are asleep)

Monster Roster:
  6× Mob Echo — CR 2 (estimated)
  1× The Burned Maker — CR 9 (estimated), invulnerable in this dream

This is a memory, and it runs in three phases. First, the mob mistakes the party for the
toymaker's helpers and turns on them while the pyre burns. Then the Burned Maker steps down
off the fire, unkillable here, and goes straight for the Marked character. The party has to
fight through the mob to the front door. Inside, they watch the dolls carry his body down
and bury it. Watch the clock: the fight ends when the party reaches the door, not when the
mob is dead.
```

> **DM Note — Dream Rules:** Damage, spell slots, and item charges spent here are real. A character who drops to 0 hit points wakes up screaming, stable at 1 hit point, with 1 level of Exhaustion, and gets no benefit from the rest. Characters who reach the end still standing get the full benefits of a long rest. See Dreams and Rest in the [README](../README.md).

---

### The Three Phases

**Phase 1: The Mob (Rounds 1 to 3).** The party appears in the yard, 20 feet south of the house. The mob notices them at once. "More of his! He's got helpers! Get them!" Six Mob Echoes attack. The rest of the crowd is scenery. At the end of round 1, the men tie Tobias to the post and light the brush.

- **Nothing the party does can save Tobias.** It's a memory. Hands pass through the men holding him. Spells aimed at the pyre don't touch it.
- **The pyre grows.** At the end of each round, the fire spreads 5 feet in every direction from the post. A creature that starts its turn in the fire takes 2d10 Fire damage.
- **The Marked character hears him.** Tobias screams from the fire, and he calls for his daughter: "Rosie! Rosie!"

> **Read Aloud — End of Round 1:**
>
> The men tie the old man to the post with rope. Someone throws a torch into the brush at his feet. The dry wood catches all at once, with a sound like a sheet shaken out, and the flames go up past his knees.
>
> He's still talking. You can hear him over the crowd. "They're happy. Please. They're happy."

**Phase 2: The Burned Maker (Round 4 on).** At the start of round 4, the pyre collapses, and the Burned Maker steps out of it.

> **Read Aloud — Round 4:**
>
> The post cracks and falls. The fire collapses in a burst of sparks.
>
> Something walks out of it.
>
> It's the old man, but burned. His skin is black and split. The cardigan is gone, and he's wearing a leather work apron that's still smoking. On his right hand is a leather glove with his carving tools fixed to each finger: chisels, gouges, and awls, a hand's length long.
>
> He looks straight at one of you.

He goes for the Marked character. He can't be hurt in this dream. The mob keeps fighting around him. He doesn't attack them, and they don't see him.

**The goal:** Reach the front door. It's 20 feet north of where the party started, on the porch. A character who steps through the door is safe from the fight. When every character still in the dream is through the door, go to Phase 3.

> **DM Tip:** If the players try to stand and fight the Burned Maker, describe their best hits doing nothing: blades pass through smoke, fire washes over him. Have the Beckoning Man appear in the doorway of the house holding up his lantern. That's a clear signal to run for the door.

**Phase 3: The Burial (no combat).** Once the party is inside, the fight is over.

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> The door shuts behind you, and the noise of the crowd cuts off.
>
> It's quiet. It's dark. It's later. You can tell because the rain is heavy now, drumming on the roof, and there's no torchlight in the windows. The mob is gone.
>
> The front door opens by itself. Rain blows in.
>
> The dolls come in out of the rain. All fifteen of them, in a line. They're carrying something between them, on their shoulders, the way people carry a coffin. It's a body, burned black and still smoking in the rain.
>
> They don't look at you. They carry him through the foyer, down the back hall, into the pantry, and down the stairs.

If the party follows, continue:

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> They take him down into the basement, through a small hall, into a room with a brick floor and a big brick kiln against one wall. The smallest dolls pry up the bricks by the west wall, one at a time, and stack them neatly. The bigger dolls dig with their hands. It takes a long time.
>
> They lay him in the hole. The girl doll in the green coat takes off her red scarf and puts it over his face. Then they fill in the dirt, and put the bricks back, one at a time, exactly where they were.
>
> Then they sit down around the bricks and don't move.
>
> In the doorway behind you, something smells of smoke.

The Burned Maker is standing in the doorway. Before anyone can act, the dream ends. Go back to Beat 6 in the session document.

### Stat Blocks

### Mob Echo

*Medium Undead, Neutral*

*A memory of one of the townsfolk who burned Tobias Marrowick, made real by his dreaming. Echoes shout the things the mob shouted that night, and they believe the party is helping the grave robber.*

| | |
|---|---|
| **AC** | 12 (work clothes) |
| **HP** | 45 (7d8 + 14) |
| **Speed** | 30 ft |
| **STR** | 14 (+2) |
| **DEX** | 12 (+1) |
| **CON** | 14 (+2) |
| **INT** | 8 (−1) |
| **WIS** | 10 (+0) |
| **CHA** | 8 (−1) |

**Damage Resistances:** Necrotic
**Damage Immunities:** Fire, Poison
**Condition Immunities:** Exhaustion, Poisoned
**Senses:** passive Perception 10
**Languages:** Common

---

**Traits:**

**Mob Fury.** The echo has Advantage on attack rolls against a creature if at least one other Mob Echo is within 5 feet of that creature.

**Only a Memory.** When the echo drops to 0 hit points, it crumbles into wet ash. At the end of the next round, it re-forms at the edge of the crowd, 30 feet from the nearest character. An echo can re-form only once.

---

**Actions:**

**Multiattack.** The echo makes two attacks, using Pitchfork or Torch in any combination.

**Pitchfork.** *Melee Attack Roll:* +4, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d8 + 2) Piercing damage.

**Torch.** *Melee Attack Roll:* +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Bludgeoning damage plus 3 (1d6) Fire damage.

---

**Tactics:**
- The echoes gang up on whoever is closest to the pyre, shouting "Grave robber!" and "Give them back!"
- Echoes with pitchforks stay back and attack with reach. Echoes with torches close in.
- Once the Burned Maker steps out of the fire, the echoes try to block the path to the front door.
- They never retreat. They don't know they're in a dream.

---

**Loot:** None. They're memories.

---

### The Burned Maker

*Medium Undead, Neutral Evil*

*The soul of Tobias Marrowick, trapped in the dreams of anyone who sleeps in his house. He believes it's still the night of the fire and that everyone he meets is the mob.*

| | |
|---|---|
| **AC** | 15 (charred hide and leather apron) |
| **HP** | 150 (20d8 + 60) |
| **Speed** | 40 ft |
| **STR** | 18 (+4) |
| **DEX** | 16 (+3) |
| **CON** | 16 (+3) |
| **INT** | 14 (+2) |
| **WIS** | 14 (+2) |
| **CHA** | 18 (+4) |

**Saving Throws:** DEX +7, WIS +6, CHA +8
**Damage Resistances:** Bludgeoning, Piercing, Slashing
**Damage Immunities:** Fire, Poison
**Condition Immunities:** Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Poisoned
**Senses:** Truesight 60 ft, passive Perception 12
**Languages:** Common

---

**Traits:**

**Invulnerable Memory (Night of the Fire only).** In this dream, the Burned Maker can't be damaged, and he ignores all conditions. He takes only one action on his turn: a single Carving Glove attack or Nightmare Visage. He doesn't use Dream Shift.

**Master of the Dream.** The Burned Maker can't die in a dream. When he drops to 0 hit points, he burns away to ash, and the dream ends.

**Knows the Marked.** The Burned Maker always knows where the Marked character is, and he has Advantage on attack rolls against them.

**Legendary Resistance (2/Day).** If the Burned Maker fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.

---

**Actions:**

**Multiattack.** The Burned Maker makes three Carving Glove attacks.

**Carving Glove.** *Melee Attack Roll:* +8, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 13 (2d8 + 4) Slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) Psychic damage.

**Nightmare Visage (Recharge 5–6).** *(Spell-like — not counterspellable.)* The Burned Maker shows his burned face. *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 16, each creature in a 30-foot Cone. *Failure:* 27 (6d8) Psychic damage, and the target has the Frightened condition until the end of its next turn. *Success:* Half damage only.

---

**Bonus Actions:**

**Dream Shift.** The Burned Maker teleports up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space he can see, or through a wall to the space on its other side.

---

**Tactics:**
- In this dream, he walks straight at the Marked character and attacks them every turn with a single Carving Glove attack.
- If the Marked character reaches the door first, he turns on whoever is closest to him.
- He talks while he fights, pleading with the "mob": "Why are you doing this? They're happy. I made them happy."
- He never runs. When the party is through the door, he stands on the porch and watches them through the window.

---

**Loot:** None. He's a dream.

### Battle Map Description

> This map must be rendered in a strict top-down, directly overhead bird's-eye view. The camera is directly above looking straight down, as if the map were a floor plan. Not isometric. Not perspective. Not a three-quarter view. Directly overhead — the viewer is looking straight down at the ground.

**Map size:** 60×60 ft. The front yard of a two-story house at night.

**Terrain:**

- **Ground surface:** Dead, trampled grass and mud across the whole yard, wet from rain. A flagstone path, 5 ft wide, runs from the gate at the south edge straight north to the porch.

- **The house front (north edge):** The front of a dark green wooden house with white trim runs along the entire north edge of the map. A covered wooden porch, 40 ft long and 10 ft deep, has three steps up in the middle. The front door is in the center of the porch. Two of the downstairs windows are smashed, with glass on the porch boards. Two carved jack-o'-lanterns sit on either side of the steps.

- **The pyre (center):** A thick wooden post, about 1 ft across, stands in the middle of the yard, 25 ft south of the porch. A ring of brush and split firewood, about 10 ft across, is piled around its base, burning with tall orange flames.

- **Iron fence and gate (south edge):** A black iron fence runs along the whole south edge, with an open gate in the middle where the path begins.

- **A dead oak tree (west side):** A large leafless oak with a trunk about 4 ft across. Its roots break up the ground around it, making difficult terrain in a 10-ft circle. It provides full cover.

- **Wheelbarrow and woodpile (east side):** A small stack of split firewood, about 10×5 ft, with an overturned wheelbarrow beside it. It provides half cover.

- **Dropped tools:** Pitchforks, shovels, and burned-out torches scattered on the grass.

- **Color and light:** Night with a thin rain. The main light is the orange glow of the pyre, brightest in the center and fading toward the edges. Scattered torches lie in the grass. The house windows are dark. Puddles on the path reflect the fire.

> Style: painterly and detailed, similar to a professional tabletop RPG battle map. Rendered strictly from directly above — top-down, no perspective, no isometric angle.
