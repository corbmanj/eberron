# Session 2: The Maker's Journal

## The Maker's House — Part 2 of 4

*A session for 4 characters of 8th level. Estimated runtime: 3 to 3.5 hours.*

---

## Session Overview

The dolls are down, and the house is quiet. The party explores the rest of the house and pieces together who Tobias Marrowick was. His journal tells them what he did, and why he believed he was doing good. It also holds the ritual that will end this. When they finally sleep, the first dream takes them back to the night of the fire. They watch the town burn him, fight their way out of the mob, and see the dolls bury his body. When they wake up, ash footprints lead down to the basement. The masked figure is standing in the workshop.

> **DM Note — Session 1 Recap:** The party was stabbed by the Masked Stalker, herded down Ashen Lane, and lured into the house by the Beckoning Man. The front door locked behind them. They fought the fifteen Bound Dolls on the main floor and knocked them all down. The Beckoning Man appeared at the top of the stairs and pointed down the upstairs hall. One character is Marked, with black stitches where the knife went in.

## Beat 1: Aftermath

The fallen dolls lie where they dropped. They don't move. The party can take a short rest safely.

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> The dolls lie where they fell. A porcelain arm in the middle of the floor. The jester on his face, one bell still rolling in a slow circle before it stops. Sawdust spilled out of the baker's side.
>
> The grandfather clock ticks. The hands still point to one minute before midnight.
>
> The house is warm and quiet. Somewhere in the kitchen, something is simmering on the stove.

**Looking closely at a fallen doll.** No check is needed now that they're still. There's fine grey grit caked in the corners of every doll's glass eyes. Anyone who rubs it between their fingers feels something like ash or chalk. A **DC 13 Intelligence (Nature or Medicine)** check identifies it as ground bone.

> **DM Tip:** If nobody worked out how to free the dolls in Session 1, the Marked character can hear them now. The fallen dolls whisper faintly, all together, one word over and over: their names. It's hard to make out any single name without the ledger.

**The kitchen food.** The stew on the stove and the bread on the table are real, warm, and safe to eat. They never run out. Tobias remembers his kitchen full.

## Beat 2: The Main Floor

Rooms 2 through 9 on [house_main_floor.svg](../maps/house_main_floor.svg). See [maps.md](../maps/maps.md) for the full key. The important ones are below.

### The Parlor (Room 4)

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> This room is burned. Black scorch marks climb the walls from the floor to the ceiling. The two front windows are smashed, and broken glass covers the floorboards under them. Beyond the windows, you can see Ashen Lane. It's empty.
>
> The furniture is fine. A settee with a knitted blanket over the back. A wooden cradle beside the hearth. A fire burning low in the grate.
>
> Burned into the wallpaper above the settee, in big uneven letters, are words.

Give the players [Handout 5](../handouts.md#handout-5-words-on-the-parlor-wall).

**The portrait.** Over the hearth hangs a painted portrait in a wooden frame. It's the old man who beckoned them in. He's younger and has fewer grey hairs, and there's a girl of about seven on his knee. She has dark curls and a gap in her front teeth. The small brass nameplate at the bottom of the frame has been scratched off with a knife.

A **DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation)** check on the nameplate makes out one word in the scratches: **Marrowick.**

### The Study (Room 8)

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> A small study with a heavy wooden desk, a bookcase full of thin books, and a pile of letters tied with string. A lamp is lit on the desk. Pinned to the wall above it is a printed notice, yellow with age.

Give the players [Handout 1](../handouts.md#handout-1-the-church-notice). Point out the pencil circles and the torn underline if they look closely.

**The books.** Toymaking manuals: carving, porcelain casting, clockwork joints, doll painting.

**The letters.** Thank-you notes from Wickford families, all written before the fever. "Our Edda hasn't put her ballerina down in a week." "Thank you for fixing Jory's horse. He cried the whole time it was gone." A player who reads a few learns Tobias was loved.

### The Dining Room (Room 5)

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> A small dining room. The table is set for two. On one side, a large plate, a knife and fork, and a mug. On the other side, a small plate, a small spoon, and a tin cup with a painted flower on it. Both places are clean. Nobody has ever eaten from them.

No check is needed. If anyone asks, the small cup has an **R** scratched into the bottom.

### The Pantry (Room 7)

The cellar door is heavy oak, carved with a row of dancing dolls. It's locked. The lock can be picked with a **DC 20 Dexterity check using Thieves' Tools**, but the lock turns back as soon as the pick is removed, and the door stays shut. *Knock* opens it for 1 minute, then it slams and locks again.

> **DM Note:** The party isn't meant to reach the basement until after the first dream. The key is in Rosie's room. If they do force the door and go down early, the workshop is empty. The faceless doll isn't there yet, because it's still out walking the streets. Let them explore the basement. The grave's location isn't known yet, and the kiln room floor just looks like old bricks.

## Beat 3: The Upper Floor

Rooms 10 through 15 on [house_upper_floor.svg](../maps/house_upper_floor.svg).

### The Upper Hall (Room 10)

The first time the party comes upstairs, the Beckoning Man is here.

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> The stairs come up into a wide hallway lined with doors. Lamps glow on small tables along the walls. At the far end, there's a window seat with a cushion on it, looking down at the street.
>
> The old man in the cardigan is standing at the window with his back to you. His lantern is on the window seat beside him. He's looking out at the front yard.
>
> He turns his head a little, just enough that you can see his spectacles. He points to the first door on your left.
>
> Then he's gone. The lantern is gone too. The cushion on the window seat still has a dent in it.

From the window seat, the party can see the front yard and the charred post in the dead grass.

### The Master Bedroom (Room 11)

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> A tidy bedroom. The bed is made, with a patchwork quilt pulled tight. There's a wardrobe against one wall and a tall standing mirror beside the window. On the nightstand is a lamp, a pair of reading spectacles, and a thick leather journal.

Give the players [Handout 2](../handouts.md#handout-2-tobiass-journal), then the ritual page, [Handout 3](../handouts.md#handout-3-the-ritual-page). Give them time to read. This is the most important clue in the adventure.

**The wardrobe.** A good wool coat, a leather work apron, and a small velvet box with a child's hair ribbon inside.

**The mirror.** When any character looks into the tall mirror, they see the room behind them normally. When the Marked character looks, they see the stitches on their side glowing faintly red. A hand mirror lies on the dresser. It's small enough to carry.

> **DM Note:** Mention the hand mirror plainly. A mirror is one of the pieces of evidence that helps talk Tobias down in Session 4. Don't point that out now.

### The Painting Room (Room 12)

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> This room smells like linseed oil. A long table is covered with small brushes, some no thicker than a needle. On an easel by the window sits a porcelain doll's head with half a face painted: one eye, one eyebrow, half a mouth.
>
> The shelves along the wall hold paint jars in neat rows. On the middle shelf, set apart from the others, there's a row of fifteen small glass jars. Each one has a paper label. Each one has a little grey dust in the bottom.
>
> Beside the jars is a small cloth-bound book.

The book is the ledger. Give the players [Handout 4](../handouts.md#handout-4-the-ledger-of-the-dead), but only the names, ages, and doll descriptions.

**The jars.** Each label is a name, matching the ledger. The dust is the leftover bone dust from each binding. Anyone who read the journal will understand what they're looking at.

> **DM Note:** The ledger gives the party the second way to free a soul: speaking its name over a fallen doll. The dolls are lying downstairs right now. A party that goes back down and reads the names aloud can free all fifteen before the clock strikes. That's a smart play, and it makes the final fight much easier. Let them do it. Each freed soul lowers Tobias's hit point maximum by 10.

### The Washroom (Room 14)

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> A small washroom with a copper tub and a basin on a stand. There's a round mirror over the basin.

When anyone breathes on the mirror, or the room fills with steam, small fingers write in the fog from the other side: **FREE US.** Then the words fade.

### The Guest Room (Room 15)

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> This room is burned black. The walls are charred down to the studs in places. The window is smashed out, frame and all. The bed is a pile of charcoal on an iron frame. A burned-out torch lies on the floor under the window.
>
> The room smells like smoke. It's much colder in here than in the rest of the house.

This is where the mob's torches came in. There's nothing to find, but it's a good place for a scare. If the party lingers, a doll-sized footprint appears in the ash on the floor behind them.

### Rosie's Room (Room 13)

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> A child's bedroom. The bed is small, with a white quilt covered in embroidered stars. There's a wooden toy chest at the foot of it. Drawings in crayon are pinned all over one wall: a big man with a beard and a small girl with dark curls, holding hands, standing in front of a green house. Some drawings have the sun. One has a dog.
>
> On the bed, propped against the pillow, sits a doll. It's porcelain, in a white nightgown, with dark painted curls and a gap in its front teeth. It's the finest doll you've seen in this house.
>
> On a hook on the wall beside the bed hangs an iron key.

This is Rosie's doll. It's different from the others:
- **It never moves.** Not when unobserved, not when touched, not when the clock strikes.
- **It doesn't whisper.** The Marked character hears nothing from it.
- **It has no grit in its eyes.** Its glass eyes are clean.
- ***Detect Magic* shows nothing.** Every other doll in the house radiates necromancy.

The iron key opens the cellar door in the pantry.

> **DM Note:** The party should take Rosie's doll with them. It's the strongest piece of evidence for talking Tobias down in Session 4. If they don't take it, the Beckoning Man can help. The next time they pass Rosie's room, the door is open, the old man is sitting on the edge of the bed with the doll in his lap, and he holds it out toward them before he vanishes.

## Beat 4: Putting It Together

By now the party should know:
- **Who Tobias Marrowick was.** A toymaker who bound the souls of Wickford's dead into his dolls, meaning well, and was burned for it.
- **What the dolls are.** Trapped souls who can be freed by crushing their eyes or speaking their names.
- **What the ritual needs.** Freshly dug remains, a vessel, bone dust in the vessel's eyes, and the words.

They don't know yet:
- **Where his body is.** The first dream shows them.
- **What vessel to use.** The faceless doll, found after the first dream.

Let them talk it through. If they're stuck, the Marked character hears a whisper from the nearest doll: "He's still here. He's under the floor. He's under the bricks."

## Beat 5: The First Dream

Eventually the party has to long rest. When they do, the dream takes them.

Before starting, ask who's keeping watch. Anyone who stays awake watches over the sleepers and can't join the dream. See Dreams and Rest in the README.

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> You fall asleep to the ticking of the clock.
>
> Then you're standing outside, in the front yard of the green house. It's night. It's cold. There's a thin rain starting to fall.
>
> The yard is full of people. Thirty of them, maybe forty, men and women in work clothes. Most are holding torches. Some have pitchforks and shovels. Everyone is shouting.
>
> In the middle of the crowd, four men are dragging an old man out of the house by his arms. He's in a brown cardigan. His spectacles are gone. He's saying, over and over: "Please. Please, they're happy. They're happy."
>
> There's a wooden post in the middle of the yard, with brush and firewood piled around its base.

**Encounter: Night of the Fire.** See [encounter_dream_night_of_the_fire.md](encounter_dream_night_of_the_fire.md) for the phases, stat blocks, and battle map.

The encounter ends with the party following the dolls into the basement and watching them bury Tobias's body under the kiln room floor.

## Beat 6: Waking Up

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> You wake up on the floor where you fell asleep. Your clothes smell like smoke.
>
> Downstairs, the grandfather clock begins to strike. One. Two. Three. It keeps going, slow and heavy. Twelve.
>
> Then silence. Then, all through the house, the sound of small things getting up off the floor.

**The dolls get back up.** Every doll that wasn't freed stands up and walks away to a random room in the house. They don't attack unless touched. If any character goes to look, the dolls are sitting still in new places, facing the doors.

**Wounds and rest.** Sleepers who survived the dream get the benefits of a long rest. Watchers saw wounds open on the sleepers' bodies during the night, and smoke rise off their clothes.

### The Footprints

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> There are footprints on the floor of the foyer that weren't there before. They're big, bare, and flat, like the feet of a statue, and they're made of grey ash. They go from the front door, through the back hall, into the kitchen, and stop at the carved door in the pantry.
>
> The cellar door is standing open.

The cellar door is unlocked now, whether or not the party has the key.

### The Faceless Doll

The footprints lead down the stairs, through the stair hall, and into the workshop. Use [house_basement.svg](../maps/house_basement.svg).

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> The stairs go down into a stone basement. It's cold down here, and it smells like wood shavings and clay. The ash footprints go straight across the floor and through a doorway on the left, where there's lamplight.
>
> It's a workshop. There's a long workbench against the far wall, a wooden lathe, and a rack of porcelain arms and legs hanging from hooks. Two oil lamps are lit on the workbench.
>
> In the middle of the floor, standing perfectly still, is the tall figure in the white mask.
>
> It isn't moving. Its arms hang at its sides. The carving knife is on the floor beside its feet.

It doesn't move no matter what they do. It's a doll now, not a puppet. Tobias isn't working it right now.

If anyone lifts the mask:

> **Read Aloud:**
>
> The mask comes away easily. Underneath is a face made of smooth white porcelain.
>
> It has no eyes. No nose. No mouth. There are two shallow, empty sockets where eyes should go.

**What the party can learn here.**
- **It's a doll.** Up close, it's porcelain and wood, with brass joints. It's the tall guardian from the journal.
- **It can't be damaged.** Blades skip off it. Hammers don't chip it. It has the Hollow Vessel trait even while it stands still.
- **It radiates necromancy.** Under *Detect Magic*, it gives off a faint glow, with a thin thread of magic running out of it toward nowhere in particular. That thread goes into the dream.

**End of Session 2.** End on the empty face.

> **DM Note — Session Wrap-Up:** The party now knows the method from the journal, the location from the dream, and the vessel from the workshop. Session 3 opens in the basement, with digging up the bones. Record freed dolls on the ledger. Remaining dolls are scattered around the house.
