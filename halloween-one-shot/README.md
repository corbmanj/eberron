# The Maker's House

## A Hallows' Eve One-Shot

*A four-session horror adventure for 4 characters of 8th level, using the 2024 (5.5e) rules. Each session runs about 3 to 3.5 hours.*

---

## Adventure Summary

On Hallows' Eve, the town of Wickford puts on costumes and goes door to door. The party joins in. A tall figure in a plain white mask follows them from street to street, and near the end of the night it stabs one of them. Nothing they do can hurt it.

It herds them to the end of Ashen Lane, where a kindly old man with a lantern waves them through the gate of a house that should be a burned ruin. The door slams and locks behind them. The old man is gone.

The house is full of dolls. There are fifteen of them, and they move when no one is watching. When the party sleeps, a burned man with a glove of carving blades hunts them through their dreams.

To escape, the party has to learn who Tobias Marrowick was, what he did, and why the town burned him. Then they have to dig up his bones, bind his soul into the doll he never finished, and either destroy him or convince him he's already dead.

---

## Background (DM Only)

Thirty years ago, Tobias Marrowick was Wickford's toymaker. Children loved his dolls. He loved making them, and he always wanted them to be better: more alive, more like real friends.

That winter, the grey fever swept through Wickford and killed dozens, many of them children. Tobias's own daughter, Rosie, was one of them.

Tobias found the only way he could to make his dolls truly lifelike. He crept into the churchyard at night and took a single small bone from each of the recently dead, usually a knuckle or a fingertip. He ground each bone to dust in his workshop mortar and worked a pinch into the painted eyes of a doll. The soul of the dead person woke up inside it. The dolls laughed, sang, and walked. He truly believed he was doing good. He wrote about it tenderly in his journal.

He made Rosie's doll first, but he could never bring himself to bind her. Her doll is the only one that stayed empty.

He put the dolls in his shop window. On Hallows' Eve, Aldous Crane walked past and heard his dead daughter Maisie singing from behind the glass. The town opened the graves, found the missing bones, and came for Tobias with torches. They dragged him onto his own front lawn, tied him to a post, and burned him. They set fire to the house too, but rain put it out before it burned down.

That night, the dolls dragged his body inside and buried it under the brick floor of his kiln room.

Tobias's soul never left. It went into the dreams of anyone who sleeps in his house, and it holds the house the way he remembers it, on the night before the fire. Every Hallows' Eve, the house opens again.

### The Pieces of Tobias

Everything strange in this adventure is part of Tobias Marrowick.

| What the party sees | What it really is |
|---|---|
| **The Masked Stalker** | A life-sized doll Tobias was building as a guardian to walk children home. It never got a face, so he covered it with a plain festival mask. His spirit works it like a puppet from inside the dream. It has no soul, so nothing can hurt it. It is also the vessel the party needs for the ritual. |
| **The Beckoning Man** | Tobias's memory of himself as a kind old toymaker. It's the part of him that knows what he did was wrong and wants it to end. He lured the party in on purpose, and he quietly leads them to the clues. He can't be touched and almost never speaks. |
| **The Burned Maker** | Tobias's soul, trapped in the dream. He's burned, wears his leather work apron, and has fixed his carving blades to the fingers of a glove. He believes the party is the mob. He can't be killed in the dream. |
| **The Bound Dolls** | Fifteen of Wickford's dead, trapped in porcelain and cloth. They attack because they're in agony and because he controls them, not because they're evil. |

## How the Party Solves It

The party needs three things. Each one comes from a different part of the adventure.

1. **The method, from the journal.** Tobias's journal is upstairs in his bedroom. It explains the binding ritual: freshly dug remains, a vessel, bone dust in the vessel's eyes, and the words to call the soul. Give the players the ritual page as a handout. See [handouts.md](handouts.md).
2. **The location of his body, from the first dream.** The first dream replays the night of the fire. It ends with the dolls burying his body under the kiln room floor.
3. **The vessel, from the house.** After the first dream, the Masked Stalker is found standing still in the basement workshop. Under the mask, the face is blank porcelain.

Then they dig up the bones and perform the ritual in the workshop. Tobias wakes up inside the faceless doll, believes the party is the mob, and fights. The party can destroy him, or talk him into understanding that he's already dead.

## Rules of the House

Read these before Session 1. They come up in every session.

### The Sealed House

Once the front door slams, there's no way out until Tobias is laid to rest.

- **Every exit loops back to the foyer.** Doors, windows, the coal chute, and holes knocked in walls all lead back into the foyer, stepping out through the front door. Show this the first time someone tries, so the players stop spending resources on it.
- **Teleportation fails the same way.** *Misty Step*, *Dimension Door*, and similar magic work inside the house. Any destination outside puts the caster in the foyer instead.
- **Messages get answered by Tobias.** A *Sending* or *Message* aimed at anyone outside gets a reply in an old man's gentle voice: "Hush now. The children are sleeping."
- **The house is always night.** Every clock inside reads 11:59. The windows show Ashen Lane on Hallows' Eve, with no one in it.

### Dreams and Rest

- **Short rests are safe.** Dozing lightly doesn't pull anyone into the dream.
- **Long rests pull everyone who sleeps into the dream.** Elves and others who trance are pulled in too. The sleepers appear together in the dream.
- **Anyone who stays awake watches over the sleepers.** Wounds taken in the dream open on the sleeper's real body. Watchers can see how the fight is going. They can't join it or wake the sleepers early.
- **Resources spent in the dream are really spent.** Spell slots, hit points, and item charges carry over.
- **Surviving the dream counts as a long rest.** When the dream encounter ends, sleepers who are still standing wake with the full benefits of a long rest.
- **Dropping to 0 hit points in the dream wakes you.** The character wakes screaming, stable at 1 hit point, and gains 1 level of Exhaustion. They get no benefit from the rest.
- **Leomund's Tiny Hut doesn't help.** The dream doesn't come through the dome. It's already inside.

### The Grandfather Clock

The grandfather clock in the foyer is stuck at 11:59. Every time a long rest ends, it strikes midnight, twelve slow chimes that can be heard throughout the house. When it does, every bound doll that was knocked down gets back up somewhere in the house.

### The Mark

The character the Masked Stalker stabs in Session 1 is **Marked**. The wound closes into a neat row of black stitches that can't be removed. *Remove Curse* or *Greater Restoration* makes them itch but doesn't remove them.

- **The Marked character hears the dolls.** They hear faint whispers from nearby dolls: names, pleas, pieces of memories. Use this to feed them clues.
- **The Burned Maker hunts them first.** In every dream, he knows where the Marked character is and goes for them first.

Pick a player who'll enjoy the spotlight. This character gets the most scares and the most clues.

### The Bound Dolls

- **Knocked down, not destroyed.** A doll reduced to 0 hit points falls still. It gets back up the next time the grandfather clock strikes, unless its soul is freed.
- **Two ways to free a soul.** A creature can take an action to crush a fallen doll's glass eyes, which scatters the bone dust. Or a creature can speak the doll's name over it, as written in the ledger in the painting room. Either way, a small light rises out of the doll and fades. That doll never moves again.
- **Freeing souls weakens Tobias.** For each soul freed before the ritual, reduce Tobias's hit point maximum in the final fight by 10. Fewer dolls also means fewer allies for him. Track freed dolls on the ledger in [handouts.md](handouts.md).

The Marked character is the natural one to discover this. When a doll falls near them, have it whisper its name, or whisper "my eyes, please."

## Spells That Touch the Mystery

8th-level characters have magic that can shortcut a mystery. These answers keep the mystery intact without shutting the players down.

| Spell or Effect | What Happens |
|---|---|
| *Speak with Dead* on Tobias's bones | Works once the bones are dug up. Prepared answers are in Session 3. |
| *Speak with Dead* on anything else | There are no other corpses in the house. The dolls aren't corpses. |
| *Divination* | "The one who made them needs a body to be unmade. Look for the tall one with no face." |
| *Detect Evil and Good* | Senses undead presence everywhere in the house at once, strongest in the basement. |
| *Detect Magic* | Every doll radiates necromancy. So does the Masked Stalker. The front door radiates abjuration. |
| *Dispel Magic* on a doll | The doll falls still until the clock next strikes. The soul isn't freed. |
| *Dispel Magic* on the front door | The lock clicks open, and the door opens into the foyer again. |
| *Banishment* on the Masked Stalker | It has no soul to banish. The spell fails and the slot is spent. |
| *Locate Object* or *Locate Creature* | Works normally inside the house. |
| *Sending* | Answered by Tobias. See The Sealed House. |

## Character Creation

- **Level and rules.** Four new 8th-level characters, built with the 2024 *Player's Handbook*. Use the 2024 *Dungeon Master's Guide* guidance for starting equipment and magic items at higher levels.
- **Costumes.** Ask each player what costume their character wears to the festival. It's a fun opener, and it gives you something to describe when the Burned Maker taunts them.
- **Why they're in Wickford.** Let each player pick one, or make up their own:
  - They grew up in Wickford and came home for the festival.
  - They're guarding a merchant's wagon that stopped here for the night.
  - They were hired by the town council to keep order during the festival.
  - They heard Wickford throws the best Hallows' Eve festival in the region.

## Running It in Eberron

The adventure is written so it works in any setting. To set it in Eberron:

- **Set it during Long Shadows.** This is the festival on the 26th through 28th of Vult, when people say the Shadow's power rises. Families keep lights burning, and children go door to door.
- **Put Wickford in Karrnath or the Eldeen Reaches.** Both have small, superstitious farming towns. Karrnath's history with necromancy makes the town's reaction to Tobias easy to believe.
- **Make the grey fever a plague from the Last War.** Tobias can be a former House Cannith tinker who left the house to make toys.
