# Encounter: Tobias, the Faceless Doll — The Workshop (Ritual Chamber)

---

### Encounter Summary

```
Difficulty:  Deadly (Hard if the party freed most of the souls)
Party:       4 players, level 8

Monster Roster:
  1× Tobias, the Faceless Doll — CR 12 (estimated)
  Every Bound Doll not yet freed — CR 1/2 each

Tobias wakes inside the seven-foot doll believing the party is the mob. He hits hard with
the carving glove at 10-foot reach, drags characters into the memory of the fire with Nightmare
Grasp, and fills the room with phantom fire through his Burning Memory lair actions. His dolls
swarm the party using his Call the Children legendary action. His hit points drop by 10 for
every soul freed before the fight. The fight can end two ways: at 0 hit points, or at three
talk-down successes. Remind the players the talk-down is open the whole time. He's pleading
with them in every round.
```

---

### Setup

- **Starting positions.** Tobias starts in the center of the chalk circle. The unfreed dolls start in the workshop doorways: the stair hall door, the kiln room door, and the mold storage door. The party is wherever they stood for the ritual, most likely right around the circle.
- **Hit points.** Tobias has **210 hit points minus 10 for each soul freed before the fight.** With all fifteen freed, he has 60. That's intentional. A party that freed every soul has earned a short, sad fight.
- **The circle.** Once he steps out of the chalk circle, it's just chalk. It has no further effect.

### Stat Blocks

---

### Tobias, the Faceless Doll

*Large Construct, Neutral*

*Tobias Marrowick's soul, called out of the dream and bound into the guardian doll he never finished, by his own ritual. He sees torches in every hand and hears a mob that burned him thirty years ago.*

| | |
|---|---|
| **AC** | 17 (porcelain and hardwood) |
| **HP** | 210 (20d10 + 100), minus 10 per freed soul |
| **Speed** | 30 ft |
| **STR** | 20 (+5) |
| **DEX** | 14 (+2) |
| **CON** | 20 (+5) |
| **INT** | 16 (+3) |
| **WIS** | 12 (+1) |
| **CHA** | 18 (+4) |

**Saving Throws:** STR +9, CON +9, WIS +5
**Damage Resistances:** Piercing, Slashing
**Damage Immunities:** Fire, Poison, Psychic
**Condition Immunities:** Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned
**Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft, passive Perception 11
**Languages:** Common

---

**Traits:**

**Porcelain Body.** Bludgeoning damage cracks the porcelain. When Tobias takes Bludgeoning damage, describe a crack spreading across the doll. He has no resistance to it.

**Legendary Resistance (3/Day).** If Tobias fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.

**Lost in the Fire.** Tobias believes it's the night of the fire and that every creature he sees is part of the mob. He can be talked down. See Hesitation.

**Hesitation.** When a creature succeeds on a check to talk Tobias down, he hesitates. On his next turn, he makes one fewer Carving Glove attack, and he can't take Legendary Actions until the start of that turn. When the third success lands, the fight ends. See Ending A in the session document.

**Last Rest.** When Tobias drops to 0 hit points, the doll cracks apart, and his soul is released. Every Bound Doll in the house falls still, and all their souls are freed.

---

**Actions:**

**Multiattack.** Tobias makes three Carving Glove attacks. He can replace one of them with Nightmare Grasp, if it's available.

**Carving Glove.** *Melee Attack Roll:* +10, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 16 (2d10 + 5) Slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) Psychic damage.

**Nightmare Grasp (Recharge 5–6).** *(Spell-like — not counterspellable.)* Tobias reaches for the mind of one creature he can see within 60 feet and pulls it into his memory of the fire. *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 17. *Failure:* 33 (6d10) Psychic damage, and the target has the Incapacitated condition until the end of its next turn as it relives the fire. *Success:* Half damage only.

---

**Bonus Actions:**

**Step Through the Smoke.** Tobias teleports up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space he can see that is inside Phantom Fire or Smoke from his Burning Memory.

---

**Legendary Actions:**

*Legendary Action Uses: 3. Immediately after another creature's turn, Tobias can expend a use to take one of the following actions. He regains all expended uses at the start of each of his turns.*

**Glove Swipe.** Tobias makes one Carving Glove attack.

**Call the Children.** Up to two Bound Dolls within 60 feet of Tobias move up to their Speed and make one Sewing Needle attack each.

**Remember the Heat (Costs 2 Uses).** Every creature within 10 feet of Tobias makes a DC 17 Constitution saving throw. *Failure:* 13 (3d8) Fire damage. *Success:* Half damage.

---

**Tactics:**
- He opens by stepping out of the circle toward whoever spoke his name, then uses Nightmare Grasp on the character with the strongest spells.
- He uses his 10-foot reach to fight from the middle of the Phantom Fire and makes characters come to him.
- He spends Legendary Actions on Call the Children while dolls remain. Once the dolls are gone, he uses Glove Swipe and Remember the Heat.
- He saves Legendary Resistance for effects that would take him out of the fight, like Hold Monster or a Banishment.
- He talks every round. He pleads with the mob, calls for Rosie, and insists the dolls are happy. Use his lines to invite the talk-down.
- He never retreats. There's nowhere for him to go.

---

**Loot:**

**Tobias's Carving Glove** *(wondrous item, rare, requires attunement).* A leather glove with chisels, gouges, and awls fixed to each finger. It counts as a simple melee weapon that deals 1d6 Slashing damage, with the Finesse and Light properties, and you're proficient with it. Once per dawn, when you hit with it, you can deal an extra 2d6 Psychic damage.

Also here: the pale brown glass eyes, the finger-bone dust, and a porcelain doll broken or standing still, depending on how the fight ended.

### Bound Doll

*Small Construct, Neutral*

*A toymaker's doll with a dead person's soul trapped behind its glass eyes. It attacks because the soul inside is in pain and because the man who made it still pulls the strings.*

| | |
|---|---|
| **AC** | 13 (porcelain and cloth) |
| **HP** | 22 (5d6 + 5) |
| **Speed** | 30 ft, Climb 30 ft |
| **STR** | 8 (−1) |
| **DEX** | 16 (+3) |
| **CON** | 12 (+1) |
| **INT** | 8 (−1) |
| **WIS** | 10 (+0) |
| **CHA** | 10 (+0) |

**Damage Immunities:** Poison, Psychic
**Condition Immunities:** Exhaustion, Frightened, Poisoned
**Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft, passive Perception 10
**Languages:** Understands Common. Speaks only in fragments, in the voice of the person inside.

---

**Traits:**

**Bound Soul.** When the doll drops to 0 hit points, it falls still. In this fight, it doesn't get back up. A fallen doll's soul can be freed in one of two ways:
- A creature within 5 feet takes an action to crush the doll's glass eyes.
- A creature speaks the doll's name over it, as written in the ledger. This doesn't take an action.

When a soul is freed, a small point of light rises out of the doll and fades. If Tobias is defeated, every remaining soul is freed at once.

**False Appearance.** While the doll is motionless, it looks like an ordinary doll.

**Swarm.** The doll has Advantage on attack rolls against a creature if at least two other Bound Dolls are within 5 feet of that creature.

**Whispers.** A Marked creature within 10 feet of the doll hears it whispering: its name, a memory, or a plea.

---

**Actions:**

**Sewing Needle.** *Melee Attack Roll:* +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Piercing damage.

**Clamber.** The doll climbs onto a Medium or larger creature within 5 feet. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 13. *Failure:* The doll clings to the target and moves with it. While clinging, the doll has Advantage on Sewing Needle attacks against that target. The target or an adjacent creature can take an action to pull the doll off, which requires a DC 13 Strength (Athletics) check. *Success:* The doll falls into an adjacent space.

---

**Tactics:**
- The dolls go for whoever is talking to Tobias. They try to stop the talk-down by swarming the speaker.
- They move and attack on their own turns, and again when Tobias uses Call the Children.
- They don't sing in this fight. They're silent.

---

**Loot:** None. Each doll is porcelain, cloth, wood, and paint.

---

### Lair Actions: Burning Memory

On initiative count 20, losing initiative ties, Tobias's memory of the fire takes over the workshop. Choose one. Don't use the same one two rounds in a row.

**Phantom Fire.** Flames burst up from the stone floor in up to three 10-foot squares that Tobias can see. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 15, each creature in those squares. *Failure:* 16 (3d10) Fire damage. *Success:* Half damage. The fire keeps burning until the next initiative count 20. A creature that enters it for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there takes 11 (2d10) Fire damage. The fire is real enough to burn, but it doesn't spread and doesn't light anything else.

**The Mob's Voices.** The walls fill with shouting: "Grave robber! Give them back!" *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 15, each creature in the workshop other than Tobias. *Failure:* The creature has the Frightened condition until the end of its next turn. While Frightened this way, it sees torches, pitchforks, and faces in the shadows.

**Smoke.** Thick grey smoke fills a 20-foot-radius Sphere centered on a point Tobias can see. The area is Heavily Obscured until the next initiative count 20.

### Battle Map Description

> This map must be rendered in a strict top-down, directly overhead bird's-eye view. The camera is directly above looking straight down, as if the map were a floor plan. Not isometric. Not perspective. Not a three-quarter view. Directly overhead — the viewer is looking straight down at the ground.

**Map size:** 35×45 ft. A toymaker's basement workshop with a stone floor.

**Terrain:**

- **Ground surface:** Grey flagstone floor, swept clean, with a few curls of pale wood shavings near the lathe.

- **The ritual circle (center):** A large chalk circle drawn on the floor in the middle of the room, about 13 ft across, made of seven concentric rings. Between the rings are bands of small hand-drawn chalk symbols: stars, keys, tiny hands, and closed eyes. The chalk is bright white against the grey stone.

- **Workbench (north wall):** A long, heavy wooden workbench running most of the north wall, about 20×3 ft. Tools are laid out on it in neat rows. A heavy stone mortar sits in the middle. Two brass oil lamps stand at either end. It provides half cover.

- **Mold storage (northwest corner):** A small 12×12 ft room walled off in the corner, with a plain wooden door on its south side. Inside, shelves full of white plaster doll molds.

- **Wood lathe (east side):** A foot-powered wooden lathe, about 6×4 ft, against the east wall. It provides half cover.

- **Parts rack (southwest):** A wooden rack hung with pale porcelain doll arms and legs, all sizes. About 7×4 ft. It provides half cover.

- **Drawer cabinet (southeast corner):** A tall wooden cabinet with dozens of small drawers with brass pulls, about 11×4 ft. It provides full cover.

- **Low bench (west wall):** A long, low wooden bench against the west wall, about 13×3 ft.

- **Support posts (2):** Two thick wooden posts holding up the ceiling beams, each about 2 ft across. One is southwest of the circle and one is east of it. Each provides half cover.

- **Doorways (east wall):** An open doorway on the east wall near the north end leads to a stair hall. A second open doorway near the south end of the east wall leads to a kiln room.

- **Color and light:** Warm yellow light from the two oil lamps on the workbench, strongest along the north wall and fading toward the south. The corners are in deep shadow. The chalk circle is the brightest thing on the floor.
