# Encounter: The Masked Stalker — Lantern Row

---

### Encounter Summary

```
Difficulty:  Unwinnable
Party:       4 players, level 8

Monster Roster:
  1× The Masked Stalker — CR — (cannot be defeated)

The Stalker can't be hurt, moved against its will, or stopped by any condition. It stabs once,
marking its target, and then only herds: it shoves characters toward Ashen Lane and blocks every
other way out. The point of the fight is for the players to realize they can't win and run.
Watch for players who keep spending spell slots on it. After round 2, make its invulnerability
impossible to miss.
```

> **DM Note — The Escape Route:** Running is the answer. The Stalker never moves faster than a walk and never chases. It only follows. The moment the party breaks away, move to Beat 4 in the session document: every street they take leads back to Ashen Lane. If the party refuses to run for more than three rounds, have a townsperson scream from a doorway: "Run! It doesn't die! It never dies!"

### Stat Blocks

---

### The Masked Stalker

*Medium Construct, Unaligned*

*A seven-foot doll of pale porcelain and dark wood, built to walk children home in the dark. It has no face under the mask and no soul inside, only the will of the dead man working it from the dream.*

| | |
|---|---|
| **AC** | 18 (porcelain and hardwood) |
| **HP** | — (see Hollow Vessel) |
| **Speed** | 30 ft |
| **STR** | 20 (+5) |
| **DEX** | 14 (+2) |
| **CON** | 20 (+5) |
| **INT** | 1 (−5) |
| **WIS** | 14 (+2) |
| **CHA** | 1 (−5) |

**Damage Immunities:** All damage
**Condition Immunities:** All conditions
**Senses:** Blindsight 120 ft, passive Perception 12

---

**Traits:**

**Hollow Vessel.** The Stalker can't be damaged, and it ignores every condition. Attacks that hit leave no mark: blades skip off the porcelain, and fire goes out against it. If a spell would banish, dispel, teleport, or control it, the spell has no effect, and the slot is spent. Any effect that would move it against its will fails. If it's somehow knocked down or pinned, it stands back up at the end of its turn.

**Knows the Marked.** Once it has Marked a creature, the Stalker always knows exactly where that creature is, even through walls.

**Unhurried.** The Stalker never takes the Dash action. When no creature can see it, it can teleport to an unoccupied space within 60 feet that no creature can see. This is how it's always waiting at the end of the street.

**Unnatural Stillness.** While the Stalker stands motionless, it looks like a tall person in a costume.

---

**Actions:**

**Carving Knife.** *Melee Attack Roll:* +9, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 21 (3d10 + 5) Piercing damage. If the target isn't already Marked, it becomes Marked (see The Mark in the README). The Stalker uses this action only once per night.

**Herd.** The Stalker shoves or steps in front of one creature within 5 feet of it. *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 17. *Failure:* The target is pushed up to 15 feet in a direction of the Stalker's choice, always toward Ashen Lane. *Success:* The target stays where it is, but it can't move closer to the Stalker until the start of its next turn.

---

**Tactics:**
- Round 1: it uses Carving Knife on the chosen character, with Advantage from the surprise approach. It never uses Carving Knife again.
- From round 2 on, it uses Herd every turn, pushing characters toward the alley that leads to Ashen Lane.
- It steps into the path of anyone trying to go another way. It ignores anyone heading toward Ashen Lane.
- When it takes a hit, describe the attack failing completely: the blade skips, the spell fizzles, the fire goes out. It doesn't react.
- It never runs, never speaks, and never turns its back.
- When the party runs, it stops and watches them go. Then it walks after them.

---

**Loot:** None.

### Battle Map Description

> This map must be rendered in a strict top-down, directly overhead bird's-eye view. The camera is directly above looking straight down, as if the map were a floor plan. Not isometric. Not perspective. Not a three-quarter view. Directly overhead — the viewer is looking straight down at the ground.

**Map size:** 60×80 ft. Lantern Row runs the length of the map from south to north.

**Terrain:**

- **Ground surface:** A cobblestone street 20 ft wide down the middle of the map, running north to south. Packed dirt footpaths about 5 ft wide run along both sides in front of the buildings. Scattered straw, dropped candy wrappers, and a few trampled paper lanterns lie on the cobbles.

- **Buildings on both sides:** Two-story timber-and-plaster house fronts line the east and west edges, each with a small wooden porch or stone step. Every step has a carved jack-o'-lantern with a candle glowing inside. The buildings are solid walls at the map edges. Their porches give half cover.

- **Festival stalls (2):** On the west side of the street, a wooden cider stall with a big iron pot on a small fire. On the east side, a candy cart with a striped canvas awning. Each is about 10×5 ft and provides half cover. The cider fire counts as a small open flame.

- **Hay wagon:** A farm wagon loaded with hay bales, parked diagonally across the southern third of the street. About 15×8 ft. It provides full cover, and the top of the hay is 6 ft up.

- **Pumpkin stacks (3):** Piles of uncarved pumpkins, each about 5×5 ft, scattered along the footpaths. Difficult terrain.

- **Stone well:** A round stone well about 6 ft across in the middle of the street at the northern third, with a wooden roof on two posts. It provides half cover.

- **The alley to Ashen Lane:** On the east side, near the north end of the map, is a gap between two buildings, 10 ft wide. It's an unlit alley. This is where the Stalker herds the party.

- **Color and light:** Night. Strings of paper lanterns hang across the street from roof to roof. About half are lit in orange and yellow, and the rest are dark. The jack-o'-lanterns cast small pools of orange light on each step. The alley is completely dark.

> Style: painterly and detailed, similar to a professional tabletop RPG battle map. Rendered strictly from directly above — top-down, no perspective, no isometric angle.
