# Encounter: The Bound Dolls — The Toy Shop

---

### Encounter Summary

```
Difficulty:  Medium
Party:       4 players, level 8

Monster Roster:
  15× Bound Doll — CR 1/2 (estimated), arriving in four waves

The dolls are weak one at a time, but they swarm. Each doll has Advantage against a creature
that already has two other dolls next to it, so they pile onto one target at a time. Six start
in the toy shop, and the rest pour in from around the house over the next three rounds. Area
spells will flatten them, which is fine: they get back up when the clock strikes. Watch for the
Marked character. The dolls are drawn to them, and this is the fight where the party can
discover how to free a soul.
```

---

### Arrival Schedule

The fight assumes someone touched a doll in the toy shop. Dolls from other rooms arrive at the start of the listed round, coming through the nearest door. If the fight starts in another room, the dolls in that room start the fight, and the others arrive one round later per room between them and the fight.

| Round | Dolls | Where They Come From | Enter Through |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Maisie, Tomas, Edda, Jory, Lottie, Dorrie | Already in the toy shop | — |
| 2 | Pip, Bertram | Foyer | Foyer door |

### Stat Blocks

---

### Bound Doll

*Small Construct, Neutral*

*A toymaker's doll with a dead person's soul trapped behind its glass eyes. It attacks because the soul inside is in pain and because the man who made it still pulls the strings.*

| | |
|---|---|
| **AC** | 13 (porcelain and cloth) |
| **HP** | 22 (5d6 + 5) |
| **Speed** | 30 ft, Climb 30 ft |
| **STR** | 8 (−1) |
| **DEX** | 16 (+3) |
| **CON** | 12 (+1) |
| **INT** | 8 (−1) |
| **WIS** | 10 (+0) |
| **CHA** | 10 (+0) |

**Damage Immunities:** Poison, Psychic
**Condition Immunities:** Exhaustion, Frightened, Poisoned
**Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft, passive Perception 10
**Languages:** Understands Common. Speaks only in fragments, in the voice of the person inside.

---

**Traits:**

**Bound Soul.** When the doll drops to 0 hit points, it falls still but isn't destroyed. It gets back up the next time the grandfather clock strikes midnight, in a random room of the house. A fallen doll's soul can be freed in one of two ways:
- A creature within 5 feet takes an action to crush the doll's glass eyes.
- A creature speaks the doll's name over it, as written in the ledger. This doesn't take an action.

When a soul is freed, a small point of light rises out of the doll and fades. The doll never moves again.

**False Appearance.** While the doll is motionless, it looks like an ordinary doll.

**Swarm.** The doll has Advantage on attack rolls against a creature if at least two other Bound Dolls are within 5 feet of that creature.

**Whispers.** A Marked creature within 10 feet of the doll hears it whispering: its name, a memory, or a plea.

---

**Actions:**

**Sewing Needle.** *Melee Attack Roll:* +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Piercing damage.

**Clamber.** The doll climbs onto a Medium or larger creature within 5 feet. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 13. *Failure:* The doll clings to the target and moves with it. While clinging, the doll has Advantage on Sewing Needle attacks against that target. The target or an adjacent creature can take an action to pull the doll off, which requires a DC 13 Strength (Athletics) check. *Success:* The doll falls into an adjacent space.

---

**Tactics:**
- The dolls go first for whoever touched the trigger doll, then for the Marked character.
- They pile onto one target at a time to get Swarm Advantage. Three or four dolls on one character is normal.
- They climb. They go up the shelves, onto the counter, and onto characters. Dolls on high shelves drop onto characters below as their move.
- They sing the rhyme from [Handout 6](../handouts.md#handout-6-the-rhyme) while they fight, in a mix of children's and adults' voices.
- Lottie, the smallest doll, doesn't attack. She hides under the counter and cries. She counts as an enemy for Swarm, but she only takes the Dodge action.
- They never retreat. They fight until every doll is down.

---

**Loot:** None. Each doll is porcelain, cloth, wood, and paint.

---

### Running the Fight

- **Show the Bound Soul rule early.** When the first doll drops near the Marked character, have it whisper its name, or "my eyes... please." If a player repeats the name or crushes the eyes, describe the light rising and fading. Don't explain it.
- **Keep track of freed dolls.** Check them off on the ledger in [handouts.md](../handouts.md). Every freed soul lowers Tobias's hit point maximum by 10 in the final fight.
- **Describe hits on the dolls physically.** Porcelain cracks. Cloth tears and leaks sawdust. A wooden arm snaps off and keeps moving on the floor.
- **Maisie is special.** When the rag doll in the blue pinafore goes down, she whispers, "Papa? Papa, is that you?" If anyone met Aldous Crane, this will land.

### Battle Map Description

> This map must be rendered in a strict top-down, directly overhead bird's-eye view. The camera is directly above looking straight down, as if the map were a floor plan. Not isometric. Not perspective. Not a three-quarter view. Directly overhead — the viewer is looking straight down at the ground.

**Map size:** 40×32 ft. The map covers the east half of the main floor of an old wooden house: the entry hall on the west, the toy shop in the southeast, and a small dining room and pantry in the northeast.

**Terrain:**

- **Ground surface:** Dark, worn wooden floorboards throughout, running east to west. A braided rag rug about 8×10 ft lies in the middle of the toy shop.

- **Entry hall (west, 16 ft wide):** Runs the full height of the map. A wooden staircase, 6 ft wide, climbs toward the north. A tall grandfather clock stands against the wall beside the stairs. A plain wooden front door is on the south wall. Open doorways lead east into the toy shop and north into a back hallway.

- **Toy shop (southeast, 22×20 ft):** Long wooden shelves, 3 ft deep, run along the north wall. They're mostly empty, with a few toys left on them. A wooden shop counter, about 8×3 ft, stands in the middle with a brass bell on top. A large display window takes up most of the south wall, with a low display platform in front of it. A small table holds a wicker basket. A boarded-up door is on the south wall near the southeast corner. The shelves and counter give half cover.

- **Dining room (northeast, 12×12 ft):** A small room north of the shop, with a wooden table set for two: one large plate and one small plate with a tiny cup. There are four chairs. An open doorway leads south into the shop.

- **Pantry (northeast corner, 10×12 ft):** Narrow shelves of glass jars of preserves along one wall. A heavy wooden door carved with dancing dolls leads to a staircase going down.

- **Color and light:** Warm yellow light from brass oil lamps mounted on the walls, two in each room. The display window shows a dark night outside, with the faint orange glow of jack-o'-lanterns. Long shadows fall under the shelves and counter.

> Style: painterly and detailed, similar to a professional tabletop RPG battle map. Rendered strictly from directly above — top-down, no perspective, no isometric angle.
